Business Platō Coffee Reaches 100 Locations Nationwide

August 1, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Platō Coffee has officially hit a major milestone 100 stores across South Africa. What started as a small idea between two brothers, Stephan and Petrus Bredell, has grown into one of the country's fastest-growing coffee brands. But for the Platō team, it’s always been about more than just coffee.

As Stephan shared in a recent post, this moment is a tribute to everyone who's been part of the journey from franchisees and partners to loyal customers who kept coming back. For the brothers, Platō was never just a business; it was about connection, consistency, and creating warm, welcoming spaces that feel like part of the community.

Their story began in December 2019, when Stephan already running Moonshot Café saw the potential in coffee’s strong profit margins. He pitched the idea of a new venture to Petrus, and soon they opened their first store: a converted container in Centurion. It was modest and operated under an informal license, but it was enough to get started especially during the relaxed COVID-19 lockdowns when foot traffic increased.

At first, Petrus ran most of the day-to-day, while Stephan kept his corporate job. Without Petrus, Stephan says, Platō wouldn’t have made it through those early days. But their hard work paid off.

They began expanding steadily, targeting areas that big coffee chains usually ignore like lifestyle centres and smaller towns while still gaining popularity in major cities thanks to their consistently high-quality coffee. Now, with 100 stores and over 500 people employed, Platō is aiming even higher. Their goal is to reach 150 stores by the end of 2025.

But expansion isn’t just about more locations. Platō is also launching a coffee academy in Cape Town’s District Six. The academy, based on Canterbury Street, will offer everything from barista training to latte art and sensory workshops. It’s designed for both professionals and casual coffee lovers and will include a showroom for top-tier equipment.

Stephan describes the academy as a place to deepen your understanding and appreciation of coffee, no matter your skill level. It's another example of how Platō isn’t just building a business they’re creating a culture.

They’re also leading the way in tech, becoming the first company in South Africa to introduce Yoyo’s card-linked loyalty program. Customers get 5% cashback every time they tap to pay, with rewards automatically tracked through the Platō app no extra cards or steps required. Even unregistered users are invited to join via SMS, unlocking any cashback they’ve already earned.

This seamless loyalty system is fully integrated with Table by Yoco, the point-of-sale platform used in all Platō stores. The goal is simple: make it easy and rewarding to come back.

So, while 100 stores is a big number, for Platō it’s just one part of a much bigger story one built on community, quality, and a deep love for the ritual of coffee.

Media SAPS Ballistics Link 4 Guns to 24 Crimes

October 28, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

SAPS ballistics head Brigadier Mishak Mkhabela has revealed shocking findings before the Madlanga Commission, testifying that four firearms tied to the murder of Vereeniging engineer Armand Swart are also linked to 24 other criminal cases across Gauteng.

Mkhabela said ballistic evidence matched shell casings from multiple scenes, showing the same weapons were used in robberies, shootings, and even gang-related killings. The discovery has reignited public anger over how firearms are handled, stored, and traced within SAPS.

The Madlanga Commission is investigating widespread failures in police evidence control, including missing weapons and tampered records. Swart’s killing, which occurred outside his home last year, is now at the center of a far larger network of crime involving recycled and unaccounted firearms.

Civil society groups and opposition parties have called for urgent reforms within SAPS, saying the revelations highlight the country’s deep-rooted issues with corruption and accountability in law enforcement.

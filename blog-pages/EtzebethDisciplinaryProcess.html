Sport Etzebeth Still in Limbo as Disciplinary Process Stalls

December 4, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Springbok lock Eben Etzebeth will only learn his fate on Sunday after a World Rugby disciplinary panel failed to reach agreement on his alleged eye gouging case. This leaves one of the sports biggest names in a state of uncertainty, with fans and analysts waiting for clarity on whether he will face suspension.

The incident, which occurred during a high pressure international clash, has already generated sharp debate among former players, commentators and supporters. Some argue that the evidence does not support a severe sanction, while others believe World Rugby must take a strong stance to protect player safety.

The panel was expected to conclude its assessment earlier this week, but officials say they need more time to review footage, medical reports and written submissions from both sides. As a result, Etzebeth remains in a holding pattern that could affect team preparations for upcoming fixtures.

The delay has frustrated supporters, who feel disciplinary processes should be more efficient, especially in professional rugby where match schedules are tight. World Rugby is expected to make a final announcement at the end of the weekend, bringing an end to the uncertainty.

Media Bodycam Footage Reveals Arrest of South African Couple in US Shoplifting Case

March 16, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Police body camera footage has revealed new details surrounding the arrest of South African couple Melany “Mel” Viljoen and her husband Petrus “Peet” Viljoen in the United States on suspicion of shoplifting.

The footage, released by authorities in the United States, captures the moments officers confronted the couple outside a retail store following allegations that merchandise had been taken without payment. According to reports, store staff alerted law enforcement after observing suspicious behaviour inside the premises.

The couple, both originally from South Africa, were reportedly visiting the United States when the incident occurred. In the video, officers are seen approaching the pair, questioning them about the items allegedly taken from the store and subsequently placing them under arrest.

Authorities have not publicly disclosed the full list or value of the items involved in the alleged incident. However, shoplifting laws in many parts of the United States can carry serious legal consequences depending on the value of the goods and whether the act is classified as a misdemeanour or a felony. The release of the bodycam footage has sparked widespread discussion online, particularly among South Africans who have been following the case closely.

Videos and commentary related to the incident have circulated on various social media platforms, prompting debate about the circumstances surrounding the arrest. Legal analysts say that the release of police bodycam footage is common in the United States, particularly in cases that attract public interest. The recordings often serve as official documentation of police interactions and are used as evidence during legal proceedings.

The Viljoens are expected to face court proceedings in the jurisdiction where the alleged offence took place. Their legal representatives may challenge aspects of the case depending on the evidence presented by prosecutors.

While the investigation continues, the case has drawn attention both locally and internationally, highlighting how incidents involving foreign nationals can quickly gain public visibility in the digital age.

Media Wynberg Court Assassination Plot Foiled: Seven Arrested

December 17, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

A potentially deadly attack at Wynberg Magistrates’ Court was averted after authorities successfully intercepted a planned assassination on Tuesday morning, resulting in the arrest of seven suspects. Security forces, including local police and national crime units, acted on intelligence indicating that the suspects intended to harm court officials or other individuals connected to high-profile proceedings.

According to police reports, the operation was meticulously planned. Officers executed raids at multiple locations to prevent the suspects from carrying out the attack. During the operation, several firearms and ammunition were seized, including semi-automatic weapons believed to be unregistered. The suspects, ranging in age and background, were detained without resistance.

Authorities say the investigation is ongoing to determine whether the assassination attempt was linked to organised crime or stemmed from personal grievances connected to cases being heard at Wynberg Magistrates’ Court. Detectives are interviewing the arrested individuals and conducting forensic analyses of digital devices, communications, and weapons recovered in the operation.

Senior police officials emphasized that the coordinated intervention likely saved lives and prevented a major disruption to judicial proceedings. “This was a well-coordinated attempt to intimidate or eliminate specific individuals connected to court proceedings,” said one officer. “Our timely intelligence and response ensured that the suspects were apprehended before any harm could occur.”

Court operations resumed as normal, though security presence has been significantly increased. Additional officers have been deployed in and around Wynberg Magistrates’ Court to ensure safety during ongoing proceedings.

Authorities continue to appeal to the public to report any information regarding possible accomplices or related criminal activity. Legal experts noted that the swift action demonstrates the critical role intelligence-led policing plays in protecting South African judicial institutions.

This remains a developing story as police continue to unravel the motives and potential networks behind the assassination plot.

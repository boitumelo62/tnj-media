Sport Rabada Ruled Out of India Tour as Proteas Chase Historic Series Success

November 21, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

At 5 pm the Proteas have been dealt a major setback after fast bowler Kagiso Rabada was officially ruled out of the remainder of the India tour due to a rib injury. Team medical staff confirmed that Rabada suffered a stress injury to his rib during a training session before the first Test in Kolkata. Despite treatment and monitoring he has not recovered well enough to take the field again.

Rabada will now begin a four week rehabilitation programme and is expected to return home after the completion of the second Test. His absence leaves a significant gap in South Africa’s bowling attack. Lungi Ngidi has been called in as cover but the loss of a strike bowler of Rabada’s calibre is a major blow at a crucial stage of the tour.

The injury comes at a time when the Proteas are enjoying one of their most memorable runs in India. South Africa recorded a landmark victory in the opening Test claiming their first Test win on Indian soil in fifteen years. The result has boosted confidence in the camp and created belief that a rare series win in India is within reach.

The tour has been intense and demanding. India remains one of the toughest destinations for visiting teams and the conditions have tested both sides. South Africa’s batters showed impressive composure in spinning conditions while the bowlers applied relentless pressure throughout the match. Rabada played an important role in that victory using experience and control to unsettle the Indian top order.

His injury forces the Proteas to rethink their balance for the second Test. Ngidi is expected to slot into the attack while the rest of the pace group will shoulder extra responsibility. The coaches have emphasised that this is an opportunity for younger bowlers to step up at a defining moment of the tour.

Despite losing one of their senior fast bowlers the Proteas remain determined to finish the series strongly. They believe their historic breakthrough in the first Test has shifted momentum and proven that they can win in India. The second Test will now be played under added pressure as South Africa look to complete what would be one of their most significant away series victories in recent years.

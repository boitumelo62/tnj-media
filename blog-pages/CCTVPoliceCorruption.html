Media CCTV Footage Links Police Sergeant to Businessman in Inquiry

November 5, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Madlanga Commission of Inquiry has received explosive CCTV footage showing an alleged exchange between police sergeant Mpho Sebola and businessman Katiso “KT” Molefe, both figures already under scrutiny for suspected corruption and abuse of power.

The footage reportedly shows the two men meeting at a restaurant in Johannesburg in what investigators believe may have been an off-the-record discussion about a police investigation involving Molefe’s company. The video, now part of the commission’s evidence, was handed over by a whistleblower who claims it exposes collusion between certain officers and business figures in exchange for protection or preferential treatment.

The Madlanga Commission was established earlier this year to probe allegations of systemic corruption within Gauteng’s police ranks. Several officers have already been suspended or reassigned pending the outcome of the hearings.

Sources close to the investigation say Molefe has been a controversial figure in Johannesburg’s business community for years, with interests in property development and logistics. He has previously faced accusations of tender irregularities but has never been convicted of any crime.

The commission’s legal team confirmed that the new footage is being examined for authenticity and relevance. If verified, it could have major implications for both the police officer involved and Molefe’s ongoing business dealings with the state.

Civil society groups have welcomed the development, saying it underscores the need for transparency in law enforcement. “This shows how deep-rooted the problem of police corruption can be when officers become entangled with powerful business interests,” one anti-corruption advocate said.

The commission is expected to resume public hearings next week, where the footage will likely be presented as part of new testimony.

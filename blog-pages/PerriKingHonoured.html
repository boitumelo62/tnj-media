October 24, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Faith and Fear has announced that its co founder and executional lead, Perri King, has been named one of the winners of the 2025 Forty Under 40 Awards a prestigious national recognition that celebrates South Africa’s most exceptional young leaders under the age of 40. Perri stood out among hundreds of nominees for her groundbreaking work in transforming the communications and brand strategy industry, as well as her commitment to building inclusive, women led economic models.

As co founder of Faith and Fear, she has reimagined what an agency can be creating a flexible, people first business that values agility, strategic thinking, and the empowerment of women. Her approach has helped leading brands such as Profmed, Liberty Group, Talent10 Holdings, and Versofy Solar strengthen their reach, refine their stories, and deliver real impact. But Perri’s influence extends far beyond her professional achievements. Through Faith and Fear’s network of more than 25 women led supplier businesses, she has contributed to meaningful economic growth, long term sustainability, and job creation across multiple sectors.

Alongside this, she co leads The Brave Collective, a social impact initiative that supports over 2,000 South African women every month through leadership training, financial education, and community building. Under her guidance, both Faith and Fear and The Brave Collective have earned national recognition, including Standard Bank Top Women in Business Award in 2024, a previous Entrepreneur of the Year finalist title, and multiple creative awards and industry features. Her work continues to shape not just how brands communicate, but how women lead boldly and unapologetically. Reflecting on the honour, Perri King said, “This award is for every woman building without a blueprint.

For those leading with heart in a world that rewards only hustle. I’m grateful to be seen and more committed than ever to building businesses and communities that invest in what really matters.”Faith and Fear is a South African, women owned brand and strategy consultancy that partners with future focused organisations to create bold ideas, measurable growth, and meaningful social impact. The company is built on the belief that bravery is good for business, combining data, storytelling, and cultural insight to help brands connect with purpose.

The Brave Collective, co founded by the Faith and Fear leadership team, is a non profit empowerment platform and sisterhood designed to help women strengthen their self worth, net worth, and network. Through its programs, it fosters brave conversations, builds financial confidence, and creates spaces for women to grow together.

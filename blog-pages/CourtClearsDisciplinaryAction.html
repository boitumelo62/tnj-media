Media Court Clears Way for Disciplinary Action Against Senior Metro Police Official

March 23, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Labour Court has ruled that disciplinary proceedings against Julius Mkhwanazi may continue, dismissing an urgent attempt to halt the process. Mkhwanazi, a senior figure within the Ekurhuleni Metropolitan Police Department, had sought to interdict the internal disciplinary hearing linked to allegations raised during the Madlanga Commission of Inquiry.

However, the court found no sufficient legal basis to stop the proceedings, effectively allowing the municipality to proceed with its case. The ruling marks a significant development in a matter that has drawn national attention due to the serious nature of the accusations.

Mkhwanazi has been implicated in claims of misconduct, including alleged abuse of authority and irregular decisions within the metro police structure. His suspension in 2025 followed mounting scrutiny over these allegations. Legal experts suggest the judgment reinforces the principle that internal disciplinary mechanisms should not be easily delayed through court interventions, especially in cases involving public accountability.

The municipality is expected to continue with its internal processes, which could ultimately determine Mkhwanazi’s future in law enforcement. The case also ties into broader concerns raised at the Madlanga Commission, where testimony has pointed to systemic issues within policing structures, including alleged corruption and governance failures.

The commission’s findings have already triggered calls for reform and stronger oversight in municipal policing. For now, the focus shifts back to the disciplinary hearing itself, where evidence will be tested and Mkhwanazi will have an opportunity to respond to the allegations against him.

The outcome of the hearing could have far-reaching implications, not only for Mkhwanazi personally but also for public confidence in law enforcement accountability in South Africa.

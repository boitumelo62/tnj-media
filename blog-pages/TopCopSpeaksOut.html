Media Top Cop Speaks Out: Mkhwanazi Signals Possible Arrest in High Profile Case

March 20, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

KwaZulu Natal police commissioner Nhlanhla Mkhwanazi has indicated that there is sufficient evidence to arrest suspended deputy national commissioner Shadrack Sibiya over alleged links to the criminal underworld. The statement marks a significant escalation in a case that has already raised serious concerns about integrity within the country’s top law enforcement ranks.

Speaking on the matter, Mkhwanazi emphasized that investigators have gathered compelling information pointing to possible criminal associations. While he stopped short of detailing the full scope of the evidence, he made it clear that authorities are prepared to act decisively should legal processes allow. The case is understood to involve alleged connections between senior officials and organized crime networks, a development that has shocked many within policing circles.

Sibiya, who has been under scrutiny for some time, has not yet responded publicly to the latest remarks. His suspension previously signaled internal concerns within the South African Police Service, but the possibility of arrest now shifts the matter into a more serious legal phase.

Experts warn that such allegations, if proven true, could severely damage public confidence in law enforcement institutions. At a time when crime remains a pressing issue across the country, the integrity of those tasked with upholding the law is seen as critical.

Government officials have reiterated their commitment to rooting out corruption within the police service, stressing that no individual is above the law. The case is expected to unfold rapidly, with potential legal proceedings likely to attract widespread attention. For many South Africans, the situation underscores the urgent need for accountability and reform within key institutions responsible for maintaining justice and security.

Media SABC Drops R1 Billion Profit Target Amid Media Industry Pressure

March 9, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The South African Broadcasting Corporation has abandoned its plan to achieve a R1 billion profit by the 2026 to 2027 financial year, citing mounting pressure in the media sector and uncertainty in the broadcasting landscape.

The public broadcaster had previously outlined the ambitious target as part of its long term financial recovery strategy. However, shifting market conditions have forced the organisation to reassess those projections.

One of the key factors influencing the decision is the ongoing transformation of the television and streaming industry. Competition from international streaming platforms has intensified, while traditional broadcasters continue to face declining advertising revenue.

Industry changes have also been highlighted by the planned closure of the streaming platform Showmax, which has added further uncertainty to the local media environment.

SABC executives say the organisation is focusing on financial sustainability rather than pursuing aggressive profit goals that may no longer be realistic in the current climate.

Despite dropping the R1 billion profit target, the corporation says it remains committed to strengthening its financial position and maintaining its role as the country’s public broadcaster.

Media analysts say broadcasters worldwide are adapting to rapid technological shifts as audiences increasingly move toward digital and on demand viewing.

Media Morero survives no confidence threat

December 3, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The no confidence motion against Johannesburg Mayor Dada Morero has been withdrawn, halting what could have become another leadership showdown in the city’s already fragile political environment.

The withdrawal came shortly before the matter was expected to be debated in council, prompting questions about the motivations behind the reversal. Those who initially supported the motion offered little detail, but political insiders say the move is linked to shifting coalition negotiations and last minute agreements between competing factions.

Morero, who has been under growing pressure over service delivery concerns, budget constraints and administrative instability, now continues in office with temporary breathing room. City governance has been characterised by frequent leadership changes in recent years, fuelled by coalition tensions that often make stability difficult to achieve.

Analysts say the withdrawal does not necessarily signal strengthened support for Morero but rather reflects the fluid nature of council alliances. With major infrastructure challenges, billing issues and service backlogs still unresolved, the mayor is expected to face renewed political tests in the coming months.

Opposition parties insist they will keep monitoring the city’s performance and have warned that further motions cannot be ruled out if conditions in Johannesburg fail to improve.

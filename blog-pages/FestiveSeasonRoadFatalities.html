Media Festive Season Begins With Sharp Rise In Western Cape Road Fatalities

December 10, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Western Cape has reported a troubling start to the festive period, with twenty eight people losing their lives in twenty one fatal crashes during the first week of December. Authorities said the numbers represent an early warning that the province may face another difficult holiday season on its roads if urgent behavioural changes are not observed.

The collisions occurred across multiple districts and involved pedestrians, private vehicles and public transport. Officials pointed to speeding, reckless overtaking, fatigue and alcohol use as recurring factors. Traffic enforcement teams have already increased visibility and roadblocks, but they noted that enforcement alone cannot reverse the trend unless road users take greater responsibility for their actions.

The provincial government appealed to families and communities to intervene early, reminding drivers to rest during long trips and to avoid late night travel when possible. There is also renewed focus on pedestrian safety, with calls for improved lighting and community based awareness campaigns. As holiday travel intensifies, authorities urged the public to treat every trip as a potential risk and to prioritise caution over convenience. The rising death toll has placed renewed pressure on transport officials to tighten operations over the coming weeks, emphasising that each life lost represents a family forever changed.

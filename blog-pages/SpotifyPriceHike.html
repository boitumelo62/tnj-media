Business Spotify Hikes Premium Prices Globally

August 5, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Spotify has announced another global increase in Premium subscription prices, with South African users among those affected. This is the second price hike in just over a year, as the streaming giant adjusts its pricing model to support profitability and future development.

In South Africa, the Individual Premium plan will increase from R64.99 to R69.99, Duo will rise to R94.99, and Family to R119.99. The Student plan will go from R34.99 to R37.99. These changes take effect from September 2025, with notifications being sent via email and in-app alerts.

Spotify says the hike will fund new features, platform improvements, and expanded content offerings. “Price updates are essential to keep innovating,” the company said in a statement.

The decision follows Spotify’s first annual profit in 2024, though its most recent quarter showed a net loss due to tax and wage costs. Still, markets reacted positively, with Spotify’s stock seeing a modest bump as investors welcomed the focus on sustained revenue. Similar increases are being rolled out in the U.K., Europe, Asia, and Latin America. In the U.S., the Individual plan will rise from $10.99 to $11.99.

In South Africa, all current Premium features ad-free playback, offline downloads, and unlimited skips remain unchanged. But with tighter household budgets, some users may reconsider their subscriptions. Rival services like Apple Music and YouTube Music currently offer slightly lower or similar pricing, with Apple Music still at R69.99 and YouTube Music around R64.99. Whether they will follow suit remains uncertain.

While the increase may seem modest, it highlights the streaming industry’s shift from user growth to profitability. Spotify is betting that its large user base will accept the extra cost in exchange for continued product evolution.

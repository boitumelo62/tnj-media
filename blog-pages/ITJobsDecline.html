Business South Africa’s IT Sector Faces Steep Decline in Jobs

September 16, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South Africa’s information technology sector is experiencing a sharp reduction in employment opportunities. The latest Employment Insights report from CareerJunction shows that job openings for IT professionals have fallen by about 20 percent compared to the same period last year, despite demand for skilled workers remaining high.

Recruiter activity in IT has remained steady from one quarter to the next, but the number of positions available has declined noticeably. This has created tougher competition for fewer roles across the sector.

Some areas, however, are seeing growth. Demand for hardware and maintenance technicians, particularly those working with assembly, repair, and servicing of electrical and electronic equipment, has increased. Creative fields within IT are also gaining traction, with graphic, print, and packaging design roles showing strong hiring growth.

In terms of pay, the sector continues to outperform the national average. Entry level positions in software development and IT analysis already offer salaries well above the average monthly wage in South Africa. Professionals with five to ten years of experience tend to earn significantly more, especially in areas such as consulting, management, security, and senior development.

Despite the higher salaries and steady demand, the sector faces challenges. More job seekers are entering the IT market, but the actual number of hires has decreased. A key issue is the gap between the skills employers are looking for and the qualifications many applicants possess. While strong demand remains for roles in systems architecture, software development, and cyber security, many candidates do not meet the specific requirements for these positions.

Industry experts warn that unless this skills mismatch is addressed, South Africa’s IT sector may continue to struggle, with fewer opportunities available even as the need for advanced technical expertise grows.

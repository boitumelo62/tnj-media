Media ANC Threatens Legal Action Over DA Billboard Targeting Lesufi

March 6, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The ANC has demanded the immediate removal of a controversial Democratic Alliance election billboard that depicts Gauteng Premier Panyaza Lesufi in a shower, warning that legal action could follow if the advertisement is not taken down within 12 hours.

The billboard has sparked political tension between the two parties, with the ANC describing the imagery as offensive and defamatory. Party officials argue that the advertisement undermines the dignity of the premier and crosses the line of acceptable political campaigning.

According to the ANC, the party’s legal team has formally requested that the billboard be removed as a matter of urgency. The governing party maintains that failure to comply with the demand could result in court action.

The Democratic Alliance has defended its campaign tactics, arguing that political messaging often includes criticism of opponents and that parties must be able to hold leaders accountable through public communication.

The dispute comes as political parties intensify campaigning efforts ahead of upcoming electoral contests. Campaign advertising has become a key tool for parties seeking to influence voters and highlight their policy differences.

Political analysts say disputes over campaign messaging are common during election periods, particularly when advertisements use satire or provocative imagery.

However, the ANC insists that the billboard in question goes beyond legitimate political commentary. Party representatives argue that public messaging must still respect certain ethical and legal boundaries.

Should the matter proceed to court, it could raise broader questions about political advertising standards and the limits of satire in election campaigns.

For now, attention remains on whether the Democratic Alliance will remove the billboard or challenge the ANC’s demand through legal channels.

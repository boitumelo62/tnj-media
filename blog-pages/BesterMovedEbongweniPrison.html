Media Bester Moved to Ebongweni Prison in Security-Based Transfer

February 2, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Department of Correctional Services has confirmed that convicted criminal Thabo Bester has been transferred to Ebongweni Prison, citing security considerations as the primary reason for the move.

In a brief statement, the department said the decision forms part of ongoing measures to ensure appropriate management of high-risk inmates within the correctional system. Officials did not provide detailed operational reasons but stressed that inmate placement is continuously reviewed in line with security assessments and risk evaluations.

Bester, who was previously at the centre of one of the country’s most closely followed prison scandals, remains under strict supervision. Authorities have faced sustained public scrutiny over his incarceration since his dramatic escape in 2022, which exposed serious weaknesses within the correctional services environment. His subsequent arrest and return to custody intensified calls for tighter controls, improved oversight and greater accountability within the department.

The transfer to Ebongweni Prison is understood to be part of broader efforts to prevent any repeat of past failures. Correctional Services maintains that movements of this nature are not unusual in cases involving inmates classified as high security risks. The department added that such decisions are made to protect the integrity of the correctional system and to safeguard both staff and other inmates.

While officials have urged the public not to speculate, the move is likely to renew debate around how high-profile offenders are managed. Civil society groups have repeatedly called for transparent communication from the department, particularly in cases that have attracted significant public attention.

Correctional Services has assured South Africans that all necessary protocols are being followed and that Bester remains securely detained. The department reiterated its commitment to restoring public confidence and strengthening internal controls following past lapses that damaged trust in the system.

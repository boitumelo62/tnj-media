Media Ramaphosa Acknowledges Charges Against Police Commissioner

March 26, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

President Cyril Ramaphosa has formally acknowledged charges brought against Police Commissioner Fannie Masemola, marking a significant development in a case involving a multimillion-rand tender. The commissioner is reportedly cooperating with ongoing investigations, as authorities seek to determine the extent of any wrongdoing.

The matter has raised serious questions about governance and accountability within the country’s law enforcement structures. While details remain limited, the allegations relate to procurement processes and the awarding of a high value contract, an area often scrutinized for potential irregularities.

Ramaphosa’s response signals a commitment to due process, with the presidency emphasizing that the investigation must be allowed to proceed without interference. The situation places additional pressure on the police service, which has faced criticism in recent years over issues of leadership and integrity.

Political analysts note that how the case is handled could influence public trust in both the police and government institutions. Transparency and swift action will be key in assuring citizens that accountability mechanisms are functioning effectively.

As the investigation continues, all eyes will be on the findings and any subsequent legal proceedings. The outcome may have lasting implications for leadership within the police service and the broader fight against corruption in South Africa.

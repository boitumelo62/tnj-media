Sports New Faces and Fresh Energy as Erasmus Names 49 Man Springbok Squad

Springbok head coach Rassie Erasmus has named a 49 man squad for the national team’s first alignment camp of the year in Cape Town, including 11 uncapped players in a bold show of intent for the 2026 season.

The expanded group reflects a blend of seasoned internationals and emerging talent, as Erasmus looks to build depth and maintain competition within the squad. The alignment camp is expected to focus on conditioning, tactical planning, and reinforcing the team culture that has defined the Springboks’ recent success.

Erasmus has long emphasised the importance of succession planning, and the inclusion of uncapped players signals a deliberate effort to widen the talent pool. With a demanding international calendar ahead, squad rotation and injury management are likely to play a significant role in team selection throughout the year.

The coach indicated that the camp will not only assess on field performance but also player adaptability and understanding of the Springbok system. Alignment camps traditionally serve as a platform to ensure consistency between franchise and national structures, helping players transition smoothly into Test rugby.

For the newcomers, the call up represents an opportunity to stake a claim ahead of major fixtures later in the season. For established stars, it is a reminder that competition for places remains fierce.

As preparations intensify in Cape Town, supporters will be eager to see how the blend of experience and youthful ambition shapes the Springboks’ next chapter under Erasmus’ guidance.

Media Criminal case opened over alleged forced recruitment of South Africans for Ukraine conflict

November 24, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Nkosazana Bongonini Zuma Mncube has formally opened a criminal case following alarming reports involving seventeen South African men who were allegedly lured abroad with promises of work but found themselves forced into the conflict in Ukraine. Families reported that the men believed they were signing up for security roles, only to discover upon arrival that they had been drawn into military activity without their consent.

Zuma Mncube said the matter is both urgent and deeply troubling. She called for a full investigation to determine whether recruitment networks are operating inside South Africa and whether these networks violated any international laws. She also highlighted the emotional and psychological impact on the families, many of whom have received limited communication from their loved ones and remain desperate for verified information.

Authorities will now need to trace the recruitment path, identify the individuals or groups responsible and establish whether financial or political motives were involved. Zuma Mncube emphasised that accountability must follow once the full facts are confirmed, adding that South Africa cannot allow its citizens to be exploited in foreign conflicts.

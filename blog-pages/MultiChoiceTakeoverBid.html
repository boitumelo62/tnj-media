Business Canal+ Wins Approval in MultiChoice Takeover Bid

July 23, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Canal+, a media company based in France, has officially received the green light from South Africa’s Competition Tribunal to move forward with its takeover of MultiChoice. The deal is valued at around R53 billion. Both companies confirmed that the approval comes with a set of conditions that they previously agreed to.

These conditions are focused on protecting public interest. For instance, they include commitments to support companies that are run by Historically Disadvantaged Persons as well as Small, Micro, and Medium Enterprises working in South Africa’s audio-visual industry. There’s also a promise to continue investing in local content both general entertainment and sports so that South African audiences can still enjoy homegrown programming.

Canal+ said this approval is a major step toward finalizing the deal and that it clears the way for the companies to move forward as planned. They described the deal as a way to unite two well-known entertainment brands and build a media powerhouse focused on Africa. Now that the Tribunal has approved the merger, the companies will begin implementing a takeover plan that respects South African laws, including rules on how much foreign companies can own or control local broadcasting licenses. A key part of this structure involves separating MultiChoice’s South African business specifically the part that deals directly with local subscribers into a standalone company.

Although this is a major milestone, it’s not the end of the road. The companies still need approval from other regulators, including the Financial Surveillance Department, the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE), the Takeover Regulation Panel, and Icasa, which oversees broadcasting and communications in the country. Despite the complexity, MultiChoice says its subscribers shouldn’t worry. Day to day services won’t be affected by the behind-the-scenes work.

MultiChoice Group CEO Calvo Mawela called the announcement a big moment for both companies. He said they’re excited to move forward and build something remarkable a global entertainment business rooted in Africa.

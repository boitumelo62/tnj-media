Media Grade 10 Milnerton High Learner Attacked by Classmates in Shocking Video

October 22, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

A disturbing video showing a Grade 10 learner being assaulted by classmates at Milnerton High School has sparked outrage and prompted swift disciplinary action from the school. The footage, filmed on 16 October, shows a 16-year-old learner being surrounded, taunted, and attacked by several students while others look on.

The video began circulating widely on social media after being shared by United Democratic Movement deputy president Nqabayomzi Kwankwa, who included a statement from the victim’s mother. In the statement, she described ongoing bullying and harassment against her son by a group of older learners, some of whom are members of the school’s rugby first team. She also accused the school of not doing enough to protect her child despite repeated complaints.

In response, school principal Gert JP van Tonder confirmed that precautionary suspension letters have been issued to eight learners while a full investigation takes place. He stated that the school does not tolerate any form of bullying or intimidation and that maintaining a safe environment for all learners remains the school’s top priority.

Rumours of possible protests by learners have circulated online, but the school has assured parents that classes will continue as normal while the disciplinary process runs its course.

Members of Parliament and education advocates have condemned the incident, calling for stronger measures to prevent violence in schools. The chairperson of the Basic Education Committee, Joy Maimela, described the attack as more than just bullying, calling it an act of assault that demands serious consequences.

The investigation is still underway, with the suspended learners awaiting a formal disciplinary hearing. The Western Cape Education Department has been notified, and the school has pledged to keep the community informed as the situation develops.

Media Protest Shuts Down Durban CBD

October 28, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Durban’s central business district came to a standstill today as hundreds of protesters took to the streets demanding justice for a young man who died after falling down a lift shaft in a city building earlier this month.

The march, led by the victim’s family and community groups, blocked several main routes and halted taxi and business operations for hours. Protesters accused the building’s owners of negligence and the city of failing to enforce safety regulations.

The victim, a 24-year-old man, reportedly fell through an unsecured shaft during maintenance work. His death sparked widespread outrage and renewed scrutiny of safety compliance in aging buildings across Durban.

Municipal officials have since promised a citywide inspection of high-risk structures, while police maintained a heavy presence throughout the march. Despite tense moments, the protest remained largely peaceful as demonstrators vowed to continue until accountability is achieved.

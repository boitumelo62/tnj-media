Business WeBuyCars share price tumbles amid profit warning

October 29, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

WeBuyCars has seen its share price plunge by more than 12 percent after cautioning investors that its earnings growth slowed during the second half of its financial year. The used car trading company, listed under Transaction Capital, said weaker consumer spending and higher vehicle financing costs had put pressure on sales volumes.

Market analysts noted that the slowdown reflects broader strain in South Africa’s retail and automotive sectors, where inflation and rising interest rates have squeezed demand. The company said it is adjusting its business model to maintain profitability, focusing on operational efficiencies and streamlining inventory turnover.

Investors, however, reacted sharply to the warning, resulting in one of the biggest single-day share declines for the company this year. The firm is expected to release its full-year financial results next month, which will provide a clearer picture of the extent of the slowdown.

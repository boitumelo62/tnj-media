Media Persons of Interest Identified in Murder of Key Madlanga Commission Witness

December 9, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Police have confirmed that three individuals have now been identified as persons of interest in the murder of Marius van der Merwe, a crucial whistleblower who helped expose alleged corruption before the Madlanga Commission. His killing sent shockwaves through legal and political circles because of the central role he played in revealing irregularities tied to senior officials and business figures.

Investigators say the persons of interest were identified following an intensive review of surveillance footage, phone records and interviews with people connected to van der Merwe. While no arrests have yet been made, authorities believe the new leads significantly narrow the search for those responsible. The motive remains under investigation, but officers have noted that van der Merwes testimony had placed him under considerable pressure, raising concerns that the attack may have been targeted and deliberately planned.

Police have increased security around other witnesses linked to the commission and are urging anyone with information to step forward. The family of van der Merwe has called for justice and protection for whistleblowers across the country. The case continues to draw national attention, with many watching closely to see whether further breakthroughs will follow.

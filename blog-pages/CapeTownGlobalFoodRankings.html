Lifestyle Cape Town Continues to Impress in Global Food Rankings

December 19, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Cape Town has once again proven that it belongs on the global food map after being ranked among the top ten cities in the world for food in the 2025 Condé Nast Traveller Readers’ Choice Awards. After claiming the top spot in 2024, the city slipped down the list this year as Tokyo, Japan, took first place. Even so, Cape Town held strong at number six, and notably, it is the only city from last year’s top ten that managed to remain in the top ten in 2025.

Condé Nast described South African cuisine as a rich blend of African, Dutch, Malay and Indian influences, creating a true melting pot of flavours. From strolling along the waterfront with a glass of crisp local wine while fresh seafood and steaks are prepared nearby, to immersing yourself in the wine culture at renowned Cape Winelands estates like Babylonstoren, the city offers experiences that are hard to match. Travellers consistently point to the sheer variety of food on offer as what makes Cape Town special, with unforgettable meals paired with some of the most beautiful views in the world. Wine is often mentioned as one of the biggest highlights, thanks to the vast number of vineyards and tasting experiences available just outside the city.

Beyond food, Cape Town continues to shine for its people. In the same 2025 awards, the city was ranked the third friendliest city in the world, moving up from fifth place the year before. It also placed third overall among the best cities globally. Condé Nast acknowledged the city’s complex history, noting that while the legacy of apartheid means communities are still healing, Cape Town represents resilience, hope and a proud multicultural identity. According to the magazine, it is this ongoing effort to rebuild and embrace diversity that makes Capetonians stand out.

The rankings were based on a global Condé Nast Traveller survey that gathered more than 750,000 responses from readers worldwide. Tokyo claimed the title of the world’s best city for food in 2025, pushing Cape Town out of the top spot. Spain had a particularly strong showing, with Madrid, San Sebastian and Palma all making the top ten. The final list placed Tokyo first, followed by Santa Fe in New Mexico, Madrid, Chiang Mai, Quito, Cape Town, Lisbon, San Sebastian, Palma and New York.

Tokyo was praised for offering almost anything a food lover could imagine, from sashimi at the Tsukiji Outer Market to elaborate multi course meals at its many Michelin starred restaurants. Santa Fe stood out for its balance of fiery flavours and earthy elegance, while Madrid was celebrated for its hidden gems that locals often like to keep to themselves. Chiang Mai impressed with everything from humble noodle stalls to atmospheric riverside dining, and Quito earned recognition for chefs reinventing Ecuadorian cuisine using local ingredients.

Cape Town’s entry highlighted the city’s many distinct neighbourhoods, each with its own tastes and traditions shaping what ends up on the plate. Lisbon was applauded for everything from wine bar snacks with ocean views to classic pastel de nata, while San Sebastian’s reputation as a fine dining hotspot was reinforced by its high number of Michelin stars per capita. Palma was noted for its modern take on Mallorcan traditions, and New York rounded out the list with its fast paced, ever evolving food scene shaped by the demands of busy locals.While Cape Town may no longer sit at the very top, its continued presence in the world’s top ten food cities shows the lasting impact and global appeal of South Africa’s Mother City.

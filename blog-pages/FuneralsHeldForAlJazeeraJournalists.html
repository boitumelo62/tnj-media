Media Funerals Held for Five Al Jazeera Journalists Killed in Gaza Strike

August 12, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Funerals are being held today for five Al Jazeera journalists who were killed in an Israeli airstrike on Gaza City yesterday, the broadcaster has confirmed.

The victims, whose names have not yet been released by Al Jazeera, were reportedly covering events in the city when the strike hit their location. According to eyewitness accounts, the attack occurred during an intense wave of bombardments that struck several neighbourhoods.

In a statement, Al Jazeera condemned the killings and called for accountability, describing the deaths as part of a “pattern of targeting media workers in conflict zones.” The network urged the international community to take action to protect journalists working in war-affected areas.

Israeli authorities have not yet commented directly on the deaths but have stated that military operations in Gaza are aimed at “neutralising threats” from armed groups.

The Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) has expressed “deep concern” over the incident, noting that the latest casualties add to a growing list of media workers killed during the conflict.

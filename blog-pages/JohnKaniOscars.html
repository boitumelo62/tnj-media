Media Dr John Kani Elected to Prestigious Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

August 28, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South African theatre and film legend Dr John Kani has been elected as a member of the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, the global body best known for organising the Oscars.

Kani, whose career spans decades as an actor, playwright, and director, is one of South Africa’s most celebrated cultural figures. His election to the Academy represents a milestone not only for him personally but also for the broader African creative community, placing local artistry on the international stage.

Known globally for his work in films such as Black Panther and The Lion King, Kani has long been a powerful voice for African storytelling. At home, he remains revered for his contributions to theatre during and after apartheid, with his plays shedding light on social justice, identity, and resilience.

The Academy membership will allow him to vote in future Oscar races, helping to shape the global film industry’s recognition of talent. His inclusion underscores the growing acknowledgement of African voices and stories in Hollywood and beyond.

Industry peers and fans alike have celebrated the announcement, hailing Kani’s election as overdue recognition for a man whose influence has transcended borders. His journey, from local theatre stages to international acclaim, continues to inspire the next generation of South African creatives.

Media Another Hawks Officer Testifies Before the Madlanga Commission

October 30, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

A third Hawks officer has appeared before the Madlanga Commission of Inquiry, adding weight to mounting allegations of misconduct within the elite crime-fighting unit. The officer, whose identity has not been disclosed publicly, provided extensive testimony concerning internal operations, accountability lapses, and potential abuse of authority within the unit.

The testimony forms part of an ongoing inquiry into alleged irregularities in the Hawks’ leadership structure and its handling of corruption cases during previous administrations. The Commission, chaired by retired Justice Madlanga, is probing claims of internal sabotage, selective prosecutions, and the misuse of investigative powers.

Observers have noted growing concern within law enforcement circles as evidence mounts, suggesting systemic failures that have undermined public trust. The Commission has indicated that further witnesses, including former senior officials, are expected to testify in the coming days.

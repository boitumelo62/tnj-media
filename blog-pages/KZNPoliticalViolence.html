Media Acting Police Commissioner Raises Alarm Over Political Killing Dockets in KZN

September 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Acting national police commissioner Firoz Cachalia has expressed concern over the unresolved dockets related to political killings in KwaZulu-Natal, warning that the integrity of investigations may be at risk.

Cachalia raised the matter during discussions about the ongoing inquiry into allegations of political interference within law enforcement agencies. He cautioned that the return of these dockets without proper oversight could undermine justice for families of victims targeted in politically motivated crimes. KwaZulu-Natal has long been plagued by political violence, with task teams established to investigate dozens of cases linked to intra-party disputes, local government positions, and factional battles. Cachalia’s intervention highlights fears that interference or mishandling of evidence may derail progress in bringing perpetrators to account.

Civil society organisations have echoed his concerns, calling for urgent transparency and accountability measures. “These cases represent a dark stain on our democracy. They must be investigated without fear or favour,” said one activist.

The inquiry is expected to review the functioning of the specialised task team and whether its independence has been compromised.

Media Tributes Pour In as Music Legend Pops Mohamed Dies at 75

December 7, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Tributes continue to pour in for South African multi instrumentalist Pops Mohamed, born Ismail Mohamed Jan, who passed away on Thursday, 4 December at the age of 75. The Department of Sport, Arts and Culture confirmed his death, with Minister Gayton McKenzie describing it as the loss of a truly important figure in South Africa’s musical landscape.

Born on 10 December 1949 in Benoni, Gauteng, Pops Mohamed dedicated more than five decades to his craft. Affectionately known as the “Minister of Music,” he became celebrated for his remarkable skill across a wide range of instruments, including the kora, mbira, African mouth bow, didgeridoo, guitar and keyboard. His work reflected a deep connection to African roots and traditions, something that earned him great respect both locally and internationally.

Throughout his long career, Mohamed received numerous accolades. Among them were the South African Music Awards Lifetime Achievement Award in 2023 and the Arts & Culture Trust Lifetime Achievement Award in 2010. He also produced Ancestral Healing (from NY to Joburg) which won the FNB SAMA Award for Best Traditional Performance in its third year.

Minister McKenzie remembered him as a guardian of South Africa’s cultural heritage a teacher and visionary whose music carried the voices of ancestors and inspired younger generations to embrace their roots.

Fans, fellow musicians and admirers have taken to social media to honour Pops Mohamed, sharing memories of his warmth, wisdom and the powerful legacy he leaves behind.

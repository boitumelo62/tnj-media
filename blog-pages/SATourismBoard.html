Media Tourism Minister Patricia de Lille Dissolves SA Tourism Board

August 21, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Tourism Minister Patricia de Lille has announced the dissolution of the South African Tourism Board with immediate effect, citing concerns over governance and an unlawful resolution taken earlier this month.

The move follows mounting criticism of the board’s recent decisions, which observers say undermined transparency and accountability within the entity responsible for promoting South Africa as a global travel destination.

According to the Minister, the board acted outside its mandate when it passed a resolution deemed inconsistent with the laws governing public entities. De Lille stated that her intervention was necessary to protect the integrity of the tourism sector and ensure that the organisation operates in line with its legislative framework.

The dissolution means that an interim structure will likely be appointed to oversee the board’s functions while a permanent replacement is considered. Industry stakeholders have expressed mixed reactions, with some welcoming the decision as a step towards restoring good governance, while others fear it may disrupt ongoing projects aimed at revitalising South Africa’s tourism industry.

Tourism remains a vital sector for the country’s economy, contributing significantly to GDP and employment. The Minister emphasised that restoring stability and credibility within the board is crucial as the industry continues its recovery following years of global and domestic challenges.

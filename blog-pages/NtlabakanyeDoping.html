Sport SAIDS Confirms Ntlabakanye Eligible Despite Doping Case

August 27, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The South African Institute for Drug-Free Sport (SAIDS) has confirmed that Springbok prop Asenathi Ntlabakanye is not suspended and remains eligible to play while his doping matter is under review.

Clarification from SAIDS

SAIDS stated that although Ntlabakanye returned an adverse test result, he is not serving a provisional ban and could technically have been included in the squad to tour New Zealand. The decision to leave him out, the organisation explained, was made by South African Rugby to allow the player to focus on his case.

The Substance in Question

The flagged substance was not performance-enhancing. It had been prescribed earlier this year under medical supervision for legitimate treatment, with documentation submitted at the time. SAIDS noted that this context will be taken into account during the upcoming hearing.

Player’s Position

Ntlabakanye has insisted that he acted in good faith and under medical advice, rejecting any suggestion of wrongdoing. He has pledged to clear his name through the proper channels.

Next Steps

A hearing before an independent panel will take place in the coming weeks, operating under South Africa’s anti-doping code and international regulations. Until then, Ntlabakanye remains free to play, but his future participation with the Springboks rests on the outcome of that process.

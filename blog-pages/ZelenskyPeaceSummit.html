Media Zelensky Open to Peace Summit with Trump and Putin in Hungary

October 21, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has expressed readiness to attend a proposed peace summit in Hungary alongside US President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin if formally invited.

Speaking to reporters on Tuesday, Zelensky said Ukraine remains committed to pursuing peace through dialogue, provided the talks are held on equal terms and aim to restore stability in the region. His remarks come amid renewed diplomatic efforts to ease ongoing tensions following years of conflict and failed ceasefire agreements.

“If such a summit is confirmed, and if it truly serves the goal of achieving peace and security for Ukraine, we will be ready to participate,” Zelensky said. “Our position has always been clear, peace cannot come at the cost of sovereignty.”

The Hungarian government has not yet confirmed any official plans for the summit, but diplomatic sources suggest preliminary discussions are underway to bring all sides together. The proposed talks would mark the first time leaders from Ukraine, Russia, and the United States meet face-to-face since hostilities intensified.

Since Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, several international attempts to broker peace have faltered. The Minsk agreements, brokered years earlier, failed to hold, and subsequent rounds of negotiations yielded little progress as fighting continued in the eastern regions. Tensions between Kyiv and Moscow have remained high, with Ukraine insisting on the withdrawal of Russian forces before any lasting peace can be achieved.

Analysts say Trump’s involvement could signal a renewed attempt by Washington to reassert influence in European peace efforts, while Moscow’s participation would test its willingness to engage in meaningful negotiations. The potential summit in Hungary is being viewed as a pivotal opportunity to assess whether diplomacy can still play a role in resolving one of Europe’s most protracted conflicts.

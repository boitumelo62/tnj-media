Media Minister Pushes for Repatriation of All Foreign Nationals in SA Prisons

December 4, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Correctional Services Minister Pieter Groenewald says he wants all twenty seven thousand foreign nationals currently incarcerated in South African prisons to be repatriated to their countries of origin. He argued that the current situation places unnecessary strain on South Africa’s correctional system and diverts resources away from rehabilitation programmes for local inmates.

Groenewald said South Africa cannot continue absorbing the financial and logistical burden of housing such a large number of foreign offenders. According to the ministry, the inmate population includes individuals convicted of a wide range of crimes, and many come from countries that already have existing transfer agreements with South Africa.

Officials say discussions have started with several governments to explore ways to fast track repatriations, although the process will require complex legal coordination and diplomatic cooperation. Human rights groups have cautioned that any transfer must comply with international standards and ensure that prisoners are not moved to unsafe or abusive conditions.

The proposal is expected to spark debate, especially as the country faces ongoing pressure to reduce overcrowding in prisons. The ministry says it will provide more details on the proposed framework once consultations with affected countries progress.

Media Keneilwe Matidze on Building the Youngins

August 10, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Weekend Sports Guide – 9–10 August 2025

Actress and casting director Keneilwe Matidze has been at the heart of building the dynamic young cast of the teen drama Youngins from the very beginning. Viewers might know her as Pearl, the vibrant and energetic life orientation teacher on screen, but her contribution behind the scenes runs even deeper.

Reflecting on the casting process, Matidze shared how electric the energy was during auditions. She described a long but unforgettable day where kids turned up from early in the morning until late into the evening. Even after hours of auditions, the atmosphere remained light and joyful, with everyone dancing together outside. She said it felt so alive and captivating that, as a viewer, she would’ve been hooked from the start. The level of talent they witnessed that day, she added, was absolutely exceptional.

What they were looking for weren’t just actors, but true triple threats young people who could act, sing, and dance. She pointed out Lebo as a perfect example. Always dancing, full of life, and radiating positive energy, Lebo seemed tailor-made for his role. According to her, his growth has been inspiring starting with raw talent and evolving into a standout actor, all while keeping that infectious spirit.

Matidze also praised Tshedza Productions, the team behind Youngins, for their collaborative approach. They’re open to actors who shine during the process, encouraging the team to explore and shape roles around standout talent. One moment that stayed with her was the casting of Katlego Moloke. The way he interpreted and brought the character Alex to life surprised even her, shaping the role into something entirely fresh and unexpected.

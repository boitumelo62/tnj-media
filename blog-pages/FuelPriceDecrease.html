Business Fuel Prices Decrease Announced for November

November 4, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Department of Mineral Resources and Energy has announced a decrease in fuel prices for November, offering some relief to consumers facing ongoing cost-of-living pressures. Petrol will drop by 51 cents per litre, while diesel will decrease by up to 21 cents per litre.

The department attributed the reduction to a combination of lower international oil prices and a stronger rand against the US dollar during the past month. According to officials, global crude oil prices declined due to increased production by major oil-producing countries and slowing global demand.

Energy experts say the adjustment is a welcome reprieve for households and transport-dependent sectors, including agriculture and logistics, which have been heavily burdened by high fuel costs. The reduction is expected to ease inflationary pressure slightly, particularly on food and transport prices.

The Automobile Association welcomed the decrease but cautioned that fuel prices remain high compared to pre-pandemic levels. The organization urged government to move forward with long-term reforms to the fuel pricing system to improve transparency and reduce volatility.

Truck drivers and taxi associations across the country have expressed cautious optimism, saying that while the decrease is small, it offers temporary relief as the festive season approaches. Some logistics firms have indicated they may use the savings to offset other rising operational costs.

The department also confirmed that ongoing monitoring of international markets will determine future adjustments. It reiterated its commitment to balancing consumer protection with fiscal sustainability. While the announcement has been met with relief, economists warn that global uncertainties could still push prices upward again before year-end.

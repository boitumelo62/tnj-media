Media Crime Intelligence Chief Outlines Rise and Fall of Political Killings Unit at Madlanga Commission

November 6, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Crime Intelligence head Dumisani Khumalo appeared before the Madlanga Commission where he delivered a detailed account of how the political killings task team was formed, how it expanded, and how it eventually fell apart. His testimony added fresh insight into the inner workings of a unit that has been the subject of intense scrutiny due to concerns over political violence and the alleged manipulation of investigations.

Khumalo told the commission that the task team had initially been created in response to a wave of politically linked murders that emerged in several provinces. According to him, the team was established to centralise intelligence gathering and improve coordination between police divisions during a period when political tensions were sharply escalating.

He explained that the unit enjoyed early success and gained trust from both provincial structures and national investigators. Khumalo said it was able to identify patterns in the killings and map out areas where political contestation had become volatile. He noted that specialised officers were deployed to hotspots and were supported by analysts who tracked threats and monitored suspicious activity.

However, Khumalo said problems began to surface when the team’s mandate was repeatedly expanded without proper resources being provided. He described a growing disconnect between leadership and operational staff, which he said affected morale and weakened the unit. There were also allegations of interference by senior officials who allegedly pushed certain investigations while sidelining others.

Khumalo told the commission that internal rivalries and poor communication eventually eroded the effectiveness of the unit. He said that by the time senior management decided to disband it, the structure had become strained and was no longer delivering the results that were expected.

He stressed that while the dissolution of the task team was unfortunate, the lessons learned have informed current efforts to rebuild trust and improve intelligence capabilities. The commission is expected to continue hearing testimony from other senior officials as it examines the broader failures that contributed to the rise in political killings.

Media FlySafair Introduces Temporary Fuel Surcharge as Aviation Costs Rise

March 12, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Low cost airline FlySafair has announced that it will introduce a temporary fuel surcharge on flights until May as the company grapples with rising aviation fuel costs.

The airline confirmed that the additional charge will be applied across its ticket prices for a limited period. According to the company, the move is necessary to offset the sharp increase in jet fuel prices which has placed pressure on airline operating costs.

Fuel is one of the largest expenses for any airline and sudden price increases can significantly affect profitability. FlySafair said the surcharge will help stabilise operations while allowing the airline to maintain its current flight schedules and service levels.

The airline stressed that the surcharge is not intended to become a permanent feature of its pricing structure. Instead it will remain in place only while fuel costs remain elevated. The company said it will review the situation regularly and remove the surcharge if conditions improve before the May deadline.

Passengers may notice a slight increase in ticket prices during the period, though FlySafair maintains it will continue working to keep fares as affordable as possible. The airline has built a reputation in South Africa for competitive pricing and reliable domestic routes.

Industry analysts note that airlines around the world often introduce fuel surcharges during periods of volatile oil prices. The aviation sector remains highly sensitive to fuel fluctuations since carriers must maintain strict safety and operational standards regardless of cost pressures.

FlySafair remains one of the country’s busiest domestic carriers, connecting major cities and regional destinations. The airline says its priority is to maintain operational stability while protecting jobs and ensuring passengers continue to have access to reliable air travel.

Travellers planning trips over the next few months are encouraged to check fare details carefully as the temporary surcharge comes into effect.

Media Suspended EMPD Chief Linked to Fraudulent Tender Deal

November 13, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

New information has emerged in the corruption probe involving suspended Ekurhuleni Metropolitan Police Department (EMPD) acting head Julius Mkhwanazi, revealing his alleged role in approving a fraudulent multimillion-rand tender linked to controversial businessman Vusimuzi “Cat” Matlala.

Investigators allege that Mkhwanazi personally authorised irregular payments connected to a municipal supply contract that benefited Matlala, a well-known tender tycoon already facing multiple corruption-related charges. The deal, reportedly worth several million rand, involved inflated invoices and falsified documentation to secure municipal contracts for Matlala’s companies.

According to police sources, internal records show that Mkhwanazi signed off on at least two major transactions without proper oversight. The Special Investigating Unit (SIU) has confirmed it is probing the matter alongside the Hawks and the Ekurhuleni Integrity Unit.

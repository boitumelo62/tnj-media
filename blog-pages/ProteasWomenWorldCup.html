Sport Proteas Women Close In on World Cup Semifinal Spot

October 20, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Proteas Women are one step away from securing a spot in the ICC Women’s Cricket World Cup semifinals after a decisive win over Sri Lanka in Colombo.

The South African side put on a dominant performance, with both bat and ball working in unison to seal the crucial victory. The win now places them on the brink of qualification, capping off a strong group-stage campaign that has showcased consistency and composure.

Captain Laura Wolvaardt praised her team’s discipline, saying their focus has been on executing plans under pressure. She added that the team’s unity and hunger to reach the knockout stages have been central to their progress.

With one match remaining in the group phase, the Proteas Women are now poised to advance if results continue in their favour. Their success has been widely celebrated as a boost for South African women’s cricket, which has seen steady growth and recognition on the global stage.

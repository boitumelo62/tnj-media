Uini Atonio was admitted to La Rochelle Hospital Center yesterday after experiencing a serious heart problem. Medical examinations confirmed that he suffered a significant heart condition requiring immediate treatment. His condition is now stable, and he remains in intensive care under close medical supervision.

Doctors have confirmed that Atonio will need a lengthy period of convalescence. It has now been established that, due to the severity of his condition, he will be unable to continue his professional playing career.

A Career Defined by Power and Consistency

Uini Atonio has been one of the most prominent figures in French rugby over the past decade. Born in New Zealand and of Pacific Island heritage, he built his professional career in France, where he became a long-serving pillar of Stade Rochelais (La Rochelle) and one of the most respected tighthead props in the sport.

After joining La Rochelle in the early 2010s, Atonio played a crucial role in the club’s transformation into a European powerhouse. Known for his exceptional strength in the scrum, imposing physical presence, and remarkable durability, he was central to La Rochelle’s success in domestic competition and their achievements on the European stage.

His performances at club level earned him international honors with France after qualifying on residency grounds. Atonio went on to earn numerous caps, representing France in major international tournaments, including Rugby World Cups. He was widely valued for his experience, consistency, and leadership, particularly in the forward pack.

Atonio’s enforced retirement brings an abrupt end to a distinguished career in one of rugby’s most demanding positions. He leaves the game as a symbol of La Rochelle’s golden era and as one of the most reliable front-row players of his generation, with the rugby community now focused on his recovery and long-term health.

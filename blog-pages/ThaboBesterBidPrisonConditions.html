Media High Court Rejects Thabo Bester Bid to Challenge Prison Conditions

February 25, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Convicted murderer and rapist Thabo Bester has suffered another legal setback after the Free State High Court dismissed his application challenging his prison conditions and access to a laptop.

Bester, who is serving multiple sentences for violent crimes, had approached the court arguing that restrictions placed on him by correctional authorities were unlawful and violated his rights. He specifically sought broader access to electronic devices, claiming they were necessary for legal work and communication.

The court ruled that the Department of Correctional Services acted within its mandate and that security considerations justified the limitations placed on Bester. The judge noted that inmates do not enjoy unrestricted access to devices that could compromise prison security or facilitate further criminal activity.

Correctional Services welcomed the ruling, saying it affirmed the department’s responsibility to balance inmate rights with public safety and institutional security. Officials said policies governing inmate access to technology are designed to prevent fraud, harassment of victims and interference with ongoing investigations.

Bester remains one of South Africa’s most notorious inmates following his previous escape from custody, which triggered national outrage and an extensive investigation into prison security failures. Authorities have since tightened protocols around high risk inmates.

Legal analysts say the ruling is likely to discourage similar applications by other inmates seeking expanded privileges, particularly where security risks are evident.

The 2025 Women's Rugby World Cup is here as the 16 teams battle it out for the trophy.

Hosts England will enter as the favourites, with the world number one out to go one better than they did previously against defending champions New Zealand.

The Wallaroos are amongst a host of teams looking to break through for their maiden title and send coach Jo Yapp on a high.

Rugby.com.au breaks down everything you need to know about the 2025 Women's Rugby World Cup.

How to watch 2025 Women's Rugby World Cup

The 2025 Women's Rugby World Cup will be shown LIVE on Stan Sport and the Nine Network.
Every match of the tournament will be broadcast on Stan Sport, including every Wallaroos match.
On top of this, certain Wallaroos games will be shown on the Nine Network.

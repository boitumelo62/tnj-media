Business Fuel Prices to Drop from 2 September 2025

September 2, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South African motorists will see some relief at the pumps this week as the Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources confirmed that fuel prices will decrease from midnight on Tuesday, 2 September 2025. Petrol prices will drop by 4 cents per litre, while diesel users will benefit more significantly, with decreases of 56 cents per litre for 0.05% sulfur diesel and 57 cents per litre for 0.005% sulfur diesel.

The adjustment comes after a review of global oil market movements and exchange rate fluctuations during August. International Brent crude oil prices eased from an average of $69.06 per barrel in July to $67.01 in August. This decline, coupled with the rand strengthening slightly against the US dollar from R17.76 to R17.73 has helped push domestic fuel prices down.

The diesel cuts are expected to have a broader economic impact, particularly for industries heavily reliant on fuel such as transport, logistics, and agriculture. Petrol users, however, may find the 4c drop less noticeable in their day-to-day expenses.

Energy economists note that while the reduction is welcome, global factors such as production cuts from OPEC+, geopolitical tensions, and demand fluctuations could reverse the downward trend in coming months. Analysts warn that currency volatility remains a major risk, as even small shifts in the rand-dollar exchange rate can quickly offset the benefits of lower international oil prices.

For consumers, the September adjustment provides some breathing space ahead of the year-end period, when demand typically rises. However, long-term relief will depend on sustained stability in both global oil markets and South Africa’s economic environment.

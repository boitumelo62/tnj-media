Media Cabinet maintains calm as questions grow over possible United States visa measures

November 24, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Communications Minister Khumbudzo Ntshavheni has moved to calm rising concern following speculation that the United States may consider possible visa revocations or travel restrictions affecting South Africans. The rumour gained traction in recent days as diplomatic tensions between the two countries appeared to heighten around broader geopolitical debates.

Ntshavheni said Cabinet is not alarmed and is engaging through the appropriate diplomatic channels. She stressed that South Africa remains confident that its long standing relationship with the United States will guide both countries toward constructive resolution rather than abrupt punitive action.

The minister added that government has received no formal communication confirming any change to the current visa regime. She urged the public to remain patient and avoid circulating misleading information, noting that such speculation often creates unnecessary anxiety.

Officials in the Department of International Relations and Cooperation are monitoring developments closely. The overall position from government remains that travel arrangements for South Africans are unchanged and that responsible diplomacy will prevail.

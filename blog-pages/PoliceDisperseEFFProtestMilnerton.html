Grade 10 Milnerton High Learner Attacked by Classmates in Shocking Video

Police used stun grenades to disperse members of the EFF Youth Command outside Milnerton High School on Thursday morning, following growing outrage over a viral video showing a Grade 10 learner being assaulted by her classmates.

The video, which has sparked widespread condemnation, shows a group of older learners attacking the young pupil in what many have called a disturbing display of school violence. The footage circulated rapidly on social media, prompting public calls for accountability from the school and the Western Cape Education Department.

Protesters gathered outside the school demanding justice for the victim and calling for those responsible to face disciplinary and criminal action. The demonstration quickly intensified, leading police to intervene after protesters refused to disperse.

According to witnesses, officers fired stun grenades to control the crowd after tensions escalated. Some parents and community members joined the protest, accusing the school of mishandling the matter and failing to protect learners from bullying and violence.

The Western Cape Education Department has confirmed that an internal investigation is under way and that counselling has been arranged for the affected learner. Meanwhile, the EFF Youth Command has vowed to continue its campaign for justice, saying that violence in schools reflects broader social failures that cannot be ignored.

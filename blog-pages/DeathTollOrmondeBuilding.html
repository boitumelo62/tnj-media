Media Death Toll in Ormonde Building Collapse Rises to Nine

March 4, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The death toll following the devastating building collapse in Ormonde, south of Johannesburg has risen to nine after rescue teams recovered the body of the third and final missing construction worker.

Emergency services confirmed that search and recovery operations concluded after several days of painstaking efforts at the site. The multi storey structure collapsed while construction was underway, trapping workers beneath tons of concrete and debris.

Rescue crews worked around the clock, using heavy machinery and specialised equipment to sift through the rubble in the hope of finding survivors. While several workers were pulled out alive in the immediate aftermath, nine people ultimately lost their lives.

Authorities have not yet confirmed the cause of the collapse, but investigations are expected to focus on compliance with building regulations, structural integrity, and safety protocols at the site.

The tragedy has reignited concerns about construction safety standards and oversight, particularly in fast growing urban areas where development projects are on the rise.

Government officials have extended condolences to the families of the deceased and pledged a full investigation. Labour unions have also called for accountability, urging authorities to ensure that those responsible are held to account if negligence is found.

As families prepare to lay their loved ones to rest, questions remain about how such a catastrophic failure could occur at a construction site in one of the country’s busiest economic hubs.

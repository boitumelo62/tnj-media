Media The Media Agencies Setting New Standards

October 22, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

This year’s MOST Awards Survey saw its second highest number of participants in history, proof that it remains a key benchmark for evaluating service and partnership excellence in the media industry. According to researcher Brad Aigner, the industry still values strong, human centred connections despite all the innovation and data driven tools we now rely on. When it came to recognising standout media agencies, participants in the survey clearly knew what great service and real collaboration look like. Agencies were evaluated in two main categories: specialist agencies, which focus on one area of media or one media type; and full service agencies, which offer both media planning and buying across multiple platforms.

In the specialist category, IntiMedia took top honours for the first time. It’s clear they’ve made an impression, with survey respondents praising their mix of professionalism, data savviness, creative thinking, and down to earth relationship building. People described them as approachable, efficient, and always responsive—traits that go a long way in this industry. It’s also worth noting how often words like "partnership," "passion," and “clear communication” came up in feedback about them. FAME Media and Alphabet Soup rounded out the top three. FAME, once named the best media agency overall, was called consistent, professional, and collaborative—people who engage even when there’s no active campaign, which media owners deeply appreciate.

Alphabet Soup also drew high praise for its detailed briefing style, cultural relevance in campaigns, and genuine willingness to experiment and engage meaningfully. Their deep industry knowledge and ability to balance small agency responsiveness with big brand capability stood out clearly. In the full service agency category, PHD Cape Town walked away with a double win: they were not only the top in their category but also named Overall Best Media Agency of the year. It’s their first win as a regional office, and by all accounts, well deserved. PHD Cape Town earned respect for their strategic use of first party data, their collaborative spirit, and a culture of openness and accountability that resonates with clients and partners alike.

Participants called them forward thinking, energetic, and deeply committed to driving meaningful outcomes. The MediaShop Johannesburg came in second and continues to show why it’s a consistent powerhouse, with a long track record of wins. The agency was described as transparent, respectful, and a pioneer in integrating data into everything from out of home to digital. People trust them, and that says a lot in this competitive space. Carat Cape Town, placing third, made an impressive debut. Their culture left a mark: people consistently mentioned how welcomed and valued they felt when working with the team. They were called “a breath of fresh air” for their creativity, curiosity, and willingness to try new things.

One especially meaningful compliment came from a participant who recognised their investment in nurturing talent—something you don’t hear often enough. As for the MOST Awards' Lamb Award, which recognises agencies for excellence in service and partnership, Meta Media Johannesburg took the top spot. Respondents saw them as approachable, responsive, and always ready to help. Red Star, in second place, earned recognition for their innovation and collaborative nature, while Meta Media Cape Town, in third, was praised for their professionalism and strong client knowledge.

At the end of the day, what stood out most in all the feedback wasn’t just the clever use of data or how well agencies could navigate media complexity. What really set the best apart was how they made others feel: trusted, heard, supported. That remains the beating heart of this industry. We may have more tools at our disposal than ever before, but the real work of media is still about trust, respect, and good communication. And as the survey clearly shows, the agencies that never lose sight of that are the ones that continue to lead.

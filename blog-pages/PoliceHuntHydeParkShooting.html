Media Police Hunt for Firearm After Hyde Park Shooting Linked to Mugabe’s Son

February 20, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Police are searching for a firearm used in a shooting incident at a luxury home in Hyde Park that has been linked to Bellarmine Mugabe, the son of former Zimbabwean president Robert Mugabe.

The incident occurred at a high-end residential property in Johannesburg, where authorities responded to reports of gunfire. While details remain limited, law enforcement officials confirmed that a firearm was discharged and that an investigation is underway to establish the circumstances surrounding the shooting.

Bellarmine Mugabe was reportedly seen at the property and was later observed in handcuffs, although police have not confirmed whether he has been formally charged. Investigators are focusing on locating the weapon involved, which is considered a key piece of evidence in determining what took place and whether any criminal charges will follow.

Sources familiar with the investigation say detectives are also interviewing witnesses and reviewing security footage from the area to reconstruct the sequence of events. Hyde Park is known for its high security estates and surveillance systems, which could assist authorities in piecing together the incident.

The case has drawn public attention due to Mugabe’s high profile family background and the long- standing political legacy associated with his father’s rule in Zimbabwe. Analysts say any developments in the investigation are likely to attract regional interest given the cross border political and social implications.

Police have urged anyone with information about the firearm or the events leading up to the shooting to come forward. The investigation remains ongoing, and authorities have indicated that further updates will be provided once more facts are verified.

Business Musk Loses $15 Billion Amid Tesla Stock Plunge and Controversial Political Endorsement

July 8, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Elon Musk’s net worth took a significant hit this week as Tesla’s stock price fell sharply following his public support for the newly formed “America Party.” The tech billionaire, who recently endorsed the party’s presidential candidate, sparked renewed controversy across political and investor circles.

The dip in Tesla’s share price—over 6% in a single day—wiped out approximately $15 billion from Musk’s fortune, according to estimates from the Bloomberg Billionaires Index. Musk remains among the world’s richest individuals, but the impact was immediate and steep, underscoring how sensitive investors are to his political involvement.

The “America Party,” a recently launched political movement positioning itself as an alternative to both Democrats and Republicans, has gained attention for its populist rhetoric and anti-establishment messaging. Musk’s vocal support, delivered via a livestream and a series of social media posts, has drawn both praise and backlash.

Some investors expressed concern that Musk’s endorsement could entangle Tesla in unnecessary political controversy, potentially damaging the company’s public image and straining relationships with government agencies and regulators. Market analysts noted that Tesla’s stock has already been under pressure from increasing competition in the electric vehicle sector and softening demand in key markets like China.

This is not the first time Musk’s statements have influenced Tesla’s market performance. His past comments on politics, cryptocurrency, and company operations have frequently caused volatility in Tesla shares.

As of this week, Musk’s net worth is estimated to have dropped below $210 billion, though it remains largely tied to the performance of Tesla’s stock. Investors are now watching closely to see whether the tech mogul’s political affiliations will continue to impact the brand’s financial trajectory.

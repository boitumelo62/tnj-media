Media Parliament Committee to Finalise Witness List in Criminal Justice Corruption Probe

February 24, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Parliament’s ad hoc committee investigating alleged corruption within South Africa’s criminal justice system is set to reconvene today to finalise its witness list and consider President Cyril Ramaphosa’s submission. The committee was established to examine claims of political interference, misconduct by senior officials, and systemic failures within key institutions responsible for law enforcement and prosecution.

Committee members are expected to deliberate on which witnesses will be called to testify, including senior government officials, former prosecutors, law enforcement leaders, and independent experts. The process is seen as a critical step in determining the scope of the inquiry and ensuring that the committee has access to comprehensive evidence.

President Ramaphosa’s written submission is also on the agenda. The president has previously stressed his commitment to the rule of law and the independence of the judiciary, while acknowledging persistent challenges within the criminal justice system. His submission is expected to outline his views on the allegations and the steps taken by his administration to strengthen accountability and institutional integrity.

The inquiry has drawn significant public attention, with civil society organisations and opposition parties calling for transparency and decisive action against corruption. Critics argue that weaknesses in the criminal justice system undermine public trust and hinder efforts to combat crime and corruption.

The committee’s findings could have far reaching implications, including recommendations for policy reforms, disciplinary actions, or further investigations by law enforcement agencies. Observers say the credibility of the process will depend on the committee’s independence and its willingness to scrutinise powerful actors.

Today’s meeting marks a key milestone in the inquiry, as the final witness list will shape the direction and depth of the hearings in the weeks ahead.

Bafana Bafana Gear Up for Crucial World Cup Qualifier Against Rwanda

South Africa clinched their place in the 2026 FIFA World Cup with a commanding 3–0 victory over Rwanda at Mbombela Stadium on Tuesday evening. This marks Bafana Bafana's first qualification for the tournament since 2010 and their first as non-hosts since 2002.

The match began with immediate intensity as South Africa took control early. In the fifth minute, Oswin Appollis broke down the right wing and delivered a precise cross to Thalente Mbatha, who struck a powerful shot from outside the box to give the hosts a 1–0 lead.

Just before the half-hour mark, Appollis doubled the advantage. After a well-executed team move, he received a pass from Evidence Makgopa and finished clinically, sending South Africa into the break with a 2–0 lead.

Rwanda struggled to break down the South African defence, and in the 72nd minute, the hosts sealed the victory. Appollis delivered a corner kick that was met by Makgopa's header, finding the back of the net to make it 3–0.

With this result, South Africa topped Group C, securing their fourth World Cup appearance. The team will now shift focus to international friendlies as they prepare for the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations in Morocco.

Rwanda, on the other hand, concluded their qualifying campaign with a loss, finishing fifth in the group standings. They also failed to qualify for the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations and are expected to play friendly matches during the next FIFA break.

South Africa's qualification was further confirmed by Nigeria's 4–0 victory over Benin, which ensured the Super Eagles finished as top-ranked runners-up. This outcome solidified Bafana Bafana's position at the top of Group C.

For the first time in more than two decades South Africa has earned its World Cup spot through pure performance on the pitch. The team now looks forward to representing the nation with pride and ambition on football’s biggest stage.

As the team celebrates this significant achievement, attention now turns to preparations for the World Cup, where South Africa aims to make a strong impact on the global stage.

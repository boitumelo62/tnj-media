Media Tshwane Tender Abuse Allegations Rock Madlanga Commission Proceedings

March 25, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

A senior Tshwane Metro Police official has told the Madlanga Commission how a security tender was allegedly abused, resulting in millions of rand in losses for the municipality. The testimony forms part of ongoing investigations into governance failures and financial mismanagement within local government structures.

According to the official, irregularities in the tender process allowed for manipulation that ultimately benefited certain parties at the expense of public funds. These claims add to a growing body of evidence suggesting systemic weaknesses in procurement processes.

The commission, which has been tasked with uncovering the truth behind allegations of corruption and maladministration, has heard multiple accounts pointing to gaps in oversight and accountability. The latest testimony highlights how internal controls may have been bypassed or ignored.

Proceedings have now been paused for two weeks, giving stakeholders time to prepare further submissions and allowing investigators to review the information presented. This break is expected to be used to strengthen the case and examine additional documentation linked to the tender.

Public reaction has been mixed, with some expressing frustration over repeated revelations of financial mismanagement, while others remain hopeful that the commission will lead to meaningful reforms. Analysts note that such inquiries often play a crucial role in exposing wrongdoing but must be followed by decisive action.

The outcome of the commission’s work could have far reaching implications, including potential disciplinary measures, criminal investigations, and policy reforms aimed at preventing future abuses. For now, attention remains on the evidence being gathered and what it reveals about the state of governance in Tshwane.

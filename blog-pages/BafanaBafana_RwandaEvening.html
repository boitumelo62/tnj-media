Sport Bafana Bafana Ready For World Cup Qualifier Against Rwanda

October 14, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South Africa’s national football team, Bafana Bafana, will take to the field this evening for their World Cup qualifying match against Rwanda at Mbombela Stadium in Mpumalanga. Kick-off is scheduled for 18:00.

Coach Hugo Broos expressed confidence during a pre-match briefing, saying the squad is well prepared following intensive training sessions and strong performances in recent friendlies. “The players are focused and motivated. We know Rwanda is a disciplined side, but we plan to dominate from the first whistle,” Broos said.

Team captain Ronwen Williams echoed the coach’s sentiment, urging fans to rally behind the national side. “Playing at home is always special. We want to make the country proud,” he said.

Bafana will be without a few key players due to injuries, but Broos hinted at tactical adjustments to maintain balance in midfield and attack. Analysts expect South Africa to field an aggressive lineup, with striker Lyle Foster and winger Percy Tau expected to lead the front line.

The match is crucial for Bafana Bafana’s qualification hopes, as a victory tonight would strengthen their position in the group standings ahead of their next fixture against Sierra Leone.

Fans have begun gathering outside Mbombela Stadium since early afternoon, with security tightened and local vendors reporting brisk business ahead of the evening kickoff.

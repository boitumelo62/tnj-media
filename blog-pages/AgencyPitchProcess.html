Business Rethinking the Agency Pitch Process

October 17, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Campaigns are heating up, brand relaunches are moving faster than ever, and the need for the right digital partners isn’t a “sometime next quarter” kind of thing it’s a “needed yesterday” scenario. But when the clock is ticking, the traditional pitch process, which can drag on for months, just doesn’t cut it. That’s where the fast track pitch comes in. At its core, fast track is a condensed way of finding the right agency partner quickly but still thoughtfully.

It strips away the long lists, endless meetings, and drawn out presentations in favor of something sharper and more focused. Instead of dragging through months of back and forth, the whole process might take just a few weeks. It’s built for marketers who know what they need and don’t have time to waste. But make no mistake fast track doesn’t mean cutting corners. If anything, it demands even more behind the scenes diligence.

Instead of relying on long presentations and slick reels, it’s about having a clear view of the agency landscape who’s good at what, which agencies have the right experience, and where real cultural alignment exists. It takes strong market insight to make the right choice quickly. This approach shines in time sensitive situations. Take, for example, an automotive client who had to pivot suddenly and needed a digital media agency almost overnight. Through a fast-track process, the right partner was onboard in less than a month.

It worked because the brief was clear, the urgency was real, and the process was grounded in solid industry knowledge. Still, it’s not a one size fits-all solution. For long term, full service agency relationships, a more comprehensive approach still makes sense. But for specific, short term projects digital launches, activation campaigns, or niche B2B work fast track offers a smart, efficient way forward when speed is everything. The marketing world isn’t slowing down.

Brands that understand when and how to use faster selection methods will be better equipped to move with the market, not behind it. The goal isn't to rush it’s to respond smartly, with purpose. Because the right agency at the right time can make all the difference. And sometimes, that time is now.

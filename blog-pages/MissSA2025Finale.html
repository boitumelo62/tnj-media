Lifestyle Miss South Africa 2025 Finale Set to Inspire the Nation

October 25, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The stage is set, the spotlight is ready, and South Africa is holding its breath as a new Miss South Africa is set to be crowned tonight at the SunBet Arena in Pretoria. The highly anticipated 2025 finale marks not just the celebration of beauty, but a powerful moment of purpose, courage, and transformation.

This year’s pageant is guided by an inspiring panel of judges Dr. Tamryn Green Nxumalo, Ndavi Nokeri, Cindy Mahlangu, Heidi Giokos, and Thebe Ikalafeng all of whom represent authentic leadership and impact. Together, they will determine who among the nine remarkable finalists will wear the crown and carry the Miss South Africa legacy forward.

Each contestant has spent months preparing, not only perfecting their stage presence but also working passionately on their Empower Youth Africa campaigns. These initiatives tackle pressing national issues such as unemployment, education, mental health, and youth empowerment a reflection of Miss South Africa’s mission to drive meaningful, lasting change in society.

The competition will proceed with nine contestants after Lebohang Msimanga from KwaZulu-Natal withdrew from the Top 10. Millions of viewers across the country are expected to tune in on Mzansi Magic to witness the moment South Africa’s next queen is crowned. At a recent press conference, the finalists spoke about their causes and what motivates them to lead.

From Soweto, 27 year old Zanele Phakathi is determined to fight unemployment through career guidance. As the founder of Za Power House, she has already reached over half a million learners, opening their eyes to career paths they never knew existed. A passionate advocate for women in technology, Phakathi uses her background in coding and STEM to inspire the next generation.

Qhawekazi Mazaleni 24, a speech therapist from the Eastern Cape and master’s student specializing in autism, has dedicated her campaign to tackling illiteracy among children. She believes that literacy begins with understanding language in one’s mother tongue and has written and self published a children’s book to support that goal. Her dream is to empower teachers, parents, and young aspiring authors across the country.

Nthabiseng Kgasi 30, from Soweto, is addressing youth unemployment by promoting digital literacy. She believes that technology is the gateway to opportunity and that every young person deserves access to the skills that open doors to entrepreneurship and remote work.

Dr. Nelly Mashile 28, from Daveyton, draws her strength from the women who raised her domestic workers who inspired her resilience and compassion. She aims to use her platform to make education more accessible and to show young South Africans that hope can transform even the harshest realities.

Luyanda Zuma , 23, a model and actress, has made it her mission to uplift the boy child. Through a film-based empowerment program, she hopes to provide young men with a platform to tell their stories, develop creative skills, and secure employment opportunities.

Karabo Mareka 27, from Alexandra, is a proud advocate for global exposure among the youth. After navigating her own path abroad without a university degree, she wants to help young South Africans gain international experience and bring their skills home to contribute to local growth.

Gizelle Venske, 27, from Vanderbijlpark, is using her background as a chartered accountant to promote financial literacy. Having grown up in a family that valued education and Ubuntu, she believes it’s time young people learned how to manage money, save, and budget effectively as they step into adulthood.

Ghee-Ann Rademan , 23, from Cape Town’s Northern Suburbs, is advocating for mental health awareness. She aims to empower young people with knowledge and tools to understand and care for their emotional well being.

Bridgette Jones, 28, from Bridgetown, Athlone, is committed to creating safe spaces for young people affected by gang violence. Her dream is to build sports and recreational facilities that will serve as havens of hope, healing, and community.

As the country counts down to the crowning moment, these nine extraordinary women are ready to step onto the stage not just as contestants, but as changemakers. Tonight, South Africa will not only celebrate beauty and grace, but also the power of purpose, resilience, and leadership.

The Miss South Africa 2025 Grand Finale takes place tonight, Saturday, October 25, at 7 p.m. at the SunBet Arena in Pretoria and will be broadcast live on Mzansi Magic.

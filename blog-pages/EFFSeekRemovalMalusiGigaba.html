Media EFF to Seek Removal of Malusi Gigaba from Defence Committee Leadership

February 17, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Economic Freedom Fighters say they will formally request the removal of ANC MP Malusi Gigaba as co-chairperson of Parliament’s Joint Standing Committee on Defence, arguing that his continued presence in the role undermines public confidence in parliamentary oversight.

The party says leadership of such a sensitive committee requires the highest standards of integrity and accountability, particularly as the committee oversees the South African National Defence Force, defence policy, and military procurement. The committee also plays a role in scrutinising budgets and ensuring that the military operates within the framework of the Constitution and civilian control.

The EFF has indicated it will pursue the matter through parliamentary processes, which could include a formal motion or a written request to the relevant parliamentary structures. It is not yet clear whether other opposition parties will support the move, which would be required for any successful change in leadership given the current balance of power in Parliament.

Political analysts say the request is likely to heighten debate around parliamentary accountability and the credibility of oversight institutions, particularly in sectors that involve high levels of spending and national security considerations. Some civil society groups have previously raised concerns about transparency in defence procurement and the effectiveness of parliamentary scrutiny.

The ANC has not yet issued a response to the EFF’s announcement. Observers expect the matter to be discussed within parliamentary committees and party caucuses in the coming days.

The development comes as Parliament faces growing public pressure to strengthen ethical standards and restore trust in key oversight structures, amid broader concerns about governance and accountability across state institutions.

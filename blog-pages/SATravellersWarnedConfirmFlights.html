Media SA Travellers Warned to Confirm Flights Amid Middle East Instability

March 2, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South Africans planning to travel to the Middle East have been urged not to proceed to airports without direct confirmation from their airlines as regional instability disrupts flight schedules.

Authorities issued the advisory after heightened military tensions led to airspace restrictions, delays and cancellations across several key transit hubs. Airlines operating routes to and through affected areas are reportedly adjusting schedules at short notice in response to security assessments.

The Department of International Relations and Cooperation advised travellers to remain in close contact with airlines and travel agents before departing for airports. Officials stressed that conditions are fluid and could change rapidly depending on developments on the ground.

Several international carriers have either suspended operations to certain destinations or rerouted flights to avoid high risk airspace. This has created uncertainty for passengers connecting through major Middle Eastern airports, many of which serve as critical links between Africa, Europe and Asia.

Travel industry representatives say the situation highlights the importance of travel insurance and flexible booking options during periods of geopolitical tension. Passengers are encouraged to monitor official airline communication channels and ensure that contact details linked to their bookings are up to date.

While no blanket travel ban has been issued, authorities caution that safety considerations remain paramount. South Africans currently in the region have been advised to stay informed through official government updates and to register with consular services where possible.

With tensions showing little sign of easing, disruptions to regional aviation are expected to persist in the short term. Travellers are urged to exercise caution and plan accordingly to avoid unnecessary inconvenience or risk.

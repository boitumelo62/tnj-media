Business BankservAfrica Rebrands as PayInc to Lead South Africa’s Digital Payment Future

August 29, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

BankservAfrica has officially rebranded and will now operate under the name PayInc. This change isn’t just about a new name or look it represents a major shift in what the organisation does and how it sees its role in South Africa’s financial system. Previously known as the country’s largest clearing house, PayInc now positions itself as a key driver in the fast changing payments landscape.

This transformation is rooted in the group’s move towards becoming a full fledged digital payment provider. In 2023, it launched the PayShap instant payment system, which has been gaining traction, with all major South African banks on board and strong support from the South African Reserve Bank (SARB).

PayShap has already facilitated over R285 billion in transactions, marking a major step toward a cashless future for the country. Ownership of the group is also evolving. The Reserve Bank, which bought 50% of the company in late 2024, is now on track to take full ownership following approval from the Competition Commission. This brings PayInc fully under the SARB’s wing, aligning with national goals for secure, inclusive, and innovative payment systems.

According to PayInc’s CEO, Stephen Linnell, the new name reflects their mission: "Pay" highlights their core service, while "Inc" represents economic inclusivity. He stresses that this change goes beyond appearances it's about building on the organisation’s legacy while taking on a bigger role in shaping the future of payments in South Africa.

For over five decades, BankservAfrica played a critical role in processing various types of payments, including electronic and card transactions, and even cheques before they were phased out in 2020. Originally established in 1972 as the Automated Clearing Bureau (ACB), the organisation streamlined many fragmented payment systems into a centralised service. It later became BankservAfrica in 2010, and now, in 2025, has become PayInc.

In addition to payments, the organisation runs several key financial indices that track salaries, economic transactions, and private pensions. These have also been renamed under the new brand now known as the PayInc Net Salary Index, the PayInc Economic Index, and the PayInc Private Pensions Index.

Looking ahead, PayInc aims to continue growing digital solutions like PayShap and support cross border payments through its TCIB service (Transactions Cleared on an Immediate Basis), which is already fostering closer economic ties across Southern Africa. At its core, the rebrand signals a strong commitment to digital innovation, financial inclusion, and economic growth offering South Africans a more connected and accessible financial future.

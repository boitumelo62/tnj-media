Media GfK Wins New RAMS Contract to Lead South Africa’s Radio Audience Measurement

October 10, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

The Broadcast Research Council of South Africa (BRC) has announced that GfK Media Measurement will be taking over the contract for the country’s new Radio Audience Measurement System (RAMS). This comes after a detailed and competitive proposal process that wrapped up in June 2025. BRC CEO Gary Whitaker said they're excited about the partnership and praised GfK’s proposal for its strong methodology and global experience.

He added that this new system is designed to deliver data that is not only accurate and dependable but also forward looking helping everyone in the media, marketing, and advertising industries make better decisions. Work on the new system kicked off in July, and the data collection phase began in September 2025. The first set of insights from GfK is expected to be shared with the market in the first quarter of 2026. GfK’s Lee Risk, who leads commercial efforts for media measurement, said the team is honored to be chosen for such a key project. He emphasized that their approach blends strong technical expertise with a solid understanding of the local market, supported by best practices from around the world.

GfK brings both international experience and local know how, with a team that includes South African experts who’ve worked in this space before. Backed by their parent company NIQ and a globally respected media measurement group, GfK is confident they can deliver a system that works well for South Africa’s unique radio landscape. One of the standout features of GfK’s approach is their hybrid model, called Radio360. This combines traditional paper diaries with digital e-diaries, capturing how South Africans are listening to radio today whether through traditional stations or digital streaming. They’ve also committed to releasing data quarterly and plan to include live streaming data in future updates, making sure the system stays relevant as listening habits continue to evolve.

The new RAMS will be built on a dual recruitment method, some participants will be engaged in person, while others will join via online panels. This ensures that a wide range of people, across different demographics, are represented. With continuous data collection and regular updates, the system will help media owners, advertisers, and agencies make timely and informed decisions. As digital audio continues to grow, GfK and the BRC plan to incorporate streaming data into the system, using a model similar to what’s already being done successfully in Australia.

Their goal is to provide a modern and flexible measurement tool that supports the industry’s changing needs. Going forward, the BRC and GfK will work closely with industry players to fine tune the system and ensure it delivers value to everyone who uses it. Regular updates and consultation sessions will keep the market involved and up to speed, as South Africa prepares for the rollout of a new, world class radio measurement system in early 2026.

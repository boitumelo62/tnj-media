Tech News Apple Bets on a Reinvented Siri to Regain Its AI Credibility

Apple is preparing a major overhaul of Siri in what could become one of the most significant shifts in the company’s software strategy in years. After failing to deliver on its promise of a deeply personalized voice assistant, the tech giant is now positioning Siri for a dramatic reinvention that could place it back into the fast moving artificial intelligence race.

According to reports, Apple plans to transform Siri into a full artificial intelligence chatbot designed to compete directly with leading platforms such as ChatGPT Gemini and Claude. The new system is said to be internally known as Campos and may be tightly woven into future versions of iOS iPadOS and macOS. Rather than existing as a separate application, the chatbot would replace the current Siri experience and be accessed through familiar methods such as the Siri wake phrase or the side button on Apple devices.

The redesigned Siri is expected to support natural language conversations and perform a wide range of tasks that mirror the capabilities of modern AI assistants. These may include searching the web summarising information generating text assisting with coding and analysing files provided by the user. What could set Apple apart is the chatbot’s ability to access on device personal data and understand what is currently displayed on the screen. This would allow it to carry out complex actions such as drafting emails linked to calendar events or adjusting device settings without manual input.

Siri is also expected to be deeply integrated across Apple’s ecosystem, including core apps like Mail Photos Messages Music and TV. This level of integration could make voice control central to how users interact with their devices and may even reduce the need for features such as Spotlight search.

Privacy remains a major concern for Apple and reports suggest the company may limit how much the chatbot can remember compared to rival platforms. Apple is expected to reveal more details at its Worldwide Developers Conference in June, positioning the new Siri as a flagship feature for upcoming devices.

While these plans remain unconfirmed, Apple appears to be aiming for a decisive comeback after recent setbacks. If successful, the new Siri could redefine how users engage with their iPhones and restore Apple’s reputation as a leader in consumer innovation.

Agentic AI’s Race Against Time and It’s Losing

We’re living in a world where technology is everywhere AI tools like ChatGPT-5 are mainstream, and businesses are racing to digitise everything from customer service to product delivery. But ironically, the more digital things become, the more people are craving human connection. According to the latest South African Customer Experience Report, 93% of consumers say it’s extremely important for brands to understand their needs and emotions.

And while digital tools are still widely used especially for browsing, comparing, and buying people are returning to physical stores for the personal experience. Over half of those surveyed said being able to touch, try and buy items in-store is still a key part of how they shop. This shift has led to a new concept called Bothism a balance between digital convenience and human interaction.

Brands like Nike are embracing this, investing in physical flagship stores that offer more than just products they offer real-world experiences. It’s no longer about choosing digital or physical, but combining both in ways that matter to customers.

There’s also a growing need for empathy. With economic pressure, job uncertainty, and the fast pace of AI adoption, consumers want to feel seen and supported. But there’s a gap most consumers value empathy and honesty, while many businesses still think it’s all about their brand message. That mismatch is risky.

Ultimately, it’s the human moments, good or bad, that shape how people feel about a brand. Companies that blend tech efficiency with emotional intelligence are the ones that will win loyalty and thrive in this new era.

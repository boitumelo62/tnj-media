Business DStv Users Get More Value Than Ever After Canal+ Takeover

November 27, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

South Africans with DStv are getting a surprisingly generous set of upgrades right now free package boosts, the ability for more people to watch at once, cheaper decoders, and access to more content than before. All of this comes from DStv’s Thol iUpsize promotion, which automatically moves active decoder subscribers to a higher package without increasing their monthly fees.

If you’re on Compact Plus or Compact, you’re being bumped up to Premium at no extra cost. Customers on Family or Access are being moved to Compact. This applies to everyone existing users, new sign ups, people renewing, and even those who pay annually. It means many households are suddenly getting channels and shows that they never had access to before.

Even Premium customers are getting something extra. Their usual limit of two simultaneous streams has doubled to four, and they’re earning more DStv Rewards points than usual. Those points can be exchanged for vouchers, discounted event tickets, and other perks. DStv is also increasing how many people can watch at the same time on one Premium subscription. Until the end of 2025, up to six people can watch different channels or on demand content on one account four through DStv Stream, one on mobile, and one via the main decoder. This makes it much easier for big families or shared households to watch without fighting over the remote.

Not long ago, DStv had heavily restricted streaming to fight piracy, even limiting users to a single stream in 2021, which caused huge backlash. They later introduced a paid “Extra Stream” in 2023, but people still weren’t happy. Earlier this year they reversed course again, raising the limit to two streams for all packages and unlike Netflix, they don’t restrict streaming to a single household. Now, as part of the latest changes, up to four different households can use the same DStv Stream subscription during November and December. Decoder prices have also dropped, making it cheaper for new customers to join or for existing ones to upgrade their hardware. Altogether, these adjustments give subscribers noticeably more value without paying more.

A lot of this comes right after French media giant Canal+ completed its takeover of MultiChoice the biggest acquisition in its history. Canal+ now oversees DStv, DStv Stream, GOtv, and Showmax, serving more than 14 million users across Africa. With this new ownership, MultiChoice is shifting strategies to stay competitive against global platforms like Netflix, Disney+, and Amazon Prime Video. Canal+ CEO Maxime Saada has talked about creating a single “super app” that brings everything together: local African content, sports, international films and series, and Canal+’s European library. The idea is to make watching content as easy and enjoyable as possible.

David Mignot, who now heads Canal+ Africa and oversees MultiChoice, has said the combined catalogs of the two companies will offer far more choice. Canal+ has a massive library of European and American films, while MultiChoice remains the strongest curator of African entertainment and sport. With both under one umbrella, the group believes it can build a larger, more competitive platform. With Thol iUpsize running and the company undergoing major changes, DStv subscribers are already starting to feel the benefits.

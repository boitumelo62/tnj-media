Media Forensic Expert Links AK47 to Murders of DJ Sumbody and Others

October 29, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Police forensic analyst Solomon Modisane has testified that he was the first to conduct a comparative examination of the firearms seized from suspects accused of killing Vereeniging engineer Armand Swart in April 2024.

Testifying before the Madlanga Commission of Inquiry, Modisane revealed that the same weapons were connected to several other high-profile murders, including those of DJ Sumbody, DJ Vintos and Don Tindleni in Orlando, Soweto.

“I found that these cartridge cases were fired from the same firearm. They share a common origin,” he said. “The firearm in question is a 7.62x39mm Russian Kalashnikov AK47 assault rifle. That AK47 was recovered from suspects arrested in the Bramley case, and it is the same weapon that killed DJ Sumbody, DJ Vintos and Don Tindleni.”

Modisane explained that the ballistics process involves microscopic comparison of bullet casings and fired projectiles.

“As ballistics experts, we study both the class and individual characteristics. These are the microscopic marks imprinted by the firearm’s components on cartridge cases and bullets during firing,” he said. “The microscope allows us to identify tiny details that the naked eye cannot see, helping determine if specimens came from the same gun.”

He also raised concerns about duplicate case entries in the national Forensic Science Laboratory system. In December 2024, he discovered that the Bramley case appeared twice, one entry in Pretoria and another in KwaZulu Natal.

“It was strange to see the same case examined in Pretoria and again in KZN,” Modisane said. “Warrant Officer Keita told me that investigators from the Bramley case submitted a letter signed by Major General Mbuso Khumalo requesting that the firearms be re-tested at Amanzimtoti FSL Ballistics by an independent analyst.”

According to Modisane, investigators alleged irregularities in the initial Pretoria tests. After discussions with management, permission was granted for a re-examination in KwaZulu Natal.

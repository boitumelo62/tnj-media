Business Dis-Chem’s Founding Leader to Transition to Deputy Chairman Role in 2026

December 5, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Ivan Saltzman, the man who co founded Dis-Chem with his wife Lynette back in 1978, will step down as an executive director in June 2026. Now 75, he already handed over the CEO role in July 2023 to then CFO Rui Morais. Even after leaving his executive position, he’ll still stay involved as a non executive director and Deputy Chairman.

Dis-Chem credits Saltzman with helping grow the company from a small family pharmacy into one of South Africa’s biggest retail pharmacy and healthcare groups. Under his leadership, the business expanded rapidly, entered new regions, rolled out stores across the country, and even listed on the JSE in 2016.

Chairman Larry Nestadt praised Saltzman as a major force behind the company’s success, calling him one of the great retail leaders of his generation. He said the group is fortunate that Saltzman will remain close to the business in his new role. Saltzman himself described his journey with Dis-Chem as a privilege and said that, with a strong leadership team in place, it feels like the right moment for him to take on a more strategic position instead of being involved in daily operations.

CEO Rui Morais added that Saltzman’s influence is deeply woven into the Dis-Chem brand, saying he not only built a company but also helped shape an entire industry. As Deputy Chairman, Saltzman will continue to guide the board and support the group’s next phase of growth as it aims to strengthen its position as South Africa’s leading health authority.

This leadership shift comes at a time of broader changes within the Saltzman family’s involvement in the business. In October, Ivan and Lynette’s son Saul resigned as an executive director, effective February 2026, although he will continue to serve on the board. Lynette stepped back from her executive duties in 2022 to focus on the beauty division.

In 2025, the couple’s other sons, Dan and Mark, inherited about R6.8 billion worth of Dis- Chem shares through a restructuring of the family’s investment vehicle. Before this, the family trust, Ivlyn, held more than 29% of the company, but its stake dropped to just over 4% after the distribution. Dan and Mark now each hold around 12%, even though they previously had no direct ownership. Despite the reshuffling, the company emphasised that the shares remain within the Saltzman family, preserving the family’s long standing connection to Dis- Chem.

Media South African Ambassador Nathi Mthethwa Found Dead in Paris Hotel

October 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South Africa’s ambassador to France, Nathi Mthethwa, has been found dead in Paris under circumstances that have drawn attention just as he was expected to feature in the Madlanga Commission of Inquiry.

Mthethwa’s body was discovered on Tuesday morning in the courtyard of a hotel in the Porte Maillot district. French authorities confirmed that he had fallen from the 22nd floor. Investigators reported no signs of a physical struggle, though the window of his room had been forced open and scissors were found nearby. While initial findings point to a self-inflicted act, a full investigation has been opened.

It is believed that the night before, Mthethwa sent his wife a message apologising and suggesting that he intended to end his life. He was reported missing shortly before his body was recovered. Mthethwa had recently been linked to the Madlanga Commission which is investigating claims of political interference in policing and the justice system. Testimony before the commission accused him of attempting to shield senior police figures from disciplinary action and prosecution. He was believed to have been expected to appear and testify in the hearings.

Mthethwa was a veteran politician who served in Cabinet as Minister of Police and later as Minister of Sports, Arts and Culture before being appointed ambassador to France in 2023. President Cyril Ramaphosa extended condolences to the Mthethwa family and described the loss as deeply painful. He assured that government would support efforts to clarify the circumstances of the death.

Day nine of the Madlanga Commission, which is day three of proceedings this week, was adjourned today after Crime Intelligence head Lieutenant General Dumisani Khumalo was unavailable. It is believed that Khumalo fell ill. According to commission spokesperson, Jeremy Michaels, Madlanga resumed the commissions hearings, before Khumalo asked to be excused as he was not feeling well a few minutes after 9am. An announcement will be made throughout the day to confirm the resumption of the commission. As of time of publication, a time and date for resumption had not been confirmed.

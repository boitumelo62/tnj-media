Business Clicks Profits Surge as Retailer Plans Hundreds of New Stores

October 23, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Clicks Group has reported strong financial results for the year ending 31 August 2025, showing that its business remains solid despite economic challenges. The company recorded a 14.1% rise in diluted headline earnings per share to 1,362 cents, while its return on equity climbed to an impressive 49.2%. Group turnover also grew by 5.3%, reaching R47.8 billion. Sales from Clicks’ private label and exclusive brands rose by 10.7%, contributing R9.7 billion to total turnover.

This growth highlights how important these products have become to the group’s overall performance. Clicks also continued to expand its footprint, ending the year with more than 990 stores after opening a net 55 new ones. Its pharmacy network grew to 780, with 60 new pharmacies added during the period. The company invested R985 million in capital expenditure, mainly focused on new stores, pharmacies, refurbishments, and improvements to its supply chain and IT systems. For the 2026 financial year, Clicks plans to spend R1.3 billion on capital projects, including R662 million for new stores and pharmacies and the refurbishment of up to 80 existing stores.

The group’s medium term goal is to reach 1,200 stores and open 10 to 15 UniCare specialised pharmacies. Clicks ClubCard, which marked its 30th anniversary, continues to be a major success. Membership rose to 12.6 million, with loyalty members accounting for over 82% of sales in Clicks stores. During the year, customers received more than R850 million in cashback rewards, bringing total ClubCard rewards paid over the past three decades to R7.5 billion. Shareholders also benefited from the company’s strong performance.The total dividend for the year increased by 14.2% to 886 cents per share, with a payout ratio of 65%.

In addition to dividends, Clicks returned R751 million to shareholders through share buybacks. Looking ahead, the group expects South Africa’s economic outlook to support retail spending over the medium term, even though discretionary spending is still under strain. Clicks believes its strong position in the health and beauty sectors gives it an edge in navigating these conditions.

Management plans to build on recent investments in Sorbet, UniCare, and the LEAP pharmacy system to strengthen the group’s offering. Private label products remain central to Clicks’ growth strategy, with the company aiming for these products to contribute 35% of front shop sales in the medium term. With its continued expansion and the addition of 60 new pharmacies, Clicks remains the largest retail pharmacy group in South Africa.

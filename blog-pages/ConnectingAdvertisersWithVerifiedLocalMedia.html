Media Connecting Advertisers with Verified Local Media at Scale

August 12, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

The Digital Solutions Group (DSG) has introduced Broadbrand, a new AI-powered advertising platform designed to support independent local publishers while offering advertisers a more effective way to reach verified local audiences. Led by Vincent Maher, a recognized leader in digital media and AI in Africa, Broadbrand aggregates ad inventory from a wide range of smaller and regional publishers, including vernacular outlets, into one unified marketplace.

This platform allows advertisers to access local audiences at scale through private programmatic deals, with plans to evolve into a full programmatic exchange. What sets Broadbrand apart is its AI-driven ad optimization technology, which has shown to improve campaign performance by 30 to 50 times, making digital advertising more efficient and valuable for both publishers and advertisers. Broadbrand addresses a major challenge for small publishers, who often miss out on national digital ad budgets due to limited reach. By pooling inventory, Broadbrand offers advertisers a single point of access to many smaller outlets, simplifying media buying and increasing revenue opportunities for publishers without requiring them to expand their sales teams or infrastructure.

The platform is already gaining traction, with several community and local publishers participating in trials, alongside interest from national media buyers. Broadbrand also ensures transparency and brand safety by requiring participating publishers to be members of the Press Council, emphasizing quality and ethical journalism. Monetization works through curated private marketplace deals, offering premium, brand-safe ad space with transparent pricing and verified performance metrics. Broadbrand also provides AI-optimized creative ad services to boost engagement and integrates its inventory into client campaigns through its digital agency division.

Built over the past 18 months, Broadbrand combines technology development with strong partnerships across the local media ecosystem. In its first year, the focus will be on onboarding publishers, demonstrating superior ad performance compared to global platforms, and fostering a community of media buyers committed to supporting South African media.

In essence, Broadbrand aims to create a more inclusive, efficient, and locally driven digital advertising ecosystem that strengthens independent journalism and offers advertisers better results through smarter technology and a focus on local audiences.

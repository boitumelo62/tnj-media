Media Zuma Foundation Rejects Claims Linking Former President to Epstein

February 3, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Jacob Zuma Foundation has dismissed claims suggesting that former president Jacob Zuma had links to convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein, describing the allegations as false and without evidence.

The claims have circulated online and in parts of international media following renewed global scrutiny of Epstein's past associations. Epstein, who died in a United States prison in 2019 while awaiting trial on sex trafficking charges, was known to have social and political connections in several countries, a fact that continues to fuel speculation and conspiracy theories.

In a statement, the Zuma Foundation said there is no factual basis for any suggestion that Zuma had a relationship with Epstein, calling the claims an attempt to tarnish the former president's reputation. The foundation stressed that Zuma never met Epstein and was not involved in his activities.

Jacob Zuma served as South Africa's president from 2009 to 2018 and remains a highly polarising figure in the country's political landscape. His name frequently features in public debate due to ongoing legal challenges and controversies linked to his time in office. As a result, analysts say unverified claims involving Zuma often gain traction quickly, particularly on social media.

Political commentators have warned about the dangers of spreading unsubstantiated allegations, noting that Epstein's notoriety has made his name a magnet for misinformation. They argue that claims of association should be supported by credible evidence before being treated as fact.

The latest denial comes amid broader discussions about accountability, public trust, and the role of responsible reporting. Observers say the episode highlights the need for careful scrutiny of claims, especially when they involve serious allegations and high-profile individuals.

Media Mashatile expects SA to join next years G20 summit

December 5, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Deputy President Paul Mashatile says he believes South Africa will be present at next years G20 leaders summit in Miami. He expressed confidence that the country will be invited, despite the United States previously blocking South Africas participation in the G20 sherpa process.

Mashatile said diplomatic engagements over the past months have shifted the atmosphere and he expects the situation to improve ahead of the summit. He added that South Africa remains a crucial voice on global economic and political issues and that exclusion from the sherpa meeting should not be interpreted as a final decision about the full summit.

He insisted that the country must continue engaging its partners while strengthening internal economic reforms to reinforce its position in international forums. Mashatile also pointed out that global cooperation on energy, trade and development requires all key regions to be represented, and South Africa plays a central role in shaping the African agenda.

His comments come amid ongoing debate about the US stance toward South Africa and questions about how geopolitical tensions are influencing multilateral platforms. While government officials remain cautious, Mashatile has struck a more optimistic tone, saying South Africa will work to secure its place and ensure its voice is heard on the global stage.

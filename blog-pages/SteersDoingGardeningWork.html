Media Steers Admits Error After Staff Filmed Doing Gardening Work

January 23, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Fast food chain Steers has acknowledged that a poor decision was made after a video surfaced showing employees performing gardening duties outside one of its outlets.

The incident took place at the Menlo Park branch in Pretoria, where workers were filmed clearing weeds from the premises. The footage, which quickly spread on social media, sparked widespread criticism and accusations of worker exploitation.

In a statement posted on its official X account, Steers South Africa said an initial investigation revealed that the employees had been instructed by their manager to remove excessive weed growth because of delays in maintenance by the landlord.

The company admitted that while maintaining a clean exterior is important, gardening work should never be assigned to staff members. Steers said this is not standard practice within the brand and described the decision as unacceptable, adding that the situation reflected a clear error in judgment.

In the viral video, one of the workers is heard responding “only God know” when questioned about why they were doing the gardening work. The clip also shows the man who recorded the video confronting the store manager and taking away the gardening tools.

During the confrontation, the man accuses the store of abusing its workers and questions why such treatment is directed at Black employees. He can be heard saying that the workers are not gardeners and should not be expected to perform such tasks.

Steers has not yet indicated what disciplinary action may be taken, but the company said it is treating the matter seriously as it reviews internal processes and accountability at the store.

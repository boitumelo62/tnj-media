Sport Proteas Women Prepare for Historic World Cup Final Against India

November 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South Africa’s Proteas Women are preparing for a historic clash against India in the ICC Women’s World Cup final this Sunday, 2 November. The match will mark South Africa’s first appearance in a World Cup final, a milestone that has ignited national excitement and pride.

Coach Hilton Moreeng said the team’s focus remains on maintaining composure and executing their game plan, especially against India’s formidable batting lineup. Captain Laura Wolvaardt expressed confidence in the squad’s readiness, praising the players for their resilience throughout the tournament.

The Proteas advanced to the final after a tense semi-final victory over Australia, where all-rounder Marizanne Kapp and spinner Nonkululeko Mlaba delivered standout performances. Cricket South Africa has urged fans to rally behind the team, calling it a defining moment for women’s cricket in the country.

The final will be played at Newlands Stadium in Cape Town, with thousands expected to fill the stands and millions watching across the globe. Victory would not only secure the Proteas’ first-ever world title but also cement their rise as one of the sport’s most consistent and determined sides.

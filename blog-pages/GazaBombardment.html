Media 84 killed in Gaza as Israeli bombardment intensifies

September 25, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Gaza City was struck by one of the deadliest assaults in weeks as Israeli forces launched heavy bombardments on Wednesday, leaving at least 84 people dead and many more injured. The strikes targeted densely populated areas, sending shockwaves through neighbourhoods already devastated by weeks of violence.

The United Nations condemned the attacks, warning that the scale of destruction and loss of life is “inflicting terror” on civilians. Tens of thousands of residents have been forced to flee, turning schools, mosques, and temporary shelters into overcrowded havens. Humanitarian organizations have raised the alarm about worsening conditions, with shortages of food, clean water, and medical supplies pushing the population closer to collapse.

Witnesses described harrowing scenes of families digging through rubble in search of survivors. Hospitals, already stretched beyond capacity, have been overwhelmed by the influx of casualties. Doctors reported that many of the victims were women and children.

Diplomatic efforts to broker a ceasefire remain stalled, with international powers calling for restraint but failing to reach consensus on meaningful intervention. For now, Gaza’s residents continue to live under relentless bombardment, their futures uncertain as the violence shows no sign of abating.

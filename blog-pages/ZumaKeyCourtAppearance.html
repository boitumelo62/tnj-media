Media Former president Jacob Zuma prepares for key court appearance on legal costs ruling

November 24, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Former president Jacob Zuma is preparing for an important appearance at the Gauteng High Court on one December, where he will seek leave to appeal a ruling that ordered him to repay more than twenty eight point nine million rand spent on private legal fees during his years in office. The ruling placed a significant financial obligation on Zuma and reopened debate about the long running legal battles that have followed his presidency.

Zuma’s legal team argues that the previous judgment needs closer scrutiny and that several aspects of the decision were not properly considered. The state, on the other hand, maintains that the funds were drawn from public resources and must be reimbursed. The outcome of the appeal process may influence future rules on how public officials fund their legal defences in both personal and official matters.

The upcoming appearance is expected to draw strong public interest. Zuma remains a polarising figure in South African politics and his court proceedings continue to generate intense national attention. The case will also test how the judiciary handles long term disputes involving former heads of state and the accountability that comes with public office.

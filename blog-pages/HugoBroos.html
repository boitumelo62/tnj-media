Sport Hugo Broos Demands Focus as South Africa Face Lesotho

September 4, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Bafana Bafana coach Hugo Broos has underlined the significance of tomorrow’s FIFA World Cup qualifier against Lesotho, stressing that only a victory will be acceptable if South Africa are to keep their hopes of reaching the 2026 tournament alive.

The match, set for a 6pm kickoff, comes at a critical stage of the qualifying campaign. South Africa remain locked in a tight group where small margins could determine who progresses. Broos insisted that his team cannot afford any slip-ups, particularly against a regional rival.

“Three points are essential,” the Belgian coach said in the build-up to the game. “Every match matters in these qualifiers, and we must approach this fixture with the same seriousness as we would against the strongest opponents. The players understand the importance of maintaining focus and converting our chances.”

While Lesotho are considered underdogs, Broos warned against complacency, pointing to the unpredictable nature of regional derbies. “They will be motivated, and matches like these are never easy,” he added. “It’s up to us to impose our game from the start and show the discipline and intensity required at this level.”

Bafana Bafana’s preparations have focused on fine-tuning attacking combinations and defensive stability, with Broos keen to see an improvement in finishing. Recent performances have shown glimpses of progress, but the coach believes consistency is now key.

Fans are expected to turn out in strong numbers, creating an atmosphere that could lift the national side as they aim to secure back-to-back victories. A win would not only strengthen South Africa’s standing in the group but also build momentum heading into tougher fixtures later in the campaign.

“We are in control of our destiny,” Broos concluded. “Now we have to deliver.”

Media Nigerian Military Reports Killing 35 Jihadists in Air Strikes Near Cameroon Border

August 26, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Nigeria’s military has announced that it carried out a series of air strikes near the country’s north-eastern border with Cameroon, killing at least 35 suspected jihadists. The operation targeted hideouts believed to be used by militant groups responsible for attacks in the region.

According to military officials, the strikes were conducted after intelligence reports indicated increased militant activity in remote forested areas straddling the Nigeria-Cameroon border. Fighter jets were deployed to dismantle camps, weapons storage sites, and gathering points used by insurgents. The Nigerian Armed Forces said the operation was part of its ongoing counterinsurgency efforts against Boko Haram and Islamic State West Africa Province (ISWAP), both of which have maintained a strong presence in the northeast despite years of military campaigns.

Security experts note that while such strikes disrupt militant operations, the insurgency remains deeply rooted, with militants often regrouping in difficult-to-access territories. Communities in Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe states continue to face insecurity, displacement, and humanitarian challenges as the conflict persists. The Nigerian military reiterated its commitment to restoring peace in the northeast and protecting border communities, while urging residents to support ongoing counter-terrorism efforts.

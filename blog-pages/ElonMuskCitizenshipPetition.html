Media Petition to revoke Elon Musk’s citizenship gains support

December 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

An online petition calling for Elon Musk’s United States citizenship to be revoked has resurfaced and is gathering momentum, with more than six thousand five hundred signatures recorded so far. The campaign also demands that he be sent back to South Africa, the country of his birth.

Supporters behind the petition argue that Musk’s influence and behaviour have become harmful in the public and political space. The petition has sparked strong debate, dividing opinion between those who feel Musk should remain protected by American citizenship and others who believe he should face consequences for his conduct.

Commentary on the issue has spread across social platforms, with discussions highlighting his business decisions, public statements and growing role in global tech and politics. While the petition does not guarantee legal action, growing traction has drawn fresh attention to Musk’s citizenship and identity.

Whether the movement will translate into formal review remains uncertain, but the growing number of signatures suggests the debate surrounding Musk is far from slowing down.

Media Attempted murder case involving alleged crime boss moved to Johannesburg High Court

November 25, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The attempted murder case involving alleged crime boss Vusimuzi Cat Matlala has taken a significant turn after authorities confirmed that the matter has been transferred to the Johannesburg High Court. The move signals a new phase in a case that has drawn national attention due to the seriousness of the charges and the long list of accusations linked to Matlala and his co accused.

Matlala is facing multiple charges that include attempted murder and other serious offences connected to organised criminal activity. The state argues that the severity of the allegations requires the matter to be handled at a higher court where the full slate of evidence and witness accounts can be presented in detail.

The transfer to Johannesburg is expected to allow prosecutors to consolidate the charges and present a more comprehensive case. Both Matlala and his co accused have made several previous court appearances, and each appearance has added new layers to a case already surrounded by claims of intimidation, violence, and long standing criminal networks.

Security around the case remains tight as the court prepares for the formal start of proceedings. The state is expected to call numerous witnesses who will outline a series of events that led to the attempted murder charges. The defence has maintained that Matlala intends to challenge every allegation and insists he is being targeted due to long running turf disputes.

The Johannesburg High Court is expected to announce the full schedule of proceedings soon. The case is likely to run for several months and will remain one of the most closely watched criminal matters in the country.

Media Massive Earthquake Strikes Off Russian Coast, Triggers Pacific-Wide Tsunami Alerts

July 30, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

One of the most powerful earthquakes ever recorded struck off Russia's eastern coastline on Tuesday, July 29, setting off a tsunami that reached shores across the Pacific, including Japan, Hawaii, and parts of the United States.

The tremor, measuring a magnitude 8.8, occurred near the Kamchatka Peninsula at a depth of approximately 21 kilometers (13 miles), according to data from the U.S. Geological Survey. The quake's epicenter was located about 120 kilometers (74 miles) northwest of Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, a city with a population nearing 200,000.

The seismic event triggered widespread tsunami alerts across the Pacific basin. Regions under warning or advisory included Alaska, Hawaii, the U.S. West Coast, and even areas as far as New Zealand. While many of the alerts were being scaled back by Wednesday morning, some advisories remained active in locations such as Alaska, Oregon, and California, according to the U.S. Tsunami Warning System.

Emergency agencies continue to monitor the situation as the full extent of the impact unfolds. This is a developing story.

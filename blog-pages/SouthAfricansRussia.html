Media South Africans Trapped in Russia Describe Fear and Betrayal in WhatsApp Group

November 21, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South African Youth Offered Work in Russia But at What Cost?

A disturbing picture has emerged from a WhatsApp group chat that captures the fear and frustration of South African men who say they were lured to Russia with promises of stable work but instead found themselves pushed toward the frontlines of the war in Ukraine.

The men describe themselves as powerless and abandoned. In the messages they speak about being promised training for protection work only to discover that they were being drawn into military operations. Panic can be felt through their words as they discuss long days filled with uncertainty and the growing fear that they will not make it home.

According to the messages the group attempted to remain calm and at times received reassurance from Duduzile Zuma Sambudla who tried to keep their spirits steady. But the tone of the chat makes it clear that reassurance alone could not overcome the despair many of them were feeling.

Government sources confirm that seventeen South African men are stuck in the conflict zone. They are believed to be between the ages of twenty and thirty nine with most coming from KwaZulu Natal and one from the Eastern Cape. Officials say the men were recruited under false job offers that painted the opportunity as secure and lucrative before transporting them into a war zone.

The Presidency has launched an investigation into how these men were recruited and whether any local networks played a role in moving them into what appears to be mercenary activity. Diplomatic efforts are underway to bring them home but progress has been slow due to the volatile environment and the complex involvement of foreign forces.

Experts have warned that young South Africans are increasingly being targeted by groups offering overseas jobs that turn out to be dangerous schemes. They believe financially vulnerable candidates are the easiest to deceive with promises of high salaries and quick career growth.

For the families back home the situation has become a nightmare as they wait for updates and hope that their loved ones will return safely. For now the men continue to send messages when they can describing fear exhaustion and a growing sense that they have been used in a conflict they never agreed to join.

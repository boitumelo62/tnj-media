Business OUTsurance Champions Fair Play with Expanded Rugby Partnership

October 3, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

OUTsurance has just taken a big step in its global journey by deepening its partnership with the United Rugby Championship (URC), one of the world’s most competitive rugby leagues. This comes shortly after the company launched its operations in Ireland, showing that it’s not just expanding its business, but also its commitment to sport across both hemispheres. The URC has become a major force in rugby, especially after 2021 when four top South African teams joined what was previously known as the Pro14. That move transformed the competition, adding a new level of intensity and drawing in fans from all over the world.

With 16 teams from Ireland, South Africa, Wales, Italy, and Scotland now competing, the URC has become a global platform and the perfect space for OUTsurance to grow its presence and continue backing the game behind the scenes. Since 2015, OUTsurance has supported South African match officials by sponsoring their kits and development programs. In 2019, they extended this support to school and club level referees, helping promote safe and fair play for young athletes. The company didn’t stop there. In 2024, they partnered with South African Schools Netball to provide kits for almost 3,000 accredited umpires.

And in 2025, they became the official kit sponsor for umpires at all levels in South African hockey from school to national games. Danie Matthee, CEO of OUTsurance South Africa, summed it up best: “It’s an honour to be associated with South African match officials and now the URC as well.” He expressed excitement about future opportunities to keep supporting fairness and development in sport. This move into the URC also makes good business sense. Following their successful launch in Ireland, the URC partnership allows OUTsurance to strengthen its presence in northern hemisphere markets while staying true to its values. It’s a way to grow the brand in places where rugby is deeply rooted and appreciated.

By choosing to support match officials the unsung heroes of sport OUTsurance is reinforcing the importance of integrity, professionalism, and safety in the game. It’s a powerful statement about what the company stands for. As the new URC season begins, you’ll see referees across South Africa and Europe wearing OUTsurance branded kits in the company’s iconic green and purple. It’s more than just branding it’s a sign of their continued dedication to fair play, grassroots growth, and building a meaningful role in international sport.

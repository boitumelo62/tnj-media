Sports Erasmus Sticks to Tried and Tested Combinations for All Blacks Battle

September 4, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Springbok coach Rassie Erasmus stuck to his tried and tested combinations for their third Castle Lager Rugby Championship encounter against the All Blacks at Eden Park in Auckland on Saturday, making only four changes to the starting team that beat the Wallabies in Cape Town, with Jesse Kriel retaining the captaincy.

The changes see the return of Eben Etzebeth to the engine room alongside Ruan Nortje, in what will mark his 136th Test. Siya Kolisi returns at No 8 for his second Test in the position, while Pieter-Steph du Toit starts at flanker. The only backline change is Willie le Roux at fullback for his 101st Test.

Erasmus made three bench changes: Jan-Hendrik Wessels comes in at hooker, Kwagga Smith shifts back to the bench, and Ethan Hooker replaces Andre Esterhuizen among the backs. In total, 17 players from the Cape Town Test squad are retained.

“We selected the best possible team we could for the match, knowing that this is going to be an epic encounter against the All Blacks at Eden Park, and many of these players have done the job for us before against them,” said Erasmus.

With the Freedom Cup on the line and the chance to break an Eden Park hoodoo dating back to 1937, Erasmus acknowledged the significance: “There are records on the line for both teams… it’s going to be a proper grind.”

The Test also marks All Blacks star Ardie Savea’s 100th cap. Erasmus congratulated him, saying: “Playing 100 Tests is one of the most special milestones a player can achieve. He’s been a true servant to New Zealand rugby.”

Stats and Facts

Springbok record vs All Blacks: Played 108, Won 42, Lost 62, Drawn 4. Win %: 38.8%.

• Last Bok win at Eden Park: 1937.
• Longest Bok winning streak vs All Blacks: 6 (1937–1949).
• Eben Etzebeth extends his record as most capped Springbok (136 Tests).
• Jesse Kriel captains the Boks for the third time.
• Total caps in starting XV: 939 (Backline 434, Forwards 505).

Media Historic First: SA Performs Robotic Kidney Donor Surgery at Tygerberg Hospital

September 5, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South Africa has achieved a groundbreaking milestone in medical science with the country’s first-ever robotic kidney donor surgery, successfully performed at Tygerberg Hospital in Cape Town. The procedure, which involved a mother donating a kidney to her daughter, marks a significant step forward in advancing minimally invasive transplant surgery locally.

The operation was carried out by a team of specialist surgeons using cutting-edge robotic technology, which allows for enhanced precision, reduced blood loss, and quicker recovery times compared to traditional open surgery. Doctors hailed the achievement as a major leap for the country’s healthcare system, bringing South Africa in line with global leaders in transplant innovation.

“This isn’t just a triumph for the medical team but a symbol of hope for thousands of patients waiting for kidney transplants across the country,” said one of the attending surgeons. The patient is reported to be in stable condition and recovering well, while the donor mother has also shown positive post-operative progress.

Robotic-assisted surgery has been growing worldwide, but this successful transplant highlights South Africa’s ability to integrate advanced medical technology into public healthcare. Experts say it could pave the way for broader adoption of robotic techniques in other complex procedures.

The breakthrough comes at a time when the nation faces a high demand for organ transplants, with many patients enduring long waiting lists. Health authorities believe this milestone could accelerate investment in transplant infrastructure and provide new options for patients who previously had limited hope.

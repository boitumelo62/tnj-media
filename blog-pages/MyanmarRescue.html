Media South Africans Help Free Over a Thousand Victims from Trafficking Compound in Myanmar

October 30, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Four South African men have been hailed for their remarkable role in helping trigger the escape of about one thousand victims from a human trafficking compound in Myanmar. The men were part of a faith-based mission that led a secret prayer gathering, which inspired coordinated action among the captives at the KK Park compound.

According to reports, the compound was one of several sites linked to a transnational trafficking network that exploited people for online scams and forced labour. Among those rescued were forty-one South Africans who have since been assisted by non-governmental organisations and the Department of International Relations and Cooperation.

Human rights advocates have described the act as both brave and timely, noting that the situation in Myanmar’s trafficking zones has become a growing humanitarian crisis. The rescued individuals are now undergoing rehabilitation and reintegration support in coordination with local and international agencies.

Media ActionSA Mayor Candidate to Hand Himself to Police Over Assault Allegation

January 16, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

ActionSA has confirmed that its Ekurhuleni mayoral candidate Councillor Xolani Khumalo will voluntarily hand himself over to the South African Police Service on Monday 19 January following what the party describes as an intended arrest linked to an alleged assault case.

National chairperson Michael Beaumont said the decision comes after information emerged from within Saps suggesting Khumalo was set to be arrested. He said Khumalo acted proactively after becoming aware of the reports and engaged police to clarify their intentions.

ActionSA says the matter stems from a 2025 police operation in Katlehong where Khumalo was present during a raid on the home of a suspected drug dealer. Police allegedly found unlicensed ammunition and arrested the suspect, who was later released without appearing in court. The party claims the ammunition was returned.

According to ActionSA, the suspect later laid a charge of assault against Khumalo, which the party maintains is false and motivated by retaliation against Khumalo’s anti-crime activities in Ekurhuleni.

Beaumont accused corrupt elements within Saps of targeting individuals who confront crime while allowing criminals to operate freely. He further alleged the suspect is linked to a wider drug dealing network involving members of the police, a matter ActionSA says is under investigation.

Khumalo is also known nationally for his role in television programmes focused on crime and community safety, where he has gained prominence for confronting alleged drug dealers and exposing criminal syndicates.

Media Former School Clerk Accused of Stealing R2.8 Million

August 26, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

A former accounting clerk at Curro East Lynne School is facing charges of theft after allegedly misappropriating more than R2.8 million from the institution.

According to court filings, the clerk is accused of manipulating financial records and diverting funds over a sustained period. The alleged misconduct came to light following an internal audit, which flagged significant irregularities in the school’s accounts.

Curro East Lynne has confirmed that it is cooperating with authorities in the ongoing investigation. The school said steps have been taken to tighten financial oversight and prevent similar incidents in the future.

The accused has appeared in court and is expected to return for further proceedings as investigations continue.

If found guilty, the clerk could face a lengthy prison sentence. The case has also raised wider concerns about financial governance within private schools and the need for stronger accountability measures in educational institutions.

Business South African Brands Enter Malaysia Through New International Resturant Deal

December 10, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South Africa’s Famous Brands announced a milestone agreement with Petronas that will see a new chain of Steers and Debonairs restaurants introduced across Malaysia. The licensing deal signals a major expansion of local food brands into a fast growing Asian market, giving both companies a strategic opportunity to merge South African flavour with Malaysian consumer demand.

Executives from Famous Brands described the partnership as a step into a new era of global presence for the group. The deal opens the door for a wide rollout of stores inside Petronas owned service stations and commercial spaces, aiming to anchor the two franchises in high traffic locations. The company said it will work closely with Malaysian partners to maintain quality, adapt to local tastes where necessary and ensure consistent standards across all outlets.

Industry analysts noted that the move could become a model for future cross border licensing arrangements. It also shows renewed confidence in South African franchises as viable international players. For many South Africans working abroad, the expansion offers a familiar connection to home, while for locals in Malaysia it introduces new menu experiences supported by a brand with decades of fast food expertise.

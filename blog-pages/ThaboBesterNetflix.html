Media Thabo Bester Moves to Block Netflix Documentary Release

Convicted murderer and rapist Thabo Bester has launched legal action against Netflix in an attempt to stop the release of its upcoming documentary Beauty and the Bester.

Bester, who made global headlines after orchestrating a daring escape from the Mangaung Correctional Centre in 2022, is arguing that the production infringes on his rights and could prejudice his ongoing legal matters. The documentary, which has already generated widespread public interest, is set to explore his crimes, his escape, and his relationship with celebrity doctor Nandipha Magudumana.

Legal experts say Bester’s challenge is likely to test the balance between freedom of expression and the rights of individuals convicted of serious crimes. While Bester’s legal team insists the documentary could affect the fairness of pending proceedings, media advocates argue that his case is already a matter of overwhelming public interest.

The documentary forms part of Netflix’s growing slate of true-crime content, which has proven highly popular among viewers. Producers maintain that the series is based on verified public records and investigative reporting, rather than speculation.

Bester’s notoriety stems from his elaborate escape from prison in May 2022, when he faked his own death in a cell fire. Authorities initially believed he had died, but an investigation later revealed he was alive and living under a false identity. His escape sparked outrage over lax prison security and alleged corruption within correctional services.

Central to the saga is Dr Nandipha Magudumana, a well-known Johannesburg aesthetics doctor who was romantically linked to Bester. She allegedly played a key role in assisting his escape, including helping him establish a new life outside prison. Magudumana was arrested alongside Bester in Tanzania in April 2023 and extradited to South Africa, where she now faces a range of charges including aiding an escape and fraud.

Families of victims and civil society groups have condemned Bester’s attempt to silence the release, calling it a bid to control the narrative around his actions. “This is about accountability and truth,” one advocacy organisation said. “The public has a right to know the full story.”

The courts are expected to make a ruling in the coming weeks, which will determine whether Netflix can proceed with releasing the documentary as planned.

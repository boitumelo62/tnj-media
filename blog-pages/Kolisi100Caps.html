Sport Kolisi set to celebrate milestone as Springboks take on France in Paris

November 7, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Springbok captain Siya Kolisi is preparing for one of the most significant moments of his international career as he approaches his 100th Test cap this coming weekend. South Africa will meet France in a high stakes Autumn Nations Series match in Paris where Kolisi will lead his side in what is expected to be an emotional and hard fought encounter.

Kolisi made his Test debut in 2013 and has since grown into one of the most influential figures in South African sport. His journey has been marked by historic achievements including captaining the Springboks to two Rugby World Cup titles and becoming a global symbol of leadership and resilience.

Speaking ahead of the fixture, team management expressed confidence in Kolisi’s readiness despite a demanding season. They described him as a steady and unifying force within the squad whose experience would be vital against a French side known for its physical intensity and tactical discipline.

Fans in France and South Africa are expected to mark the milestone with strong support. Social media platforms have already been filled with messages honouring Kolisi’s contribution to the sport and his wider influence beyond the field.

The match will also carry weight for the Springboks who are eager to measure themselves against the hosts as preparations for future international competitions continue. Kolisi said his focus remains on delivering a strong team performance but acknowledged that earning 100 caps is a moment he will cherish for the rest of his life.

The game is scheduled for Saturday evening and is expected to draw a large global audience.

Media Businessman Survives Attempted Assassination in Vosloorus

November 5, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Vosloorus businessman Brown Mogotsi has reportedly survived an attempted assassination after gunmen opened fire on his vehicle late Monday night. Mogotsi, a well-known figure in the East Rand’s construction and logistics sectors, declined to open a police case, citing personal reasons and a lack of trust in local law enforcement.

According to sources close to the incident, Mogotsi was driving home when unknown assailants in a white sedan allegedly followed his car before shooting at him near the Chris Hani Crossing area. Witnesses described chaotic scenes as motorists ducked for cover during the late-night ambush. Despite several rounds hitting his car, Mogotsi escaped unharmed and drove himself to safety.

Community members have expressed concern over what appears to be a growing trend of targeted attacks on business owners across Gauteng. In recent months, police have investigated several similar shootings linked to alleged business rivalries and extortion attempts.

A Gauteng police spokesperson confirmed that officers responded to the scene but stated that no formal case could be opened since the victim chose not to pursue charges. Law enforcement has nonetheless initiated a preliminary inquiry to establish whether the attack is connected to organized crime.

Mogotsi, who has previously been involved in high-value public works contracts, is said to have received threats over the past year relating to business disputes. Security experts have urged authorities to take the matter seriously, warning that failure to protect entrepreneurs could harm economic activity and investor confidence in township business hubs.

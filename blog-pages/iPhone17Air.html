Tech News Apple Set to Launch Ultra-Slim iPhone 17 Air in September

August 18, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Apple is preparing to roll out its latest flagship smartphone range, the iPhone 17 series, with industry insiders pointing to an early September launch. The event is expected to introduce four models: the iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, and a brand-new addition, the iPhone 17 Air.

Slimmest iPhone Yet

The iPhone 17 Air is tipped to be the thinnest iPhone ever produced, measuring just 5.5 millimeters thick. Designed as a replacement for the Plus line, the Air is aimed at consumers who prefer a lightweight, streamlined device without compromising on power.

Pricing and Availability

Analysts suggest the entire lineup will see a $50 price increase compared with last year’s models. The iPhone 17 Pro is expected to start at around $1,050, while the Air could enter the market at $899. Apple is likely to follow its traditional schedule, announcing the devices during the second week of September, opening pre-orders a few days later, and delivering the first shipments by mid-month.

Features and Upgrades

All models are rumored to run on the new A19 chip, with the Pro versions powered by an enhanced A19 Pro. Memory will be upgraded to 12GB of RAM across the series, ensuring stronger multitasking and faster performance. The Air may also transition fully to eSIM technology, removing the physical SIM slot altogether.

Pro models are set to feature a striking new design with a horizontal camera bar and improved cooling systems to support high-performance tasks. Across the lineup, displays are expected to adopt LTPO ProMotion technology for smoother visuals and more efficient battery use.

Camera Advances

Apple’s focus on photography continues with major enhancements. The front-facing camera will reportedly jump to 24 megapixels, while the Pro models may receive a 48-megapixel telephoto lens. The Air will likely keep things simple with a single 48-megapixel rear camera.

Looking Ahead

With its ultra-thin design, upgraded chipsets, and improved camera systems, the iPhone 17 family signals a bold refresh for Apple’s smartphone range. If the expected September launch timeline holds, Apple fans will soon get their hands on what could be the company’s most innovative iPhone lineup yet.

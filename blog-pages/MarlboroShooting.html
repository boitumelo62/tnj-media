Media Police Investigate Marlboro Shooting Linked to Taxi Industry

November 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Police in Johannesburg have launched an investigation into a deadly shooting in Marlboro that left two people dead, including a senior official from the Alexandra, Randburg, Midrand Taxi Association.

The incident occurred late Thursday night near an industrial site, where gunmen reportedly ambushed the victims’ vehicle. Witnesses told investigators that several shots were fired before the attackers fled in a getaway car. The motive remains unclear, but detectives are probing possible links to ongoing turf battles within the taxi industry.

Gauteng police spokesperson Colonel Mavela Masondo confirmed that a task team specialising in taxi-related violence has been assigned to the case. He said authorities are following several leads and have recovered multiple cartridges from the scene for forensic analysis.

The killing has sparked renewed concern about violence within the province’s taxi sector, which has seen an escalation in shootings tied to route disputes and leadership rivalries. Community leaders have called on police to increase visibility in transport hubs and prioritise the dismantling of hit squads operating in the industry.

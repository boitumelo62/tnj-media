Media Six Milnerton High School Learners Granted Bail After Viral Assault Video

October 24, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Grade 10 Milnerton High Learner Attacked by Classmates in Shocking Video

Six learners from Milnerton High School, who were arrested in connection with the brutal assault of a fellow pupil, have been granted bail of R2,000 each by the Cape Town Magistrates Court.

The students face eight counts of assault with intent to do grievous bodily harm following an incident that was captured in a viral video earlier this month. The footage showed a group of pupils attacking another learner on school grounds, sparking widespread outrage among parents and community members.

According to the State, the assault was both premeditated and severe, resulting in significant injuries to the victim. The prosecution opposed bail, arguing that the accused could interfere with witnesses or intimidate other learners. However, the court ruled that the teenagers, all minors, should be released under strict conditions pending their next appearance.

Education authorities have confirmed that the Western Cape Education Department is conducting a separate internal investigation. The department condemned the assault, saying such behaviour “has no place in schools or society.”

Community members have since called for stronger anti-bullying measures and improved school safety protocols to prevent similar incidents.

The case has been postponed for further investigation.

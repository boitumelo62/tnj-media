Media Parliament’s Committee to Hear Testimony from Cat Matlala

November 11, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Parliament’s ad hoc committee will later this month hear testimony from attempted murder accused Vusimuzi “Cat” Matlala at Kgosi Mampuru C Max Prison in Pretoria. The committee is preparing to question Matlala as part of its broader inquiry into the handling of violent crime cases and the quality of oversight in high-security detention facilities.

Matlala has been in custody while awaiting trial on allegations linked to a violent attack that drew national attention. His court appearances have been marked by tight security, and the case has sparked ongoing debate around gang networks inside correctional centres and the broader issue of safety within prisons. Members of the committee believe that speaking directly to Matlala may help clarify how the incident unfolded and whether internal failures played any role.

Officials involved in the inquiry say the testimony will be handled under strict conditions due to the sensitivity of the case. They also expect Matlala’s legal team to be present to safeguard his rights throughout the process. The committee will then compile its report for Parliament with recommendations that may influence future procedures in cases involving serious violent crime suspects held in maximum-security facilities.

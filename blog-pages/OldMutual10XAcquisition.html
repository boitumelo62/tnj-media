Business Old Mutual tables R2.2 billion offer to acquire 10X Investments

October 29, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Financial services group Old Mutual has announced a proposed R2.2 billion deal to acquire full control of 10X Investments, one of South Africa’s leading providers of low-cost passive retirement products.

The acquisition would give Old Mutual a stronger foothold in the rapidly growing retirement savings market and boost its competitiveness against newer digital investment platforms.

Old Mutual said the transaction is in line with its strategy to expand its portfolio of affordable, tech-driven financial solutions. The company plans to integrate 10X’s products and expertise into its wealth and asset management divisions, enhancing accessibility for cost-sensitive investors.

10X Investments, founded in 2008, is known for disrupting the traditional retirement industry through its low-fee, index-tracking approach. If approved by regulators, the acquisition is expected to be finalized by mid-2026.

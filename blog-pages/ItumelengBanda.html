Media Itumeleng Banda Breaks New Ground with SuperSport Presenter Role

September 7, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Itumeleng Banda, well known for her work in TV and radio, has taken an exciting new step in her career by joining SuperSport as a presenter on Your World of Champions. For her, this isn’t just another job – it’s the realization of a long-held dream. Reflecting on the achievement, she shared that being part of SuperSport feels surreal and described it as the ultimate goal she's always worked toward. She’s excited to bring her energy, learn from the experience, and add her unique spark to the brand.

Banda’s journey to this point has been anything but ordinary. She first gained recognition as the host of the award-winning radio show Feminine First Touch, and later broke new ground by becoming the first woman to anchor the African Football League on SABC. But her contributions go beyond the broadcast booth. She’s also deeply involved in development work and advocacy, holding a SAFA D Licence in coaching and having worked in football development. Her work with the Children’s Radio Foundation took her across the African continent, where she used her voice to raise awareness about gun violence and to uplift young people.

Over the years, she’s been a presence on some of South Africa’s biggest stages – hosting events like the Momentum Gsport Awards, Basadi Music Awards, and the South African Amapiano Awards, and appearing on SABC sports shows like FootAfrique and Break Point. Through all her work, there’s a clear mission: to create platforms where young people feel seen, heard, and inspired to step confidently into their own spaces.

To those hoping to follow a similar path, Banda offers a simple but powerful piece of advice: just start. Whether it’s watching matches, writing about them, snapping photos, or launching a YouTube channel, what matters is beginning with what you have. The road may not be easy, but it’s in the process that character is built. And when challenges come, remembering your purpose – your “why” – will keep you moving forward.

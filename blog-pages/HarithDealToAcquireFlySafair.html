Business Harith Reaches Deal to Acquire FlySafair, Subject to Regulatory Approval

February 11, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Infrastructure investment firm Harith has reached a deal to acquire low-cost airline FlySafair, in a transaction that could reshape South Africa’s aviation market. The acquisition is subject to approval by the Competition Commission and aviation regulators.

FlySafair has grown into one of the country’s leading budget carriers, with a strong presence on domestic routes and expanding regional operations. The airline has built a reputation for competitive pricing, reliable operations, and a growing fleet, making it a key player in the local aviation sector.

Harith has not disclosed the value of the transaction or the structure of the deal. Industry observers say the move signals growing private sector interest in aviation and transport infrastructure, particularly as passenger demand continues to recover and expand following recent disruptions in the industry.

The Competition Commission will assess whether the acquisition could reduce competition, influence ticket prices, or increase market concentration. Aviation regulators will also review the transaction to ensure compliance with ownership, licensing, and safety requirements.

Analysts suggest the deal could bring new capital and strategic direction to FlySafair, potentially supporting fleet expansion and route development. However, they caution that regulatory scrutiny will be thorough, given the airline’s market position and the importance of maintaining fair competition.

FlySafair has indicated that there will be no immediate changes to operations, bookings, or staffing while the approval process is underway. Passengers are expected to continue flying as normal during the regulatory review period, which could take several months.

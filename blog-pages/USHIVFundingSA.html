Media US Injects $115 Million to Sustain HIV Treatment in South Africa

October 17, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South Africa has welcomed a $115 million funding package from the United States aimed at supporting HIV treatment and prevention programmes over the next six months. The initiative, described as a “bridge plan”, will ensure that life-saving services continue uninterrupted while longer-term funding arrangements are finalised.

According to the Department of Health, the support will target critical areas such as antiretroviral therapy supply, healthcare worker training, and community-based testing campaigns. The funding is expected to sustain treatment for millions of South Africans living with HIV, preventing potential medication shortages or disruptions.

Health officials have praised the move, saying it demonstrates Washington’s ongoing commitment to South Africa’s fight against HIV and AIDS. The department said the bridge plan will provide crucial breathing room as it works with the President’s Emergency Plan for AIDS Relief (PEPFAR) and other partners to strengthen the country’s response.

The latest grant comes at a time when health systems continue to recover from the strain of the COVID-19 pandemic, ensuring that prevention and treatment remain a public health priority.

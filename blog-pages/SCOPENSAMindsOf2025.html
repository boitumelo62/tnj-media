Media SCOPEN Highlights SA’s Most Influential Creative and Media Minds of 2025

December 9, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

SCOPEN has announced South Africa’s most admired creative and media agency professionals for 2025, chosen entirely through spontaneous nominations from marketers and agency peers. What makes the AGENCY SCOPE study unique is that recognition doesn’t come from panels or judges, but from the very people who work with and compete against the industry’s top talent.

This year’s research drew insights from 542 participants, including 311 marketers representing 190 brands, along with 231 agency and media professionals. The study offers one of the clearest views yet of the country’s creative, digital and media landscape. SCOPEN Global President and CEO Cesar Vacchiano describes the results as a true industry benchmark, noting that many of those who made the list also contribute significantly to the growth and development of the broader marketing community.

The professionals recognised this year stand out for their expertise, visibility, influence and measurable impact on marketing performance. Because the nominations come directly from peers, the list reflects the people who are genuinely shaping South Africa’s creative and media future.

The most admired creative agency professionals for 2025 are Pepe Marais of Joe Public, Carl Willoughby from TBWA\Hunt Lascaris, Sbu Sitole of The Odd Number, Xolisa Dyeshana from Joe Public and Ogilvy’s Pete Case. In the media category, the most admired professionals include Chris Botha of Park Advertising, Celia Collins formerly of Publicis Media, Merissa Himraj from WPP Media, Koo Govender of Publicis Media and Lerina Bierman of Dentsu Media Brands.

Together, these individuals represent the highest level of creative and strategic leadership in the industry. Their recognition is not only a reflection of personal accomplishment but also an acknowledgment of how they continue to raise industry standards and inspire the next generation of marketing talent.

Media A Year That Tested Us, A Future That Inspires The Media in 2025

It’s time for the media industry to take a well deserved breather after what has been a challenging, thought provoking and often exhilarating year in the business of media. Whether you’re unwinding on the beach, catching up on Netflix, staying in with a good book, or cooking up a storm for friends and family, we know the media never really leaves your mind. So if you do find yourself missing it, we’ve brought together all five issues of The Media magazine from 2025 in one place.

This is for those moments when you feel like catching up on what South Africa’s media professionals had to say over the past year. The 2025 editions take a clear eyed look at what we need to be aware of as we navigate the technology, services, creativity and financial pressures shaping the world right now. We asked contributors to share what they believe truly matters, and we listened closely to our audience about what they need to know. To everyone who shared their insights, foresight and hard won knowledge, thank you. The Media focuses squarely on the business of media, and our contributors offer perspectives on South Africa’s media outlook that are ruthlessly honest yet cautiously optimistic.

As Park Advertising’s Chris Botha so perfectly put it, we are all “making lemonade out of lemons”. Click on the cover to read The Business of The Media.One message comes through loud and clear mentorship and sponsorship of young women entering the media profession is absolutely vital. From journalism and media ownership to agencies and creative advertising shops, mentoring is where the real work of empowerment begins. Thank you to our contributors and commercial partners for once again making Women in The Media an issue to be proud of. These stories remind us that real power lies in collective action.

There is no doubt that the global media industry is in turmoil, but there is also no doubt that the media sector is a remarkable survivor. That resilience is reflected in the results of the 2025 MOST Awards Survey, powered by South Africa’s media professionals working on the frontline to survive, adapt and thrive despite relentless challenges. The MOST rankings offer valuable insight into what truly drives value in relationships today.

The Telkom Radio Awards edition of The Media magazine celebrated the enduring power of radio. Our contributors agree that convergence and integration are central to radio’s sustainability, even as the industry continues to grapple with the implications of artificial intelligence. This year’s Telkom Radio Awards winners are innovating, experimenting and executing at an exceptional level, ensuring that radio will not fade into history. If anything, next year’s awards will showcase the results of this bold and creative work. Congratulations to all the winners, and heartfelt thanks to our contributors. You are all stars in our book.

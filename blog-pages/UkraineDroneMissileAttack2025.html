Media Russia Launches One of Heaviest Drone and Missile Attacks on Ukraine in Weeks

August 22, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Russia has carried out a large-scale aerial assault on Ukraine, launching 574 drones and 40 missiles in one of the heaviest bombardments in recent weeks. Ukrainian officials confirmed that the overnight attack targeted multiple regions, including Kyiv, Kharkiv, and Odesa, with waves of drones and missiles striking infrastructure and residential areas.

Ukraine’s air force reported that most of the drones and a significant number of missiles were intercepted by air defense systems, but some managed to hit energy facilities and communication lines. Authorities said several injuries were recorded, while emergency services were deployed to restore electricity in areas that were left without power.

The scale of the attack highlights Moscow’s continued reliance on drone and missile campaigns as its ground forces face limited territorial gains. Analysts suggest that Russia is attempting to exhaust Ukraine’s air defenses, stretch its resources, and weaken morale by targeting critical infrastructure ahead of the winter months.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky condemned the strikes, calling them a deliberate attempt to terrorize civilians. He renewed his appeal for Western partners to accelerate deliveries of advanced air defense systems, stressing that Ukraine’s ability to protect its cities depends on continued international support.

The assault drew swift condemnation from the United States and European Union, both of which accused Moscow of escalating the conflict through indiscriminate attacks. NATO officials reiterated that member states would continue providing Ukraine with military and humanitarian assistance.

This latest barrage comes amid signs of a prolonged stalemate on the battlefield, with neither side making decisive advances. Observers warn that Russia’s intensified air campaign could mark the beginning of another winter offensive designed to test Ukraine’s resilience and international backing.

As assessments continue, Ukrainian authorities have urged citizens to remain vigilant, while recovery teams work to restore power and repair damaged infrastructure in the affected regions.

Media Five Mexican Nationals Arrested in Crystal Meth Lab Bust

September 23, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South African police have arrested five Mexican nationals after uncovering a large-scale crystal methamphetamine laboratory on a farm, in what authorities are calling one of the most significant drug busts of the year.

The operation was carried out following an intelligence-led investigation that linked the farm to a suspected international drug trafficking network. When officers raided the property, they discovered industrial-grade equipment, large quantities of precursor chemicals, and evidence of an ongoing production process.

According to police sources, the lab had the capacity to manufacture crystal meth on a commercial scale, raising concerns that South Africa is being targeted as a base of operations for international cartels. Crystal meth, commonly known as “tik” in local communities, has long been a driver of addiction and violent crime across the country.

Police spokesperson Brigadier Thembinkosi Kinana said the arrests highlighted the effectiveness of cross-border intelligence sharing. “This operation demonstrates that organised crime syndicates, regardless of their origin, will not find safe haven in South Africa,” he said.

The five suspects remain in custody and are expected to appear in court later this week. They face charges of drug manufacturing, trafficking, and contraventions of immigration laws. Investigations are ongoing to establish whether the group is linked to larger international criminal organisations.

Authorities have warned that drug syndicates are increasingly exploiting rural and remote areas to conceal their operations, underscoring the need for continued vigilance and community cooperation in reporting suspicious activity.

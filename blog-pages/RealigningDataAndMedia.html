Media Realigning Data and Media for Better Results

July 31, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Popular Blogs:

How Personal PR Shapes Your Future

Media

Influencer Marketing Strategy

In today’s complex media world, advertisers are under more pressure than ever to show results. With so many platforms and changing audience habits, proving that your ad spend is actually working isn’t just important it’s essential. Yet there’s something many brands already have that can make a huge difference: first party data.

This is the data you collect directly from your audience through your website, your app, your emails, surveys, loyalty programmes. It’s accurate, relevant, and up to date. And because it’s collected with consent, it’s also future proof in a world where cookies and third party data are becoming less reliable and harder to use. Still, there’s a problem. There’s a clear gap between the potential of first party data and how it’s actually used in practice. Often, it’s not brought into media planning early enough. It gets treated as an afterthought, when it could have been the foundation of the whole campaign.

Why does this happen? Because client teams and media buyers often operate in silos, with different goals, timelines, and processes. The data sits there ready, valuable but doesn’t get fully activated. Some of the biggest issues come down to planning. Without an integrated approach, the data doesn’t flow into the campaign in time to make a real difference. There’s also a mismatch in priorities media teams may focus on click-through rates or cost per click, while the client side might care more about long-term customer value or brand strength. Add in unclear ownership who holds the data, who’s allowed to use it, who’s responsible for protecting it and things can stall even further.

But this isn’t unsolvable. In fact, the fix is pretty straightforward. It’s not about complicated tools or major restructures. It’s about collaboration getting media buyers involved earlier, aligning goals across teams, and building shared processes where data can be used consistently from planning through to execution.

When that happens, everything becomes more effective: targeting is sharper, sequencing is smarter, and the campaign performs better. It also helps to think beyond individual platforms. Instead of building channel-specific campaigns, brands and agencies can work together to create cross-channel experiences, using the same data to guide the message across touchpoints. That makes the customer journey feel more connected and relevant.

Of course, none of this works without trust. As data privacy becomes a bigger concern globally, there needs to be clear, transparent governance. Everyone involved should understand how data is collected, stored, shared, and used. When those rules are clear, activation becomes faster and more confident.

Ultimately, what’s needed isn’t just better data usage it’s a mindset shift. From short-term campaign execution to long-term strategic planning. From working in isolation to building together. First party data holds huge potential, but unlocking it depends on the strength of the relationships between brands and their media partners. The tools are already there. The data is ready. What matters now is whether we’re willing to close the gap and work together to make smarter, more connected campaigns a reality.

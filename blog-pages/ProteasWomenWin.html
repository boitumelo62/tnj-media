Sports Proteas Women Climb to Top of World Cup Table After Pakistan Win

October 22, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South Africa’s Proteas Women have surged to the top of the Cricket World Cup standings after a convincing victory over Pakistan. The team’s latest performance has strengthened their campaign and boosted hopes of reaching the semifinals.

The Proteas delivered a composed batting display, supported by sharp fielding and disciplined bowling, securing a crucial win that moved them above Australia and India on the log. Star all-rounder Marizanne Kapp led with both bat and ball, while captain Laura Wolvaardt praised the squad’s growing confidence and teamwork.

The team will now focus on their next fixture, knowing that another win could guarantee a place in the knockout stage. The victory marks one of South Africa’s strongest starts to a World Cup campaign in recent years, reaffirming their status as one of the tournament favourites.

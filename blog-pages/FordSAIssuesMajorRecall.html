Business Ford SA Issues Major Recall Covering Vehicles Built Between 2021 and 2025

August 20, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Ford South Africa has announced a large-scale recall of thousands of vehicles manufactured between 2021 and 2025, including the popular Ranger, Everest, Puma, and EcoSport models.

The company confirmed that the recall is linked to a potential safety concern identified during routine quality checks. While Ford has not yet specified the exact nature of the defect, it said the issue could affect the safe operation of the affected vehicles and required urgent attention.

Customers will be contacted directly by dealerships and advised on the process for inspections and necessary repairs. Ford assured vehicle owners that all work carried out under the recall will be free of charge.

In a statement, the automaker said the recall reflects its commitment to ensuring the highest levels of safety and reliability across its fleet. "We are taking every step to resolve this matter swiftly and transparently. Customer safety remains our top priority," Ford South Africa noted.

The recall adds to a series of global quality control efforts by major manufacturers in recent years, as carmakers face growing scrutiny over production standards. Analysts say Ford's move is significant given the popularity of the Ranger and Everest models in the local market, where both are widely used as family and utility vehicles.

The National Consumer Commission has been notified of the recall, and Ford has urged all affected owners to respond promptly once they receive communication from the company.

Business Turning PR and Digital Marketing into a Growth Engine for Small Tech Businesses

August 18, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

For small and medium-sized businesses, especially in the tech space, investing in PR and digital marketing isn’t just a nice to have – it’s essential for building credibility and making sure the right people know who you are and what you do. These tools help establish trust, raise awareness, and ultimately drive growth.

Working with an experienced agency gives businesses access to skills and expertise they might not have in-house. It also means you get a team that understands how to align your marketing and PR efforts with your bigger business goals, instead of just throwing content out into the world and hoping it sticks. That said, many small businesses try to handle PR and marketing on their own to cut costs. While understandable, it’s important to recognise how layered and complex the field has become. It's not just about posting on social media or writing the occasional blog.

Effective PR and digital marketing involve strategy, content creation, media relations, SEO, social campaigns, lead generation, and more. These areas often need specialist knowledge to truly move the needle. If you are taking the DIY route, it’s crucial to approach things strategically. First, get crystal clear on your goals, messages, and audience before choosing your tools or channels.

Don’t try to do everything at once – focus on one or two activities that can give you the best return based on your specific goals. Pay attention to what’s happening in the world and in your industry (what’s known as newsjacking), and make sure your efforts are relevant, especially if you’re targeting media or markets outside of South Africa. Set clear expectations for what success looks like, and don’t skip the step of putting measurement in place. Tracking your impact with real data is the only way to know what’s working and where to improve.

This takes time and patience – media relationships, content effectiveness, and visibility are all built over months, not days. Doing it yourself is a learning curve, but that knowledge and those connections are valuable long term. PR and digital marketing also need to be treated as extensions of your business strategy. They can’t operate in a bubble. If the aim is to boost your brand’s visibility, build trust, generate leads, and drive sales, then these activities must be aligned.

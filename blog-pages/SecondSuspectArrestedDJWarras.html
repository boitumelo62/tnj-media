Media Second Suspect Arrested in DJ Warras Murder Case

February 2, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Police have arrested a second suspect in connection with the murder of DJ Warras, with authorities indicating that the individual is believed to be the alleged shooter.

The arrest marks a significant development in a case that has drawn widespread attention and concern within the entertainment industry and among the public. Law enforcement officials confirmed that the suspect was apprehended following ongoing investigative work and intelligence gathering.

According to police, the individual is expected to appear in court soon, where formal charges related to murder and possible additional offences will be outlined. Investigators are continuing to piece together the events leading up to the fatal shooting, with detectives working to establish motive and the roles of all parties involved.

The first suspect was arrested earlier in the investigation, and authorities have maintained that they were pursuing further leads. The breakthrough comes as police intensify efforts to curb violent crime and ensure accountability in high profile cases.

While details surrounding the circumstances of the shooting remain limited, police have urged members of the public not to speculate and to allow the legal process to unfold. They have also encouraged anyone with additional information to come forward.

The murder of DJ Warras has sent shockwaves through the local music community, with tributes continuing to pour in from fellow artists and fans. Industry figures have called for justice and greater protection for performers who often operate in vulnerable environments.

Police have reiterated their commitment to securing convictions in the matter, stating that investigations remain active. Authorities say further arrests cannot be ruled out as the case progresses through the criminal justice system.

January 12, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Africa Cup of Nations has reached its decisive stage, with the semi-final fixtures now confirmed ahead of matches scheduled for Wednesday. Senegal will face Egypt, while Nigeria are set to take on Morocco in what promises to be two high intensity encounters between some of the continent’s strongest footballing nations.

Defending champions Senegal will meet record title holders Egypt in a clash that brings together recent AFCON success and long-standing pedigree. Senegal have impressed throughout the tournament with their physical strength, tactical discipline and depth across the squad. Egypt, meanwhile, have relied on experience and composure, navigating difficult matches with resilience and strong defensive organisation. The fixture is expected to be tight, with fine margins likely to decide the outcome.

In the second semi-final, Nigeria will come up against hosts Morocco in a match that blends flair, pace and tactical variety. Nigeria have looked sharp going forward, combining youthful energy with experienced leadership. Morocco, backed by home support, have shown control in midfield and efficiency in front of goal, making them formidable opponents on familiar ground.

Both semi-finals carry significant stakes, not only for a place in the final but also for national pride and continental status. Coaches and players have stressed the importance of focus and discipline as fatigue sets in at this late stage of the tournament.

With four teams remaining, the AFCON title race is wide open. Wednesday’s matches are expected to deliver drama, quality football and moments that will shape the legacy of this year’s competition.

Media Former Radio DJ Warrick “DJ Warras” Stock Shot Dead in Johannesburg CBD

December 17, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Johannesburg is in shock following the confirmed fatal shooting of former 5FM and Cliff Central radio presenter Warrick Stock, popularly known as DJ Warras, in the city’s Central Business District on Tuesday afternoon. Stock, 40, was reportedly leaving a building near the Carlton Centre when he was ambushed and shot multiple times by assailants. Authorities have confirmed his death at the scene, marking yet another violent incident affecting South African media personalities.

Police say Johannesburg Metropolitan Police Department officers were the first responders after members of the public flagged down authorities following the shooting. Superintendent Xolani Fihla confirmed that while the JMPD attended the scene, the criminal investigation has been handed over to the South African Police Service, which is now leading inquiries into the killing.

Investigators are working to determine whether the attack was targeted. CCTV footage reportedly shows multiple suspects approaching Stock moments before the shooting, with one individual allegedly firing several rounds before the group fled. Early reports indicate that Stock may have been under threat prior to the attack, as sources claim he had filed protection orders citing security concerns in the weeks leading up to his death.

Security experts have raised concerns about the increasing number of attacks on high-profile figures, especially in the entertainment sector. Chad Thomas of IRS Forensic Investigations commented on the troubling trend of celebrity DJs falling victim to violent attacks in recent years, highlighting a possible pattern of criminal targeting within competitive or protection-related disputes.

Colleagues and friends have expressed shock and grief, describing Stock as a vibrant personality with a profound impact on South Africa’s radio landscape. Tributes on social media highlight his contribution to the industry and his warm relationships with peers and listeners.

Authorities have urged the public to refrain from speculation while the investigation continues. No arrests have yet been made, and police are calling for anyone with information about the suspects or circumstances surrounding the shooting to come forward.

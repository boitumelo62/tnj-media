Media Former Radio DJ Reportedly Killed in Johannesburg CBD Shooting

December 16, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Unverified information circulating this afternoon suggests that former 5FM radio presenter and Cliff Central DJ Warrick Stock, popularly known as DJ Warras, has been fatally shot.

Johannesburg Metropolitan Police Department spokesperson Superintendent Xolani Fihla confirmed that a shooting incident did take place in the Johannesburg central business district earlier today. He said JMPD officers were the first to arrive at the scene after being alerted by members of the public who flagged them down.

According to Fihla, officers found that a person had been shot but were unable to confirm the identity of the victim or the circumstances surrounding the incident. He added that JMPD is not leading the investigation and that the case has since been handed over to the South African Police Service.

“The department can confirm that there was a shooting this afternoon in the Johannesburg CBD. Our officers responded after being informed by the public. At this stage, we cannot provide details regarding the victim or what led to the incident,” Fihla said.

He further explained that SAPS will conduct a full criminal investigation and will release additional information once details have been formally established.

Meanwhile, Chad Thomas from IRS Forensic Investigations expressed concern about what he described as a troubling trend within the entertainment industry. He noted that it is alarming how several well known DJs and media personalities in South Africa have been victims of fatal shootings in recent years, raising questions around safety and possible links to security and protection disputes.

Authorities have urged the public to avoid speculation while investigations continue. Official confirmation regarding the identity of the victim and the motive behind the shooting is still pending.

This remains a developing story and more information will be shared as it becomes available.

Business Takealot Partners with Mr D to Enable On Demand Deliveries in Major Cities

December 3, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Takealot has rolled its TakealotNOW service directly into the main Takealot app, aiming to make shopping even faster and more convenient. The company has started a new pilot that links its on demand delivery option with the help of Mr D, allowing customers to get certain items almost immediately. When browsing the app, shoppers will now see a “Get it Now” button on products that qualify for instant delivery, and those orders will be fulfilled through Mr D.

For now, the pilot is only available in selected parts of Cape Town, Johannesburg, and Pretoria. Takealot says the goal is to make life easier by giving people what they need as quickly as possible, building on the trusted shopping experience customers already know. According to Chief Marketing Officer Karla Levick, the company wants to create an e commerce ecosystem that truly understands South Africans, and nothing makes customers happier than getting what they want within minutes.

The new integration also builds on the success of TakealotMORE, the loyalty programme launched in 2024. It already offers unlimited free delivery across Takealot, Mr D, and TakealotNOW, along with perks like free grocery delivery from Pick n Pay at in-store prices and complimentary access to News24 all for less than R4 a day. TakealotNOW takes things further by offering everyday essentials and more in just a few minutes.

Head of TakealotNOW, Marnus Engelbrecht, said that Takealot pioneered on demand delivery for general merchandise in South Africa in 2023, proving that customers want access to instant convenience. While the rollout is still in early stages, the team is excited to keep improving and speeding up the service over time.

The timing of the new feature is important for Takealot, which is working toward reporting its first full year profit in the 2026 financial year. Although the group has been operating for 15 years without posting a profit, CFO Tessa Ackermann revealed that the company did turn a profit in the most recent six month period. The group still recorded a R68 million EBIT loss in the first half of the 2026 financial year, ending in September 2025, but the profit in the second half is expected to push Takealot into positive territory. The company is optimistic, especially with the October to December period being its busiest retail season.

With the TakealotNOW integration, the company says its mission remains clear to simplify and brighten customers’ lives by delivering a faster and happier shopping experience.

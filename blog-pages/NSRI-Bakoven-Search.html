Media NSRI Continues Search for Missing Teen Near Bakoven

September 29, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The National Sea Rescue Institute has continued its search for an 18 year old from Delft who went missing at sea near Bakoven rocks in Cape Town. The incident has drawn concern from both rescue teams and the local community as the teenager was last seen entering the water over the weekend.

Search operations have involved boats, rescue swimmers, and aerial support in an effort to locate the missing youth. Strong currents and rough seas have made conditions difficult but teams have persisted in combing the area. Volunteers and emergency services have worked closely with the family who have been present at the scene waiting anxiously for news.

Residents have expressed their support for the search effort and many have offered assistance including providing food and supplies for the teams involved. The NSRI emphasized that while hope remains, time is critical in cases of this nature. The organisation has also used the opportunity to remind the public of the dangers of swimming in unmonitored areas known for unpredictable waves and rip currents.

As the search continues, the community remains united in hope that the teenager will be found and that closure can be provided for the family.

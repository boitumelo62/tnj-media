Media Malusi Gigaba Expected To Hand Himself Over Amid Transnet Corruption Case

November 10, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Former minister and ANC MP Malusi Gigaba is preparing to hand himself over to police in Pretoria on Friday as the long-running investigation into corruption linked to Transnet reaches a critical point. His legal team has been in contact with investigators and confirmed that he will cooperate with authorities ahead of his first court appearance.

Gigaba has been under scrutiny for several years over allegations tied to the state capture era. The accusations centre on claims that he used his influence during his time in Cabinet to facilitate irregular dealings within Transnet. These matters resurfaced during the Zondo Commission, where testimony pointed toward questionable procurement processes and the alleged involvement of politically connected individuals.

For months investigators have been working through witness statements, financial records, and earlier findings from state capture hearings. According to officials close to the case, the latest developments provided enough grounds to move forward with formal criminal proceedings. While the exact charges have not been confirmed, they are expected to relate to corruption and abuse of public office.

Gigaba has maintained his innocence throughout the broader state capture fallout and has repeatedly insisted that he never benefited from corrupt dealings. He is expected to make a public statement after handing himself over. ANC officials have not commented on whether the party will take internal action, saying they will wait for the legal process to unfold.

His expected arrest marks another high-profile moment in the ongoing clean-up of state institutions. It also raises questions about how many more senior political figures may still face prosecution as investigators unpack contracts and decisions made during the peak of the state capture era.

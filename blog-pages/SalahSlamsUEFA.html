Media Salah Slams UEFA Over Tribute to Palestinian Footballer

August 11, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Liverpool forward Mohamed Salah has publicly criticised UEFA for its recent tribute to late Palestinian footballer Suleiman al-Obeid, saying the message failed to acknowledge the circumstances of his death.

UEFA posted the tribute on social media following al-Obeid’s passing, but omitted any mention of the political and humanitarian context surrounding it. Al-Obeid, a former Palestine national team player, died amid the ongoing conflict in Gaza, an issue that has drawn international concern. Salah, known for using his platform to speak on humanitarian matters, expressed his disappointment in a statement on X (formerly Twitter), calling the omission “a failure to respect the truth and the struggles faced by Palestinian athletes.” His comments have reignited discussions on the role of sports bodies in addressing politically sensitive topics.

Human rights advocates have backed Salah’s stance, with the Palestinian Football Association also issuing a statement urging global sporting organisations to “tell the full story” when paying tribute to players affected by conflict.

UEFA has yet to respond publicly to Salah’s criticism.

Business Checkers Launches Largest Halaal Store in South Africa

November 3, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Checkers is expanding with new high end stores designed to meet the needs of different communities, including the largest halaal Checkers in South Africa. The retailer’s parent company, Shoprite, owns several major brands such as Shoprite, Checkers, Usave, and Sixty60. It is the biggest retailer in South Africa, leading in market value, sales, profits, employees, and customers.

The company runs more than 3,400 stores across eight Southern African countries, with supermarkets being its main focus. Over the past year, Shoprite added 281 new stores, including 184 supermarkets in South Africa alone. This growth brought in an additional R20.6 billion in sales and created over 8,700 new jobs, increasing the company’s total workforce to nearly 169,000 employees.

CEO Pieter Engelbrecht said the company is paying special attention to reaching underserved communities while continuing to grow its online grocery delivery platform, Sixty60. The Sixty60 app, which delivers groceries in as little as one hour, saw sales jump by almost 48% over the last financial year. It has expanded beyond groceries to include products from Shoprite, Petshop Science, and Medirite Plus. Sixty60 now operates in nearly 700 locations and covers over 80% of South Africa’s online shopping market.

Shoprite also acquired full ownership of Pingo Delivery, its delivery logistics partner, to strengthen its last mile capabilities. In addition, it upgraded the Sixty60 app and launched the first transactional Checkers website. Through its Checkers brand, the company is opening more premium stores to compete with Woolworths. Its first Checkers Foods store opened in the Western Cape in 2022, offering sushi, artisan breads, and restaurant quality meat. The presence of a Starbucks inside signaled a direct challenge to Woolworths Food.

Recently, Checkers opened its largest halaal certified store at Saxony Westwood Mall in Westville, KwaZulu-Natal. The store was developed with guidance from the National Independent Halaal Trust to ensure every department, from the butchery to the bakery, meets strict halaal standards. It created over 200 new jobs and provides a unique shopping experience with a mix of cultural and culinary offerings.

Shoppers can find Dubai inspired desserts, Indian style coffees and teas like Karak and Elaichi chai, wood fired tandoor dishes, and freshly made naan. The bakery sells mithais, baklava, and other Middle Eastern treats, while the butchery offers a wide selection of 100% halaal meats, including boerewors and steakhouse quality cuts. Checkers also opened a new store in Cavendish Square in Cape Town’s southern suburbs. This modern supermarket includes a bakery with artisan breads, a meat market, fresh produce, a deli, and a coffee bar.

It created 38 new jobs and is managed by Rubin Louw, who has been with the company since 2014. Overall, Shoprite and Checkers continue to grow rapidly, blending convenience, quality, and inclusivity while setting new standards in South African retail.

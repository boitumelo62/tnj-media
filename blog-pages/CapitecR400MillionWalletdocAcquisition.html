Business Capitec Expands Digital Footprint With R400 Million Walletdoc Acquisition

Capitec Bank has announced a major expansion into the digital payments sector after securing a deal to acquire full ownership of fintech company Walletdoc. The agreement, valued at roughly four hundred million rand, marks one of the banks most significant technology investments as it seeks to strengthen its position in the competitive digital finance landscape.

Walletdoc is well known for providing fast and convenient bill payment tools, merchant systems and online transaction services that are widely used by small businesses and consumers. Capitec says the acquisition will support its broader strategy to streamline digital banking by integrating seamless in app payment functions that remove the need for third party services.

Industry analysts say the move positions Capitec to compete more aggressively in the growing fintech space, where user experience and digital convenience have become key drivers of customer loyalty. They note that the acquisition also signals a broader trend of large banks buying established fintech firms rather than building solutions from scratch.

Capitec has assured existing Walletdoc users that services will continue uninterrupted during the transition period. The bank plans to roll out new features over time, including faster payment processing and expanded merchant tools designed to attract businesses looking for simpler and cost effective digital solutions. The deal is expected to be finalised once regulatory processes are complete.

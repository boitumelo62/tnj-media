Tech News Chinese AI Start Up Launches Atoms to Power One Person Businesses

January 19, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Chinese artificial intelligence start up DeepWisdom has launched Atoms, a new multi agent application designed to help solo entrepreneurs build digital products with minimal cost and effort.

The platform allows users to create websites, apps and games by describing their ideas in natural language. A virtual team of AI agents then completes the work, covering roles such as engineering, product management, data analysis and marketing, without users needing to write any code.

Founder and chief executive Alex Wu Chenglin said Atoms aims to make it easier for individuals to turn ideas into reality and operate like full organisations on their own.

Previously known as MGX, Atoms is powered mainly by open source AI models and is backed by investors including Ant Group and Baidu Ventures. Wu described the system as a move beyond basic prompt driven coding, with AI agents acting as full time digital workers.

Founded in 2019, DeepWisdom employs about 100 people and plans to double its staff next year. The company says Atoms attracted around 500,000 users within its first month and continues to see strong global growth.

Wu said AI driven labour could dramatically boost productivity in the future, placing greater value on human ideas rather than traditional coding skills.

Media Ramaphosa Engages European Leaders on Russia-Ukraine Peace Efforts

August 25, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

President Cyril Ramaphosa has held discussions with several European leaders, including French President Emmanuel Macron and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, as part of renewed international efforts to find a pathway toward peace in the Russia-Ukraine conflict.

The talks centered on exploring diplomatic solutions and reaffirmed South Africa’s position that negotiations, rather than military escalation, remain the most sustainable route to ending the war. Ramaphosa highlighted the devastating humanitarian toll of the conflict and emphasized that global cooperation is essential in restoring stability, both in Europe and in the wider international community.

This is not the first time South Africa has played a role in peacebuilding efforts linked to the war. In 2023, Ramaphosa joined a delegation of African leaders who traveled to Kyiv and Moscow to present a 10-point plan for de-escalation and humanitarian relief. While that initiative did not bring an immediate breakthrough, it underscored Africa’s commitment to contributing to global conflict resolution.

By re-engaging with European partners, Ramaphosa is seeking to strengthen South Africa’s role as a mediator and to reinforce the continent’s voice in shaping global security discussions. The Presidency noted that these latest talks form part of a broader commitment to ensuring South Africa contributes constructively to international peacebuilding.

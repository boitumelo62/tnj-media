Tech News Google moves to make Gemini easier to use on Android desktop

February 10, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Google is taking another step to expand the reach of its Gemini artificial intelligence assistant, with new updates aimed at making the tool easier to access on Android devices operating in desktop mode. The move signals the company’s broader strategy to position Gemini as a central productivity feature across phones, tablets and emerging Android desktop environments.

Android desktop mode allows smartphones to function like a traditional computer when connected to an external display, keyboard and mouse. Until now, accessing AI tools in this mode has been less intuitive, but recent changes are designed to streamline how users interact with Gemini in a larger screen setup.

According to reports, Google is refining the interface and system integration so Gemini can be launched more directly, reducing friction for users who rely on desktop mode for work or multitasking. This aligns with Google’s push to transform Android into a more versatile computing platform that can compete with desktop focused operating systems.

The update forms part of Google’s wider transition away from Google Assistant, with Gemini set to become the default digital assistant across Android devices in 2026. The company has been rolling out features that deepen Gemini’s integration into core system functions, including messaging, calling and device controls, while also expanding creative and productivity tools.

Industry analysts say improving access to Gemini on Android desktop mode could help Google attract power users and professionals who want a portable workstation powered by AI. As mobile devices become more capable, the line between smartphones and traditional PCs continues to blur, and Google appears keen to position Android as a flexible computing platform for both casual and professional use.

The changes are expected to roll out gradually through software updates, with further enhancements likely as Android desktop mode matures.

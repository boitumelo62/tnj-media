Media Showmax Shutdown Sparks Concern Over Future of Local Productions

March 9, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Uncertainty is growing within South Africa’s film and television industry following news that the streaming platform Showmax is expected to close, shifting key content decisions to the new international owners of MultiChoice.

The development has raised concerns about the future of hyperlocal productions that have become a defining feature of the platform in recent years.

Showmax has invested heavily in South African stories, producing original dramas, documentaries, and reality series that showcased local talent and culture. Industry figures now fear that these productions may face an uncertain future if programming decisions are moved outside the country.

MultiChoice recently entered into a major partnership with an international media group based in Paris, which will play a role in shaping the next phase of the company’s streaming strategy.

Producers and writers say the platform played a crucial role in funding local storytelling. For many filmmakers, Showmax offered one of the few large scale opportunities to create premium television content rooted in South African experiences.

Some industry insiders worry that international management may prioritise broader global audiences over niche regional productions. Others argue that the partnership could expand distribution opportunities for African content if handled correctly.

For now, many within the industry are waiting for clearer communication from MultiChoice about its long term strategy and the future of locally produced content.

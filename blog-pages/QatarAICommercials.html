Media Qatar Airways & Google Make History with First Inflight AI Generated Commercials

November 21, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Qatar Airways and Google have set a new benchmark for the aviation and tech industries by completing the first-ever inflight commercial production created entirely using the fastest Wi-Fi in the sky and Google’s Gemini image and video generation models. This milestone was made possible through Qatar Airways’ growing rollout of free Starlink Wi-Fi, now available on more than 100 of its Boeing 777 and Airbus A350 aircraft flying some of the world’s longest and most demanding routes.

To showcase what this connectivity can do, Qatar Airways launched the Sky Studio Challenge, an industry first creative experiment developed with Google. The idea was simple but ambitious prove that a full commercial generated, edited, finalised, and published could be completed entirely during a single flight. That challenge became reality on 17 November 2025 aboard a Starlink-equipped Qatar Airways Airbus A350 flying 15 hours from Doha to Atlanta. At an altitude of more than 10,000 metres, and using Google’s newest Gemini AI models, two complete commercials were created from scratch.

Everything, from heavy file transfers and complex scene rendering to cloud based editing and real time collaboration with teams on the ground, happened through the aircraft’s free, gate to gate high speed internet. The process relied on connection speeds comparable to home broadband something previously unheard of in aviation.To push the experiment even further, Qatar Airways brought in two of the most influential names in AI filmmaking, PJ Accetturo and Torey Kohara. Accetturo is known for visually striking, high energy storytelling, while Kohara is celebrated for cinematic, emotionally driven narratives that simplify complex tech ideas.

Their contrasting styles highlighted just how creatively flexible AI-driven production can be when backed by dependable, high speed inflight connectivity.Although many brands have filmed content inside aircraft before, none have produced complete AI-generated commercials during a single flight let alone published them before landing. Starlink’s bandwidth made it possible to handle editing, rendering, and cloud processing that traditionally require ground based systems. Both films created during the Sky Studio Challenge are now live on Qatar Airways’ YouTube channel.

The winning commercial will be chosen based on audience engagement during the first week, and viewers can also watch behind the scenes clips and footage captured onboard during the experiment.

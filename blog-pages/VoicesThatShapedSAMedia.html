Media Voices That Shaped South Africa’s Media in 2025

December 22, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

It’s time for the media industry to take a well deserved breather after a year that was tough, fascinating, and anything but dull. We hope you’re unwinding in whatever way works for you maybe on the beach, maybe binge watching Netflix, maybe curled up with a book or cooking for friends at home. And if, in between all that relaxing, you find yourself missing the media business just a little, we’ve gathered all five issues of The Media magazine from 2025 in one place for you to enjoy.

This is your chance to catch up on what South Africa’s media professionals had to say over the past year. The 2025 edition takes a clear eyed look at what we need to pay attention to as we navigate fast moving technology, evolving services, creativity under pressure, and financial markets that keep us on our toes. We asked contributors to share what they believe really matters, and we listened closely to our audience about what they need to know. Thank you to everyone who shared their insights, foresight, and hard earned knowledge.

The Media focuses firmly on the business of media, and our contributors have been characteristically honest about South Africa’s outlook. There’s no sugar coating the reality, but there is optimism too. As Park Advertising’s Chris Botha puts it, we’re “making lemonade out of lemons”. One message came through loud and clear: mentorship and sponsorship of young women entering the media profession is not optional, it’s essential. From journalism and media ownership to agencies and creative advertising shops, mentoring is where real empowerment begins. Thank you to our contributors and commercial partners for once again making the Women in The Media issue something to be proud of. These stories remind us that real power lies in collective action.

There’s no denying that the media industry globally is in turmoil, but there’s also no denying that this sector knows how to survive. That resilience is reflected in the 2025 MOST Awards Survey, powered by South Africa’s media professionals who are on the frontline every day, adapting, innovating, and pushing forward despite the challenges. The MOST rankings offer valuable insight into what truly drives value in relationships today.

The Telkom Radio Awards edition of The Media magazine put the spotlight on radio, and the message from contributors was clear. Convergence and integration are no longer nice to have they are critical to radio’s sustainability. Broadcasters are balancing this shift while also grappling with the ongoing impact of artificial intelligence. This year’s Telkom Radio Awards winners are proof that radio is far from fading away. They are innovating, experimenting, and executing with purpose, ensuring the medium will not go the way of MTV. We look forward to seeing how this exceptional work shows up in next year’s awards. Congratulations to all the winners, and thank you to every contributor. You are truly stars in our book.

Media Government Declares Full Readiness for G20 as International Attendance Shifts

November 14, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Cabinet announced today that preparations for the upcoming G20 Leaders Summit are complete, with officials expressing full confidence in South Africa’s capacity to host the event. The summit, which is now nine days away, has been described as a significant opportunity for the country to showcase stability, organisational efficiency, and diplomatic leadership.

Government spokespersons confirmed that security deployments, venue logistics, transport coordination, and media infrastructure are fully in place. Special attention has been given to crowd management, emergency response plans, and international protocol arrangements to ensure the comfort and safety of global delegates. However, the international attendance profile of the summit has shifted following confirmed updates from two of the world’s most influential nations. Chinese President Xi Jinping will not be present, with Premier Li Qiang representing China instead. In addition, United States President Donald Trump has indicated that his senior officials will not attend the conference. Analysts say the absence of these key leaders may influence the tone and intensity of discussions but should not disrupt the agenda entirely.

Experts note that fluctuating attendance is not uncommon at global forums. South Africa remains focused on facilitating productive engagement on economic recovery, technology cooperation, and inclusive growth. Government officials maintain that the event will proceed as planned and that even with altered participation, important outcomes are still expected from the summit.

Media How Data and Creative Alignment Are Redefining OOH Success

August 8, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

The use of data is playing an increasingly important role in how out-of-home (OOH) advertising campaigns are planned and executed. By tapping into detailed audience insights, advertisers are now making smarter, more informed decisions that lead to better targeted campaigns and stronger creative results. This shift is helping build greater trust and accountability in the media buying process, particularly when it comes to choosing where and how to advertise.

As a result of this push for better data and clear performance measurement, more industry players are joining the Outdoor Measurement Council (OMC). The OMC is key to creating a consistent way of measuring audiences across the OOH landscape, helping the industry move toward standardisation and reliability.

Despite this progress, the industry still faces long standing regulatory issues. Challenges like bylaw compliance, inconsistent rules across municipalities, and lax enforcement continue to cause problems.

The growth in billboard installations many of them unapproved or non-compliant has made things more complicated. These billboards often remain in place because local authorities don’t have the resources or legal backing to remove them. This creates unfair advantages, erodes trust, and hurts those who play by the rules, including legitimate media owners and municipalities that lose out on revenue. On the positive side, the industry has made major strides in using data to better plan and measure campaigns. But to keep up with advertiser expectations, further investment is needed. Tools like real-time data access and performance tracking are becoming essential to stay competitive in the current media landscape.

What’s also becoming clear is the growing need for closer collaboration between media owners, creative teams, and agencies. Clients today expect more than just visibility they want tailored, data informed campaigns that can show real results. Achieving that requires early stage coordination, particularly when it comes to creative elements and digital executions. Great OOH work doesn’t just happen it’s the result of understanding what makes the medium special: its scale, location relevance, and visual impact.

When agencies work hand in hand with trustworthy media owners, they’re in a stronger position to deliver campaigns that not only stand out but also meet strategic objectives. To continue growing, the industry must focus on key areas. That includes better collaboration among media owners, increased transparency in spending, consistent audience measurement, and ongoing education for creative teams to make the most of OOH’s potential.

There’s also a need to further embrace technologies like AI, programmatic media buying, dynamic creative tools, and real-time audience verification. OOH advertising has the power to be one of the most effective channels in the mix. The challenge now is ensuring that everyone involved media owners, agencies, creatives, and advertisers works together more closely and uses data smartly to prove the true value this medium brings to brands.

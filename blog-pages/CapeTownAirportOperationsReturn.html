Media Cape Town Airport Operations Gradually Return to Normal After Fire Incident

February 25, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Operations at Cape Town International Airport are gradually returning to normal following a fire that disrupted services and caused delays for passengers.

Airports Company South Africa confirmed that the fire occurred in a restricted area of the airport, prompting safety protocols and temporary operational disruptions. Firefighters responded swiftly and managed to contain the blaze before it could spread to critical infrastructure.

No injuries were reported, but several flights were delayed or rescheduled as authorities conducted safety checks and assessed damage. Passengers were advised to arrive early and monitor flight updates as systems were restored.

ACSA said contingency plans were activated to minimise disruption and ensure passenger safety. Technicians and safety teams worked through the night to inspect facilities and restore full functionality.

The cause of the fire is still under investigation. Preliminary assessments suggest it may have been linked to an electrical fault, though officials have not confirmed this.

Cape Town International Airport is one of Africa’s busiest airports and serves as a key gateway for tourism and business travel. Authorities said they are committed to maintaining high safety standards and preventing similar incidents in the future.

Media Seven Killed in Deadly Mass Shooting at Marikana Informal Settlement in Philippi

January 19, 2026 ● Tebogo Zongwana

Seven people have been shot and killed in a mass shooting at the Marikana informal settlement in Philippi on the Cape Flats, prompting an extensive police investigation and renewed concern over violent crime in the area.

Police confirmed that officers were called to the settlement following reports of multiple gunshots. On arrival, they discovered several victims with fatal gunshot wounds. Paramedics declared seven people dead at the scene.

The identities of the victims have not yet been released, pending notification of their families. It is also not yet clear whether the victims were targeted or caught in crossfire during the attack.

The motive for the shooting remains unknown and no arrests have been made. Police have not ruled out gang related violence, which continues to affect many communities on the Cape Flats. Investigators are exploring all possible angles, including whether the shooting may be linked to ongoing territorial disputes or retaliatory attacks.

Western Cape police said senior detectives and forensic teams have been deployed to the area. Crime scene experts are analysing ballistic evidence, while officers are reviewing witness statements and any available surveillance footage from the surrounding area.

Residents described a scene of fear and chaos as gunfire erupted, forcing families to flee their homes or take cover. Community members say violent crime has become a daily reality, particularly in informal settlements where policing resources are stretched.

Local leaders and civil society organisations have again called for increased police visibility, improved intelligence driven operations and stronger social interventions to address the root causes of violence.

Police have urged anyone with information that could assist in the investigation to come forward, stressing that cooperation from the community is critical to bringing those responsible to justice. Investigations into the shooting are continuing.

Media IDT Head Tebogo Malaka Resigns After Bribery Allegations

January 14, 2026 ● Uhone Kwinda

Tebogo Malaka has resigned as head of the publicly owned Independent Development Trust following allegations that he attempted to bribe a journalist, a controversy that has drawn renewed attention to governance at the state entity.

The allegations surfaced last year when reports emerged that Malaka had allegedly tried to influence media coverage by offering a bribe to a journalist. The claims sparked widespread criticism and prompted calls for accountability, given the IDT’s role as a government agency responsible for managing and implementing infrastructure and development projects on behalf of national and provincial departments.

The Independent Development Trust oversees projects in sectors such as education, health and social development, and manages billions of rand in public funds. As a result, concerns were raised that the allegations could undermine public trust in the institution and its ability to carry out its mandate effectively.

Malaka had previously denied wrongdoing, but pressure continued to mount from political parties, civil society organisations and media groups demanding clarity and decisive action. His resignation is seen as an attempt to protect the credibility of the organisation while investigations and scrutiny continue.

Government officials have said the IDT will ensure continuity of operations while steps are taken to stabilise leadership and restore confidence. The development comes amid broader efforts by authorities to strengthen governance, transparency and ethical conduct across state owned entities.

Tech News Pinterest Fires Engineers Over Internal Layoff Tracking Script

February 4, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Pinterest has dismissed two software engineers after they were found to have improperly accessed internal systems to determine which employees were affected during a recent round of job cuts.

The incident followed Pinterest’s announcement that it would reduce its workforce by about 15 percent, amounting to roughly 700 roles. In an internal message to staff, chief executive Bill Ready said the company was shifting further toward an AI-forward strategy, a move that would require structural changes across the business. While investors were informed of the scale of the layoffs, the company did not publicly disclose which teams or individuals were impacted.

According to a company spokesperson, the two engineers created custom scripts that accessed confidential internal data to identify the names and locations of employees whose accounts were being removed following the layoffs. The information was then shared more widely within the company.

Pinterest said the actions violated internal policies as well as the privacy of affected employees. The company stressed that safeguarding confidential employee information is a core requirement, particularly during sensitive processes such as retrenchments.

The scripts reportedly monitored internal communication tools used by staff, flagging when user accounts were deactivated or removed. This provided indirect but clear insight into who had lost their jobs. Monitoring internal platforms for disappearing colleagues has become a common, though informal, way for workers in the tech industry to track layoffs during periods of uncertainty. The identities of the two engineers have not been made public, and they have not commented on the dismissals.

The incident comes amid continued job losses across the global technology sector. Companies have cited slowing growth, cost cutting pressures and increased investment in artificial intelligence as reasons for reducing headcounts. In recent months, several major technology firms have announced large scale layoffs, affecting tens of thousands of workers worldwide.

Industry analysts say the Pinterest case highlights the tension between transparency and privacy during layoffs, as well as the heightened scrutiny on how employees use internal data and tools. It also underscores how sensitive workforce information has become in an era of frequent restructuring, where trust and data governance remain critical issues for technology companies.

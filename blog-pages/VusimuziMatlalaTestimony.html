Media Matlala Says He Fears for His Life and Labels Sibiya Criminal

November 3, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Businessman Vusimuzi ‘Cat’ Matlala told the Madlanga Commission of Inquiry he fears for his life, pointing the finger at Lieutenant General Shadrack Sibiya and suspended Minister of Police Senzo Mchunu as central figures in what he described as orchestrated pressure against him.

Matlala is a Pretoria-based businessman known for his involvement in several government and police-linked contracts through his company Medicare 24 Tshwane District. He was arrested earlier this year on charges of attempted murder, conspiracy to commit murder, and money laundering. The charges stem from an incident in which his former girlfriend was shot during an ambush near Sandton in 2023. His name has also appeared in investigations into questionable tender dealings and possible fraud involving state institutions.

During his testimony, Matlala claimed he was drawn into a relationship with Sibiya, where he provided the senior police officer with financial support in exchange for protection. He said that when police raided his home and businesses in December 2024 despite assurances from Sibiya that no action would follow, he felt betrayed and feared for his safety.

According to a witness at the commission, Matlala described Sibiya as a criminal himself and said he continued cooperating only because he feared for his life if Sibiya found out he had stopped. The witness added that Matlala had intended to file an affidavit in the High Court implicating Mchunu in criminal activity and claimed his police contract was cancelled after he refused to open a case against a team of officers.

The Madlanga Commission is investigating allegations of collusion and corruption involving politicians, senior police officials, intelligence operatives, and prosecutors.

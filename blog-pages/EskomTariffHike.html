Business Eskom Confirms 3% Tariff Hike Over Next Two Years

September 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Eskom has confirmed that South Africans will face a 3% electricity tariff increase over the next two years, following an agreement reached with the National Energy Regulator of South Africa (Nersa).

The state-owned power utility said the hike is part of a settlement designed to stabilise its financial position while addressing ongoing operational challenges. The adjustment comes as households and businesses continue to grapple with high living costs, sparking concerns about affordability.

Analysts argue that the tariff increase, while modest compared to previous hikes, may still place additional pressure on struggling consumers. “Every increase, no matter how small, eats into disposable income and raises costs for small businesses,” an energy economist explained.

Eskom maintains that the increase is necessary to sustain its recovery plan, reduce debt, and fund maintenance programmes aimed at improving grid stability. Despite this, load-shedding and supply constraints remain a reality for millions of South Africans.

The decision follows years of tension between Eskom and Nersa over pricing, with the regulator balancing the need for consumer protection against the utility’s financial sustainability.

Media Trump to Send Senior Delegation to G20 Summit in Johannesburg

September 18, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

US President Donald Trump will not attend the upcoming G20 Summit in Johannesburg in person but is expected to send a high-level delegation, the White House has confirmed.

The announcement comes after weeks of uncertainty, as Trump had previously signalled he might skip the gathering altogether. According to White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt, the US delegation will include senior cabinet officials and top economic advisers, ensuring “full American participation” in the summit.

“President Trump remains committed to advancing US interests on the global stage. He has instructed his team to engage robustly with counterparts at the G20 to discuss global economic stability, trade, and security issues,” Leavitt said in a press briefing.

South Africa, which is hosting the summit, had faced mounting criticism over the possibility that the US might not be represented at the highest level. Analysts warned that a no-show by Washington could undermine confidence in multilateral cooperation, particularly at a time when major economies are grappling with slowing growth and geopolitical tensions.

Johannesburg is preparing to welcome world leaders later this month, with security operations already underway across the city. Key agenda items are expected to include energy security, global debt relief, and coordinating responses to conflicts affecting global supply chains.

Trump’s decision to send senior officials rather than attend personally is seen by some as a compromise — ensuring that the US has a strong voice at the table while the president focuses on domestic priorities ahead of the 2026 midterm elections.

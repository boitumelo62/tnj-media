Sports LA28 Promise Affordable Olympic Tickets Ahead of Ticket Registration

January 14, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Organisers of the 2028 Los Angeles Olympic and Paralympic Games have reiterated their commitment to making the event accessible and affordable for as many people as possible as preparations continue ahead of ticket registration.

Officials confirmed that a lottery system for ticket purchases is opening, allowing fans to register for a chance to secure a time slot to buy tickets when general sales begin later this year. The system is aimed at managing demand fairly while giving more people an opportunity to attend the Games. LA28 chairman Casey Wasserman said organisers plan to release around one million tickets priced at just 28 dollars for Olympic and Paralympic events. In addition, approximately a third of all tickets are expected to be priced at 100 dollars or less, reinforcing the organisers’ focus on affordability.

The announcement was made during a ceremonial lighting of the Olympic cauldron at the historic Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, where officials also revealed that more than 150 000 volunteers have already signed up to support the Games.

Organisers say the pricing strategy is a deliberate effort to avoid excluding fans and to ensure the Olympics remain a shared global experience, particularly at a time when the cost of attending major sporting events has come under increasing scrutiny.

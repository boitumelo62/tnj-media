Media Over Eight Thousand Illegal Entry Attempts Recorded in the Second Quarter of 2025

December 8, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

New data from the Border Management Authority shows that more than eight thousand people attempted to enter South Africa illegally during the second quarter of this year. The figures reflect ongoing pressure at several border points and highlight the continued demand for tighter security as the festive season approaches.

Officials say the majority of attempts involved individuals trying to cross through unofficial routes in remote areas. Others involved people who arrived at recognised entry points but tried to bypass immigration procedures or presented false documents. The authority notes that the scale of the illegal movement remains significant despite increased patrols and surveillance technology in the past year.

With travel and tourism expected to rise sharply during December and early January, the authority is preparing for increased demand at airports and land ports. More officers are expected to be deployed to manage long queues and to help verify documents more efficiently.

According to the authority, the aim is to strike a balance between legitimate travel and firm border control. The agency emphasised that while South Africa continues to welcome visitors, it must also ensure that entry requirements are respected and that national security risks are kept under control.

Officials say they will continue to tighten operations and monitor the border more closely over the coming weeks as the holiday season places extra strain on the system.

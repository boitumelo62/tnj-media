Lifestyle Anthony Hamilton Honors South African Women

August 9, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Anthony Hamilton was all smiles and heart during a press conference held at Primedia Place in Sandton on August 7, 2025. Sitting alongside South African music giants Zonke, Vusi Nova, Sjava, and US R&B singer Joe Thomas, Hamilton spoke with genuine excitement about his upcoming performance in South Africa and he made it clear this show is more than just another gig.

For him, it’s all about love, emotion, and connecting with the people who support him, especially the women. With Women’s Day just around the corner, the concert set for August 9 at the iconic DP World Wanderers Stadium is being billed as a celebration of women through music. And Hamilton made it clear he understands the assignment.

“I did my research. I know what Mzansi wants to hear,” he said, flashing a smile that lit up the room. He explained that fans especially women are constantly reaching out to him with requests, and he listens. “They’re in my inbox! They tell me what songs to sing,” he said. “This show is about love. It’s about understanding what women want to hear.”

It’s that kind of thoughtfulness and attention to his audience that’s kept Hamilton at the top of his game for years. Known for his raw, soulful voice and the emotional depth of his performances, he promised nothing short of a memorable night. “I’m gonna show up, show out, and tear my shoes up,” he joked, earning laughter and applause from everyone present. But even behind the humour, you could sense how seriously he takes this moment.

“I care about the people,” he continued. “I want people to feel connected to who I am, not just as an artist, but also as a father and a man. I don’t want to be that guy who comes to Mzansi just to perform and leave. I want to connect.”

The upcoming show is set to feature a stellar line up. Zonke, Sjava, and Vusi Nova all artists celebrated for their soulful sound and emotional honesty will be taking the stage alongside Hamilton and Joe Thomas. Nova hinted that fans can expect a mix of timeless favourites and fresh new tracks: “We’ll be doing some old classics and new music for the audience. There’s something for everyone.”

There’s no doubt the night will be filled with powerful moments. Beyond just a concert, it’s shaping up to be a tribute a celebration of South African women, their stories, and the music that speaks to their hearts. With tickets flying fast and expectations high, it’s clear fans are eager to be part of what promises to be a deeply moving night of soul, connection, and celebration.

Media Madlanga Commission Reveals How Senior Officials Sabotaged Justice System

November 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Explosive testimony before the Madlanga Commission has revealed that senior government officials deliberately dismantled specialised police units and interfered with key investigations to shield criminal networks and political allies.

According to witnesses who testified under oath, the units were dissolved under the pretext of restructuring, but the real motive was to obstruct investigations into organised crime and political assassinations. The commission heard that senior officials redirected resources, transferred critical investigators, and even tampered with evidence in major cases.

Sources close to the inquiry say some of the sabotage is linked to the unsolved murders of prominent figures, including rapper AKA and several political leaders in KwaZulu-Natal and Gauteng. Testimony also pointed to internal power struggles within law enforcement agencies that undermined public trust and emboldened criminal syndicates.

Justice Madlanga warned that the findings expose a deep rot in state institutions and urged Parliament to act decisively to restore accountability and integrity within the justice system. The hearings are expected to continue into November, with more whistleblowers expected to testify.

Business Stefanutti and Eskom conclude five-year dispute over Kusile project

November 25, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Construction group Stefanutti has reached the end of a long running five year dispute with Eskom over work done at the Kusile power station. The matter has now been settled, clearing the way for the company to receive about five hundred and eighty million rand after both parties agreed on outstanding claims linked to the project.

The dispute began during one of the most difficult periods in the construction sector, with delays, cost pressures, and operational challenges affecting progress at Kusile. Stefanutti argued that it had carried out work that had not been fully recognised or compensated. Eskom disputed several of the claims, which resulted in a lengthy and technical back and forth process involving engineers, auditors, and legal teams.

The settlement brings certainty to a relationship that has been strained for years. It also allows Stefanutti to stabilise its financial outlook after navigating significant pressure linked to the slow pace of large infrastructure programmes in the country. Executives within the company said the agreement allows them to close a difficult chapter and refocus on current projects and future opportunities.

For Eskom, the resolution removes one more complication from its complex build programme at Kusile. The station has faced repeated setbacks over the years, and disputes with contractors have added delays and financial strain to a project already under public scrutiny.

Industry analysts say the agreement is an important reminder of the need for clearer contracts and stronger oversight on major projects, especially within the energy sector where delays and disputes often affect national power supply planning.

The settlement marks the final step in a prolonged disagreement and brings both sides back to more stable ground as operations at Kusile continue.

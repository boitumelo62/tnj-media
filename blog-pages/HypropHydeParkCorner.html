Business Hyprop pulls plug on Hyde Park Corner sale and leaseback deal

January 16, 2026 ● Uhone Kwinda

Hyprop Investments has confirmed that its proposed disposal of a half share in the Hyde Park Corner shopping centre will not proceed after the buyer failed to meet key contractual requirements.

The property group announced in July 2025 that it had agreed to a sale and leaseback transaction involving 50% of Hyde Park Corner for R805 million. The deal included an option for the buyer, a subsidiary of Millennium Equity Partners, to acquire the remaining stake within two years. Like many large property transactions, the agreement was dependent on several conditions being met before it could be finalised.

Although the Competition Commission granted its approval, Hyprop said the transaction has now been cancelled because the purchaser did not fulfil certain conditions precedent within the agreed timeframe. As a result, both the initial sale and the future option to buy the balance of the asset have been terminated.

Hyprop noted that the collapse of the deal has not derailed the operational recovery of Hyde Park Corner. The centre has shown steady improvement, supported by lower vacancy levels and a stronger tenant mix. Recent additions include a Checkers Fresh X store, which opened in August 2025 and has delivered encouraging trading results, as well as the newly launched Al Capone outlet. The group has also begun work on an integrated solar photovoltaic and battery storage project, which is expected to boost net operating income once completed.

Hyde Park Corner, which opened in 1969, remains one of Hyprop’s long standing assets. The mall offers approximately 38,750 square metres of retail space and is located between Rosebank and Sandton in one of Johannesburg’s most affluent districts. It serves a high end customer base and is anchored by well known brands and attractions such as Woolworths, Nu Metro, Exclusive Books, Jo Malone, Olives & Plates, and Le Parc by Tasha’s.

Media Ivory Park police manhunt underway

December 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Gauteng police have begun an urgent manhunt for two suspects believed to be responsible for the fatal shooting of two police officers in Ivory Park, Johannesburg. The attack took place on Friday, leaving the province’s law enforcement community shocked and demanding swift action.

According to preliminary reports, the officers were on duty in the area when they were ambushed and shot at close range. Both officials died at the scene before emergency personnel could intervene, prompting the immediate activation of specialised investigative units.

Police have since intensified operations in and around Ivory Park, with detectives gathering surveillance footage, interviewing witnesses and analysing ballistic evidence to trace the suspects’ movements. Roadblocks and targeted patrols have also been deployed in an effort to track the gunmen before they leave the area or flee the province.

Authorities have appealed to community members for any information that could help lead to arrests. Gauteng residents have been urged to report suspicious behaviour or individuals who may be linked to the shooting, with police stressing that all information will be treated confidentially.

The killing of on duty officers has drawn strong condemnation from policing structures and community leaders, who have called the incident a direct attack on the justice system. The SAPS has assured the public that every available resource will be used to bring the suspects to account and restore safety to the community.

Investigations remain ongoing and police have promised updates as progress is made in the hunt for the pair believed to be behind the deadly attack.

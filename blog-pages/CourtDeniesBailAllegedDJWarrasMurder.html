Media Court Denies Bail to Alleged DJ Warras Murder Planner

January 26, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The man accused of orchestrating the killing of Johannesburg DJ Warrick Stock, popularly known as DJ Warras, will remain behind bars after the Johannesburg Magistrates Court denied his bail application.

Judgment was delivered on Monday morning, with the court finding that the accused had failed to show exceptional circumstances that would justify his release. Victor Majola, a 44 year old Soweto taxi boss, was arrested last year in connection with the high profile murder and remains in custody.

DJ Warras was shot dead in the Johannesburg CBD on 16 December. Investigators believe the killing was a planned hit linked to work carried out by Stock’s security company, which was reportedly involved in activities related to hijacked buildings in the city centre.

In her ruling, Magistrate Annalise Tlhapi said the evidence presented against Majola was compelling. She added that granting bail could undermine the ongoing investigation and potentially interfere with witnesses or evidence.

The court concluded that releasing the accused would not be in the interests of justice and that the seriousness of the charges warranted his continued detention. The case has drawn widespread attention, with growing focus on the alleged mastermind behind the killing rather than only the shooter.

The matter will return to court as investigations continue and prosecutors prepare for the next phase of the case.

In other news

Eskom has confirmed that it is assisting police with an investigation into a shooting at the Kriel Power Station that claimed the life of one of its employees. The incident occurred over the weekend and involved a married couple who both work for the power utility.

According to Eskom, the husband was killed in the attack, while his wife survived and is currently recovering in hospital. The utility said it is providing support to the affected family as well as colleagues at the power station during what it described as a difficult time.

Eskom spokesperson Daphne Mokwena said the company would continue to share updates as information becomes available. She also urged caution following claims circulating on social media suggesting the shooting was a targeted hit.

Mokwena said such claims were premature and inappropriate, calling on the public and media to avoid speculation and allow law enforcement space to complete its investigation.

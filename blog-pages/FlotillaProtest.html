Media South African Activists Urge End to Trade with Israel After Flotilla Incident

October 6, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

A group of South African activists participating in the Global Sumud Flotilla have called on the government to immediately suspend trade relations with Israel following an incident that led to the detention of five citizens over the weekend.

The flotilla, part of an international solidarity mission, sought to raise awareness about the humanitarian situation in Gaza and to advocate for global action against what activists describe as ongoing violations of international law by Israel. The vessel was reportedly intercepted in international waters, resulting in the temporary detention of participants by Israeli authorities.

Speaking upon their release, representatives from the South African contingent urged Pretoria to take a firmer stance by halting all economic and diplomatic engagement with Israel until the blockade of Gaza is lifted. They argued that continued trade undermines South Africa’s historic commitment to human rights and justice.

Civil society organizations, including the South African Boycott, Divestment, and Sanctions Coalition, have echoed these demands, calling for a national review of all bilateral agreements with Israel.

Meanwhile, the Department of International Relations and Cooperation confirmed that it is in communication with Israeli authorities to ensure the safe return of all detained citizens. The department added that it remains committed to promoting peaceful dialogue and a two-state solution in the Middle East.

The incident has reignited debate within South Africa’s foreign policy circles, where calls for stronger solidarity with Palestine continue to grow amid rising global tensions over the conflict.

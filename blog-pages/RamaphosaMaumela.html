Media President Ramaphosa Denies Link to Fraud Suspect

October 14, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

President Cyril Ramaphosa has firmly rejected claims linking him to alleged tender-fraud mastermind Hangwani Maumela, following viral footage that appeared to show the president outside Maumela’s residence.

The Presidency issued a statement dismissing the allegations as “false and malicious,” asserting that the video circulating on social media had been manipulated to mislead the public. According to the statement, President Ramaphosa has never visited Maumela’s home nor had any personal or business dealings with him.

Hangwani Maumela, a businessman facing multiple fraud and corruption charges, has been implicated in several irregular tender processes involving government departments. His name has surfaced in ongoing investigations by the Special Investigating Unit (SIU) concerning public procurement during the pandemic.

Presidential spokesperson Vincent Magwenya confirmed that legal avenues are being explored to identify the source of the edited footage and hold those responsible accountable for spreading disinformation.

Opposition parties have called for a transparent review of the president’s alleged connections to individuals under investigation, while ANC insiders say the claims appear to be part of a coordinated smear campaign ahead of the party’s upcoming conference.

Authorities are continuing to trace the origin of the footage as part of a broader probe into politically motivated misinformation campaigns targeting public officials.

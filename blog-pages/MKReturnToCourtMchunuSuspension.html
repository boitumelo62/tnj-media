Media MK Party Returns To Court Over Mchunu Suspension Challenge

November 28, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The MK party has made its way back to the Pretoria High Court as it continues its challenge against President Cyril Ramaphosa’s decision to suspend Police Minister Senzo Mchunu. The legal battle has been ongoing, rooted in claims that the suspension was improperly executed and lacks constitutional grounding.

At the centre of the dispute is the argument that the suspension was politically motivated and not based on due process. The MK party insists that Mchunu was removed without fair consultation or valid justification. The presidency, however, maintains that the decision was necessary to safeguard governance and to allow investigations to proceed without interference. The court process is expected to analyse both the procedural correctness of the suspension and the reasoning behind it. Political observers note that the case could carry significant implications for executive accountability and the balance of power between the presidency and cabinet structures.

As proceedings continue the outcome is poised to shape political discourse in the coming weeks. Both parties remain firm in their positions and the court will now determine whether the suspension stands on lawful ground or whether it will be overturned.

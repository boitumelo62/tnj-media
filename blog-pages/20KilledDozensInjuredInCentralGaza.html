Media 20 Killed, Dozens Injured After Aid Convoy Overturns in Central Gaza

August 8, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

At least 20 people have died and dozens more were injured after four aid trucks overturned in central Gaza on Wednesday, according to reports from Al Jazeera and local health authorities.

The vehicles, which were part of a humanitarian convoy delivering food and medical supplies to communities affected by ongoing conflict, lost control along a severely damaged stretch of road near Deir al-Balah. Emergency services responded to the scene within minutes, but many victims were reportedly crushed beneath the overturned trucks.

The cause of the crash is still under investigation, but preliminary reports suggest poor road conditions, overcrowding, and excessive speeds may have contributed to the tragedy. Eyewitnesses described a chaotic scene as civilians rushed toward the trucks in an attempt to collect supplies before they were properly offloaded.

"This is a heartbreaking incident,” said a spokesperson from Gaza’s Health Ministry. “These were people already in desperate need. They had gathered in hope and instead were met with more loss."

At least 40 people have been hospitalized, with several in critical condition. Rescue workers are continuing to clear the site and recover supplies. The incident has triggered fresh calls for improved safety protocols and better coordination between aid agencies and local authorities.

The humanitarian situation in Gaza remains dire, with widespread food insecurity and collapsing infrastructure following months of violence and blockade. International aid organizations have expressed condolences and renewed urgency for safe and sustained humanitarian access to the region.

Further details are expected to emerge as investigations continue and identities of the deceased are confirmed.

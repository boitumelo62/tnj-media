Media Julius Malema Convicted in Firearms Discharge Case

October 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

EFF leader Julius Malema has been found guilty on charges arising from the discharge of a firearm during a political rally in 2018. The ruling was handed down in the East London Regional Court on Wednesday, bringing to a close a long-running case that has drawn widespread public attention.

The matter dates back to a rally in Mdantsane, where Malema was seen in video footage appearing to fire a gun into the air while addressing supporters. He and his co-accused, security aide Adriaan Snyman, were subsequently charged under the Firearms Control Act for unlawful possession of a firearm and ammunition, as well as for discharging a firearm in a public space.

During the trial, prosecutors argued that Malema’s actions endangered those present and undermined public safety. The defence maintained that the evidence was unreliable and that Malema had no case to answer. Snyman, who was accused of providing the firearm, was acquitted after the court found the state had not proven his involvement beyond a reasonable doubt.

Magistrate Twanet Olivier ruled that the state had presented sufficient evidence to secure a conviction against Malema, rejecting the defence’s attempts to cast doubt on the footage and testimony. Sentencing proceedings are expected to follow, with Malema’s legal team likely to consider an appeal.

The conviction is a significant moment for the EFF leader, who has faced numerous controversies since founding the party in 2013. It also places renewed scrutiny on political accountability and the handling of firearms at public gatherings.

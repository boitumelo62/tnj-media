Media The Next Chapter of South Africa’s Media Evolution

November 6, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Popular Blogs:

The Media Agencies Setting New Standards

Business

The Future of Marketing Leadership: From Function to Force

For more than a decade, South Africa’s media industry has been fuelled by a relentless focus on performance. Every click, view, and conversion has been measured, optimised, and reported. This approach has turned media from something guided by instinct and creativity into a data driven science. It has brought real benefits clear returns, efficiency, and accountability. But it has also revealed gaps. Insights often sit in silos, reports don’t always connect, and attribution can feel inconsistent. As a result, we’ve lost sight of the bigger picture: the full, connected story of the consumer.

Now, with technology, data, and audience behaviour changing faster than ever, the industry stands at a turning point. The question is no longer just how to perform better, but how to think smarter how to move beyond performance into a world of connected intelligence. Globally, the media landscape is transforming. Television, digital platforms, retail media, and e-commerce are no longer separate they’re merging into one seamless ecosystem. Connected IDs, which recognise audiences across devices and transactions, are reshaping how media is planned, activated, and measured.

Picture someone watching a cooking show on a connected TV, ordering ingredients through an app, and then seeing a grocery delivery ad later on social media. Connected media links these moments together, showing not only which channels work, but how people truly behave and make decisions. It turns scattered data into meaningful insights and everyday interactions into lasting impact.

We’re starting to see this evolution in South Africa too. Checkers Sixty60’s blend of retail media, digital advertising, and mobile commerce shows how brands can unite awareness and conversion in one connected plan. Takealot’s commerce led campaigns also highlight the strength of understanding behaviour across the full consumer journey. These examples prove that even in markets where data is fragmented, connected ecosystems can thrive driven by intelligence, not just performance.

South Africa’s media environment is unique. Fragmented data sets, walled gardens, and uneven digital access create challenges for identity based planning. Yet, the country’s mobile first habits, strong retail loyalty programs, and growing connected TV market offer powerful advantages. The real opportunity lies in collaboration. Media owners, brands, agencies, and technology partners must come together to build a privacy first identity framework one that connects audiences while maintaining trust. If we achieve this, we can create a planning model where decisions are guided by deep insight rather than surface level metrics.

Connected media is ultimately about more than just measurement. It’s about understanding people their intent, emotions, and behaviour. It’s about connecting not only data points but also purposes, ensuring that every interaction has meaning.For media leaders and strategists, this marks a major shift. Planning is no longer just about chasing the lowest CPM or perfecting channel buys. It’s about designing connected ecosystems that bring together brand, content, and commerce in a unified way. This new era demands a balance of analytical skill and creative thinking a mindset that can see patterns, anticipate needs, and shape experiences that resonate.

The future will belong to those who can build bridges between data and creativity, between media and commerce, and between technology and human understanding. The goal isn’t just to reach people but to truly connect with them. Connected intelligence is not the end point it’s the foundation of a smarter, more integrated, and more human approach to media. As we move beyond performance, we finally have the chance to answer the question that has always defined our work not just how many people we reach, but who we reach, how we reach them, and why it truly matters.

Lifestyle Eastern Cape’s Qhawekazi Mazaleni Takes the Miss South Africa Crown

October 26, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

After months of anticipation and several changes within the organisation, Miss South Africa has finally crowned its new queen, Qhawekazi Mazaleni. The crowning took place on Saturday, 25 October, at the SunBet Arena in Pretoria, where Mia le Roux passed on the title to her successor. Luyanda Zuma was announced as the first princess, while Karabo Mareka earned the title of second princess.

Qhawekazi Mazaleni, who hails from the Eastern Cape, holds a degree in social sciences and is deeply passionate about youth empowerment and community development. Long before winning the Miss South Africa crown, she was already known for her work in education and mentorship programmes for young women. Mazaleni shared that her mission as Miss South Africa is to use her platform to promote equal access to education and uplift communities across the country.

As part of her victory, Mazaleni will receive a prize package valued at over R3 million, which includes a R1 million cash prize, a Mercedes-Benz GLC Coupé, and a luxurious three bedroom penthouse apartment to enjoy during her reign.

However, she will not be representing South Africa at the upcoming 74th Miss Universe competition next month. The Miss South Africa Organisation announced earlier in October that the 2025 titleholder would not take part in international pageants this year due to time constraints and the organisation’s ongoing restructuring process. According to creative director Sean Lane, the decision allows the organisation to realign its focus and operations for future success.

Instead, South Africa will be represented at Miss Universe by Mellisa Nayimuli, who was officially named Miss Universe South Africa. This follows African Beauty International’s recent appointment of former Miss South Africa Bokang Montjane Tshabalala as the new national director for Miss Universe South Africa.

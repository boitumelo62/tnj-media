Media Parliament Establishes New Oversight Committee for the Presidency

December 4, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Parliament has approved new rules that create a dedicated committee to oversee the Presidency, marking the first time such a structure has been formalised in South Africa’s democratic era. The move is seen as a significant shift in the balance of oversight and accountability at the highest level of government.

The new committee will monitor the work of the Presidency, evaluate the implementation of key programmes and examine spending and performance reports. Lawmakers say the structure is needed to strengthen transparency and ensure that the Presidency is held to the same level of scrutiny as other departments.

Supporters of the reform argue that the Presidency has grown in influence over the years, with expanded responsibilities that now require more consistent parliamentary attention. They say the committee will help prevent lapses in governance and provide clearer channels for MPs to raise concerns.

Critics, however, warn that the committee must be properly resourced and must operate independently, without political interference or factional influence. They caution that the effectiveness of the committee will depend on whether it is allowed to conduct its work fully and openly.

The new oversight body is expected to begin operating once Parliament finalises additional procedural guidelines in the coming weeks.

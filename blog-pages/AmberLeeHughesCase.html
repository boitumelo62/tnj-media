Media NPA Pushes for Maximum Sentence in Amber Lee Hughes Case

August 29, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The National Prosecuting Authority (NPA) has confirmed it will seek the maximum sentence for Amber Lee Hughes, who was found guilty of the rape and murder of four-year-old Nada-Jane Challita.

Hughes, the ex-girlfriend of the child’s father, was convicted after a trial that revealed shocking details of the crime. Forensic evidence and witness testimony placed her at the scene, with the court finding her guilty on all charges.

Speaking after the verdict, NPA spokesperson Sipho Nkosi said: “This was a brutal and senseless crime against a vulnerable child. The prosecution will argue for the maximum sentence because anything less would not reflect the gravity of what was done.”

The family of the victim welcomed the decision. In a brief statement, the child’s grandfather said: “We cannot bring Nada-Jane back, but we hope the sentence will send a message that children in this country must be protected at all costs.”

Child rights organisations have also weighed in, urging the courts to take a firm stance. Nomvula Radebe, from the Children’s Justice Network, commented: “Too often, crimes against children do not result in harsh enough punishment. This case presents an opportunity to affirm that justice will be uncompromising when it comes to protecting minors.”

The sentencing hearing is expected to take place in the coming weeks and is likely to attract significant public and media attention.

August 14, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Retail Media Is Reshaping Ecommerce in South Africa

Location data has evolved from simply counting how many people walk past a store to becoming a powerful strategic tool for marketers and brand strategists. It now offers real-time insights into consumer behaviour, movement, and intent helping brands understand not just where people go, but why and how often. This shift, driven by AI and machine learning, allows marketers to predict future behaviours and make smarter, more effective decisions.

At Rook Digital, we use location intelligence to create high-intent audience segments based on real world behaviours, like frequent visits to gyms or quick-service restaurants. We then activate these insights across digital platforms for precise, relevant targeting. By combining anonymised location signals with machine learning, we identify patterns such as dwell time, movement paths, and uplift in visitation building a clearer picture of consumer journeys.

Location data is especially valuable in high-traffic spaces like malls and airports, where brands can use real time insights to optimise ad placements and tailor messaging. It also enhances campaign measurement by linking online exposure to offline results, providing a full picture of impact from awareness to conversion. A good example is the AKTV Summer Resorts campaign, where we geo targeted media placements based on historical travel patterns and tailored messaging by region. This led to a 185% increase in conversions and a significant revenue uplift.

Looking ahead, location data will be central to predictive marketing. Brands that learn to harness its full potential won’t just react to trends they’ll anticipate them. I’ll be unpacking more of this at the MMA South Africa CMO Summit on 10 September. Hope to see you there.

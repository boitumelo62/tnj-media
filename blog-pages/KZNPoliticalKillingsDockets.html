Media KZN Political Killings Task Team Hands 121 Dockets to Inquiry

September 3, 2025 ● TNJ Media Desk

The Madlanga Commission of Inquiry into political violence in KwaZulu-Natal is set to receive 121 case dockets from the province’s dedicated task team investigating political killings. The handover represents a significant step in efforts to address the long-standing scourge of politically motivated assassinations in the province.

KwaZulu-Natal has grappled with persistent violence linked to political rivalry and power struggles, with many cases dragging on without resolution. Investigators say the files contain crucial evidence and testimonies that could help establish accountability and expose networks behind the killings.

Civil society groups have welcomed the development, urging the commission to ensure transparency and prioritize justice for the families of victims. Analysts believe the commission’s work could have far-reaching implications for political stability in South Africa, particularly as the country moves closer to another election cycle.

Business Retail Media Is Reshaping Ecommerce in South Africa

August 11, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Retail media is becoming a serious force in the advertising world, especially in South Africa, where it's beginning to take off in a big way. Big players like Takealot, Checkers Sixty60, Makro, and Woolworths are adopting this approach not just to grow their revenues, but also to connect more meaningfully with shoppers in the digital space.

If you’re in marketing, this is something worth paying close attention to. Retail media opens up a new avenue for performance marketing one that’s highly targeted, driven by data, and full of potential.

So what exactly is retail media? At its core, it’s about placing ads on a retailer’s own platforms like their websites, apps, or even in-store digital screens to reach people who are actively shopping or about to make a purchase. It’s not a new idea; we've seen versions of it for years through print catalogues and in store displays. But what makes it powerful now is the digital layer: the ability to target precisely, measure accurately, and act immediately.

As more South Africans shop online, this kind of advertising is becoming more effective. With the help of data and artificial intelligence, retailers and brands can now place personalised ads in front of customers at the exact moment they’re considering buying something. Think of it like programmatic ads on Facebook or Google, but right inside a shopping app or site where people are already primed to make a purchase.

The growth in ecommerce is a major driver here. In 2023, online retail in South Africa jumped 29% to hit R71 billion, and that momentum is only increasing. By 2026, it's expected that online shopping will make up about 10% of total retail sales in the country. That’s a big shift and it presents a huge opportunity for brands.

Retailers have realised they can do more than just sell products directly. They can also become advertising platforms by allowing brands to run campaigns targeting their customer base. Companies like Massmart (with its Rainmaker Media platform) and Takealot (with Takealot Ads) are already building systems that help brands reach consumers through targeted placements, AI enhanced product suggestions, and smarter pricing strategies. You might be wondering, if display ads, search ads, and social media already work, why add retail media into the mix? The simple answer is: timing and intent. When someone is browsing a retailer’s app or site, they’re much closer to making a purchase than someone just scrolling through Instagram. That means higher conversion potential.

Another benefit is access to the retailer’s first party data. This lets brands create ads that speak more directly to customers using their shopping habits, preferences, and behaviours to deliver messages that feel more relevant and personal.

Retail media also makes it easy to track performance. You can follow a shopper’s journey from seeing your ad to completing a purchase, which gives you more clarity about what’s working and what’s not. And with AI tools built into these platforms, you can constantly test, tweak, and improve your campaigns for better results.

It’s also a channel that fits well into your wider digital strategy. The messaging can stay consistent across different touchpoints, reinforcing your brand and helping you maintain a unified presence in front of your audience. Looking ahead, AI will play a bigger and bigger role in how brands use retail media. With the right approach, it can drive engagement, improve targeting, and make campaign management more efficient. For example, brands can merge their own customer data with what retailers already know to fine tune their ad targeting. AI can automate the process of testing different ad versions to find what works best. It can personalise product suggestions based on shopping behaviour. And it can even enhance the shopping experience through chatbots that guide customers to the right products.

Ultimately, retail media is more than just another box to tick in your marketing plan. For brands that approach it smartly combining data, creativity, and close collaboration with retailers it can become a high performing channel that drives real sales and builds deeper customer relationships. As South Africa’s ecommerce scene continues to expand, retail media is set to become a core part of how brands market and grow.

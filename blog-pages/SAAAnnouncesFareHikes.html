Media SAA Announces Fare Hikes as Global Jet Fuel Prices Surge

March 13, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South African Airways has confirmed that passengers will soon face higher ticket prices after the airline announced fare increases linked to rising global jet fuel costs. The move comes as airlines across the world grapple with a sharp increase in fuel prices, driven largely by instability in the Middle East and disruptions in global energy markets.

Fuel remains one of the largest operational expenses for airlines. Industry analysts estimate that it can account for more than a third of an airline’s total operating costs. When fuel prices rise significantly, airlines often have little choice but to adjust fares in order to maintain financial stability.

SAA officials said the increases will be introduced gradually across various routes, both domestic and international. While the airline did not specify exact price changes for every route, passengers can expect noticeable adjustments depending on distance and demand.

The airline stressed that the decision was not taken lightly, noting that the aviation sector is still recovering from several years of financial strain. Travel disruptions during the pandemic and ongoing global economic uncertainty have already placed pressure on airlines, and rising fuel costs have added another layer of difficulty.

Aviation experts say the latest increase reflects a wider global trend. Airlines worldwide have been warning that geopolitical tensions and energy supply concerns could push fuel prices higher. As a result, ticket prices across the industry are likely to rise.

For South African travellers, the timing may be particularly challenging. Many households are already facing increased living costs, including higher fuel prices and inflation affecting everyday goods. Travel demand, however, remains relatively strong as both business and leisure travel continue to recover.

SAA says it will attempt to manage the increases carefully to avoid discouraging travel, while still protecting the airline’s financial position. The carrier has also indicated that it will continue exploring ways to improve efficiency and reduce operational costs where possible.

Passengers planning travel in the coming months are being encouraged to book early, as prices may fluctuate further depending on global fuel markets and currency movements.

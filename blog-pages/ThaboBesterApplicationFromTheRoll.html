Media Pretoria High Court Strikes Thabo Bester’s Urgent Application from the Roll

February 11, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Pretoria High Court has struck convicted rapist and murderer Thabo Bester’s urgent application from the roll, after he challenged his transfer to Ebongweni Prison in KwaZulu Natal.

Bester was moved from a Gauteng facility following his dramatic escape from Mangaung Correctional Centre in 2022 and subsequent recapture in Tanzania in 2023. In his court papers, he argued that the transfer was unlawful and that it infringed on his rights, including access to legal representation and family visits. His legal team asked the court to treat the matter as urgent, citing the impact of his relocation on his legal preparations and personal circumstances.

However, the court ruled that the matter did not meet the legal threshold for urgency and should instead proceed through normal court processes. As a result, Bester will remain at Ebongweni Prison while any further legal challenges are heard through standard litigation channels.

The Department of Correctional Services has previously defended the transfer, citing security and operational concerns. Officials have described Bester as a high-risk inmate, noting the intense public interest in his case and the need to ensure both inmate and public safety.

Legal analysts say the ruling underscores the courts’ reluctance to grant urgent relief unless there is clear and immediate harm. They add that Bester still has the option to pursue the matter through a standard review or application process, although this could take months to resolve.

Bester is serving a life sentence for murder and multiple rape convictions. His escape and recapture triggered widespread public outrage and led to investigations into prison security failures and possible corruption within the correctional system.

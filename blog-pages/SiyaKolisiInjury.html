Sports Injury Rules Siya Kolisi Out of Rugby Championship Clash Against Australia

August 19, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Springbok captain Siya Kolisi has been ruled out of Saturday's Rugby Championship clash against Australia after suffering a knee injury over the weekend.

The setback comes as a major blow to South Africa's campaign in the tournament, with Kolisi playing a central role in the team's leadership and defensive structure. Medical staff confirmed the injury after assessments in Cape Town, ruling the skipper unavailable for selection.

Kolisi's absence means the Springboks will have to reshuffle their pack ahead of the crucial match. Coach Rassie Erasmus is expected to name a replacement later this week, with senior players likely to step up in his absence.

The injury has raised concerns about Kolisi's availability for the remainder of the Rugby Championship as South Africa pushes to maintain momentum in the competition. The captain, who has been a symbol of resilience and inspiration for the national team, will undergo further evaluation to determine the extent of his recovery period.

Fans expressed disappointment at the news but rallied behind the Springbok leader on social media, sending messages of support and encouragement as the team prepares for one of the toughest fixtures on their calendar.

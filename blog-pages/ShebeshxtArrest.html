Media Shebeshxt Arrested for Attempted Murder After Polokwane Shooting

November 13, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Popular Limpopo musician and rapper Shebeshxt has been arrested on two counts of attempted murder following a shooting incident that took place in Polokwane late last month. The controversial artist, whose real name has not been officially released by authorities, appeared briefly in court this week after being taken into custody by the South African Police Service.

According to police sources, the charges stem from an altercation that reportedly turned violent outside a local entertainment venue in the Flora Park area. Witnesses claim an argument broke out between Shebeshxt and two men in the early hours of the morning, leading to gunfire that left both victims seriously injured. They were rushed to a nearby hospital and are believed to be recovering under police protection.

Limpopo police spokesperson Brigadier Hlulani Mashaba confirmed that a case of attempted murder had been opened and that ballistic evidence linked to a firearm allegedly owned by the musician was being examined. “The suspect was arrested after a thorough investigation by the provincial task team. He faces two counts of attempted murder and will remain in custody pending a formal bail hearing,” Mashaba said.

This arrest comes just months after Shebeshxt made headlines for several public altercations and a string of controversial social media posts that raised concerns about his behaviour. Despite his popularity in the Limpopo music scene, his career has been repeatedly marred by allegations of violence and misconduct.

Fans have expressed mixed reactions online, some defending the musician and calling for due process, while others have voiced disappointment over what they describe as a “pattern of reckless behaviour.” Legal experts say the attempted murder charges are serious and could carry a lengthy prison sentence if convicted.

The court has ordered further investigation into the events surrounding the shooting, including the possible role of alcohol and the legality of the firearm involved. Shebeshxt is expected to appear in the Polokwane Magistrate’s Court later this week for a formal bail application. Meanwhile, police have urged witnesses who were present during the shooting to come forward to assist with the ongoing investigation.

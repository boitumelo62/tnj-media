Media Pressure Mounts on McKenzie as Offensive Posts and Allies’ Controversies Surface

August 11, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Calls for the removal of Minister Gayton McKenzie are growing louder after a series of old racist social media posts resurfaced, sparking outrage among civil society groups and opposition parties. The posts, allegedly dating back more than a decade, have reignited debates over accountability and the conduct of public officials, especially those holding high office.

McKenzie, leader of the Patriotic Alliance, has faced mounting criticism over the remarks, which critics say are incompatible with the principles of equality and non-discrimination. “It doesn’t matter how old these comments are they reveal a mindset that has no place in leadership,” said Thandi Mokoena, spokesperson for the advocacy group Equal Voices SA. The controversy has also cast a spotlight on the internal dynamics of the Patriotic Alliance, particularly after its Deputy President, Kenny Kunene, was recently suspended. Kunene is under fire for offensive tweets that resurfaced online, adding to the party’s public relations woes. Analysts note that the close political alignment between McKenzie and Kunene has intensified scrutiny on both men.

“This is no longer about isolated incidents,” political analyst Mxolisi Ntuli told The Citizen. “We are seeing a pattern of conduct that raises serious questions about the culture of accountability within the Patriotic Alliance.” Political observers say the twin scandals threaten to weaken the party’s influence at a crucial time, as coalition politics continue to shape the national and municipal landscape. The ruling party and its partners now face the challenge of balancing political alliances with the growing public demand for ethical governance.

While McKenzie has not yet issued a detailed public statement addressing the resurfaced posts, pressure from both political opponents and civil society groups suggests that silence may no longer be an option. “The public is watching,” said Mokoena. “We expect action, not excuses.”

Media Trump Warns of “Very Severe Consequences” if Putin Refuses to End Ukraine War

August 14, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Former US President Donald Trump has issued a stark warning to Russian President Vladimir Putin, saying there will be “very severe consequences” if the Kremlin does not agree to end the war in Ukraine.

The remarks come ahead of a highly anticipated summit in Alaska on Friday, where Trump is expected to meet with world leaders to discuss the ongoing conflict, now in its fourth year. Trump did not specify what measures would follow if Putin refuses, but political analysts suggest they could range from intensified sanctions to possible military and economic actions coordinated with US allies.

Speaking at a campaign-style event, Trump reiterated his belief that the war must be brought to a swift conclusion, citing global economic instability and the humanitarian toll as urgent reasons for resolution. “We have an opportunity to stop the destruction, save lives, and bring peace. But if Russia continues on this path, the world will respond in ways they won’t like,” he said.

The Kremlin has not yet commented on Trump’s statement. However, sources close to Russian officials told local media that Moscow remains skeptical of US-led peace proposals, accusing Washington of prioritising Western strategic interests over genuine peace.

International observers say Friday’s meeting could be a pivotal moment in the conflict, though many remain doubtful that a breakthrough is imminent.

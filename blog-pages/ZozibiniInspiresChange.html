Lifestyle Zozibini Tunzi Inspires Change in South Africa

November 15, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Zozibini Tunzi returns to Johannesburg this November with the same grace and clarity of purpose that have shaped her journey since her Miss Universe days. Whenever she speaks, there’s a calm strength in her voice that draws people in, not because of the crown she once wore, but because of the conviction behind every word. As she prepares to host one of South Africa’s leading women empowerment gatherings, she brings warmth, intention, and a deep sense of responsibility to the stage.

To Tunzi, empowerment isn’t a distant ideal it’s something urgent and necessary. She often reminds audiences that gender equality is still more than a century away at the current pace. For her, that’s a call to act now rather than later. She believes that when women gain access to resources, representation, and confidence, entire communities shift for the better. At EmpoweringHER 2025, she’s not just there to guide the program. She sees her role as tuning in to the energy of the room and reflecting it back, adding stories and insights that help people feel seen and inspired.

She hopes that young women especially will look at her and understand that whatever they dream of is within reach, that their goals aren’t distant or impossible. Visibility matters deeply to Tunzi. She knows the power of watching women thrive in every field business, the arts, activism, entrepreneurship and how that inspires others to rise. The more women are given the tools to succeed, she says, the closer society gets to true empowerment on a global scale.

Her journey from a small town in the Eastern Cape to international stages has taught her how influence can be used with intention. She doesn’t take the weight of her platform lightly. Each time she speaks about leadership or empowerment, she hopes it encourages young women to keep learning, keep leading, and keep passing that strength along to those who follow.

For Tunzi, empowerment is a daily choice. It’s shaped by bravery, by community, and by the people who speak life into us at the right moment. She hopes that everyone who attends the upcoming conference will hear something that ignites a spark within them a word, a story, or simply the atmosphere of being surrounded by possibility. Through her work and her voice, Zozibini Tunzi shows that power often begins quietly, in one person’s determination to lift others. But when those voices come together, the impact grows louder, stronger, and impossible to ignore.

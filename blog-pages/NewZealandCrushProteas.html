Sports New Zealand Crush Proteas to Reach T20 World Cup Final

March 5, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

New Zealand delivered a dominant performance to defeat South Africa by nine wickets in the semi final of the ICC Men's T20 World Cup at Eden Gardens, ending the Proteas’ hopes of lifting the global trophy.

The match saw New Zealand comfortably chase down South Africa’s total with clinical batting and disciplined bowling. South Africa struggled to build momentum throughout their innings, losing key wickets at crucial moments and failing to post a total that could seriously challenge the Black Caps.

New Zealand’s bowlers applied early pressure, restricting scoring opportunities and forcing mistakes from the South African batting lineup. The Proteas eventually managed to put runs on the board, but the total proved well below what was needed on a batting friendly surface.

When New Zealand came in to bat, their top order made the chase look straightforward. The opening partnership set a confident tone, rotating the strike effectively while punishing loose deliveries. South Africa’s bowlers searched for breakthroughs but found little assistance from the pitch.

The Black Caps reached the target with ease, losing only one wicket in the process. The comfortable victory highlighted New Zealand’s calm approach and ability to execute under pressure in knockout matches.

For South Africa, the defeat adds another painful chapter to their history in major international tournaments. The team entered the semi final with strong form during the competition, raising hopes among supporters that they could finally break their long standing curse in global events.

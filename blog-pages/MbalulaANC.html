Media Mbalula: Not Everyone in the ANC Is Clean

October 20, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

ANC secretary-general Fikile Mbalula has admitted that corruption continues to tarnish the image of the African National Congress, acknowledging that not everyone within the ruling party is clean.

Speaking during an engagement over the weekend, Mbalula said corruption has become a stigma the ANC now carries as a result of years of misconduct by some of its own members. He stated that while the party remains committed to renewal and accountability, it cannot ignore the reality that corruption has deeply eroded public trust.

Mbalula’s comments come amid growing scrutiny over the party’s internal discipline and its handling of cases involving state capture and mismanagement of public funds. He reiterated that the ANC leadership must continue to confront these issues head-on if it hopes to regain the confidence of South Africans ahead of the next general elections.

He added that the fight against corruption requires more than public statements, calling on all ANC members to embody ethical leadership and transparency in their work. Mbalula’s remarks mark a rare public acknowledgment from a senior figure that the ANC’s image crisis remains one of its biggest political challenges.

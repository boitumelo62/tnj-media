TNJ Media In Loving Memory of Sylvester Moss Tau
(3 April 1997 – 2026)

February 18, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

It is with profound sadness that we announce the passing of Sylvester Moss Tau, a talented young man whose light touched all who knew him. Born on 3 April 1997, Sylvester was the second and last child of Sam Mashobao and the late Sylvia Tau. He grew up surrounded by love, nurtured by his family, and shaped into the remarkable individual we remember today.

Sylvester began his education at Kim-Kgolo Primary School and continued at Tetlanyo High School, where he matriculated in 2016. He went on to pursue his studies at Vaal University of Technology, where he obtained a Diploma in Public Relations. During his time at VUT, Sylvester’s creativity and talent shone through. He participated in several music competitions, winning VUT Got Talent in 2017, and later earned a golden ticket on Idols in 2021, showcasing his passion and versatility.

His talents opened doors professionally, including an internship at David Tlale Studios, where he gained valuable industry experience. Driven by a commitment to growth, Sylvester furthered his studies with an Advanced Diploma in Public Relations, which he was set to complete this April. At the time of his passing, he was working in Public Relations at TNJ Chronicles, where he was admired for his dedication and professionalism.

At 28, Sylvester’s life was a testament to passion, creativity, and determination. His gifts in music, dedication to learning, and commitment to his work left a lasting impression on everyone he met. Whether performing on stage, excelling in his studies, or contributing at TNJ Chronicles, Sylvester’s impact will be remembered and cherished.

At the time of his passing, Sylvester was staying with his sister, Faith, in Kroonstad. The funeral service will take place in his hometown of Kimberley in the Northern Cape on 21 February.

Sylvester leaves behind his loving father, his aunt who was a mother figure in his life, his sisters and brothers, nephews and nieces, and the extended family who cherished him deeply. He is also fondly remembered by his friends, colleagues, and the community whose lives he touched.

Donations will be accepted in support of the family. Those wishing to contribute may donate to Faith Msimango, Account Number 1612690910, Capitec Bank, Branch Code 470010.

Sylvester’s life reminds us of the power of pursuing one’s passions, sharing joy, and leaving a positive mark on those around us. Though he has left us too soon, the memories he created and the joy he shared will live on in the hearts of all who knew him.

May his soul rest in eternal peace.

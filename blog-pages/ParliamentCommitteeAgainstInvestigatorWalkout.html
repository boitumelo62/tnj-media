Media Parliament Committee Moves Against Investigator After Dramatic Walkout

March 12, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Tensions have escalated within Parliament’s ad hoc committee investigating allegations of corruption within the police service after members announced plans to pursue a criminal complaint against forensic investigator Paul O’Sullivan.

The decision follows a dramatic moment during committee proceedings when O’Sullivan reportedly walked out of a session that was examining evidence related to alleged misconduct within police ranks. Committee members described the move as disruptive and potentially disrespectful to the parliamentary process.

The committee was established to examine claims of corruption and interference within law enforcement structures. Lawmakers say the inquiry is an important step toward restoring accountability and strengthening public confidence in the criminal justice system.

Members of the committee argue that O’Sullivan’s departure from the proceedings undermined the work of Parliament and disrupted efforts to obtain crucial testimony. As a result, they are now considering laying a criminal complaint linked to his conduct during the hearing.

Supporters of O’Sullivan have suggested that tensions during the session may have contributed to the incident. However committee representatives insist that all participants in parliamentary inquiries are expected to respect procedural rules and remain engaged in the process.

The situation has added a new layer of controversy to an inquiry that is already politically sensitive. Allegations of corruption within policing structures have long been a concern in South Africa and the committee’s work is being closely watched by both political parties and civil society groups.

Legal experts say the decision to pursue criminal action would be unusual but not unprecedented if Parliament believes its processes have been deliberately obstructed.

The committee is expected to continue its investigation while deciding on the next steps regarding the potential complaint

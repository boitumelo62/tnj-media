Media TikTok Secures US Future Through New American Controlled Joint Venture

January 23, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

TikTok will continue operating in the United States following the creation of a new company that places control of the platform largely in American hands. The move ends years of political pressure on TikTok to separate from its Chinese parent company ByteDance.

The new entity, TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC, now oversees TikTok’s US operations as well as other ByteDance owned apps in the country, including CapCut and Lemon8. ByteDance retains a minority stake of 19.9 percent, while control rests with American investors and leadership.

Ownership of the joint venture is shared among several major firms. Forty five percent is split equally between US private equity firm Silver Lake, US technology company Oracle, and Abu Dhabi based investment firm MGX. The remaining shares are held by mostly American investors, including the Dell Family Office and Vastmere Strategic Investments, an affiliate of Susquehanna International Group.

The restructuring follows years of claims by US lawmakers that TikTok posed a national security risk through potential access to US user data and influence over political content. TikTok has consistently denied these allegations, and no public evidence has supported them.

Under the new arrangement, all US user data will be stored on Oracle cloud servers within the United States. The joint venture will also control privacy, cybersecurity, content moderation, and trust and safety policies for the US market.

The recommendation algorithm for American users will be retrained using US data and operated on Oracle infrastructure, creating a US specific version of the platform.

Leadership includes CEO Adam Presser and chief strategy officer Will Farrell. The board features representatives from Silver Lake, Oracle, TPG Global, Susquehanna International Group, and MGX, along with TikTok CEO Shou Zi Chew as a director.

TikTok remains available to millions of Americans, though its operations and governance are now firmly US based.

Media Former MP Vincent Smith Sentenced to Seven Years for Corruption

March 6, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Johannesburg High Court has sentenced former Member of Parliament Vincent Smith to seven years in prison after he was convicted on corruption related charges.

Smith, who previously served as an ANC MP and chaired Parliament’s correctional services committee, was found guilty of accepting benefits linked to Bosasa, the controversial facilities management company that became central to several corruption investigations.

The case forms part of a wider crackdown on corruption linked to Bosasa, which was accused of using gifts and financial incentives to influence political figures and secure government contracts. During the trial the court heard evidence that Smith received payments and other benefits from individuals associated with the company. Prosecutors argued that these payments were linked to his position in Parliament and his influence over matters relating to correctional services.

The court ultimately found that the evidence supported the charges against him, leading to the prison sentence handed down this week.

Smith’s conviction marks one of the more prominent outcomes of corruption cases involving political office bearers. The judgment has been welcomed by anti-corruption advocates who say it demonstrates that public officials can be held accountable for abusing positions of power.

Legal experts say the sentence also sends a strong message regarding the consequences of corruption within government institutions.

The Bosasa scandal has remained one of the most significant corruption controversies to emerge from inquiries into state capture, drawing attention to the relationships between business interests and political leaders.

Smith now joins a growing list of individuals convicted in corruption related cases as authorities continue efforts to strengthen accountability and restore public trust in state institutions.

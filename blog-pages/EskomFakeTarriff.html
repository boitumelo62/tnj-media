Media Fake Eskom Price Hike Message Circulates On WhatsApp

October 14, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

A false message claiming that Eskom will introduce a new electricity tariff increase from 1 November 2025 is circulating widely on WhatsApp, causing confusion among consumers across the country.

According to the power utility, the message originated from a spoofed community radio website based in Richards Bay that was recently re-registered in India. Eskom has urged the public to ignore the post and verify information only through its official communication platforms.

The fake notice, which mimics Eskom’s official formatting, falsely claims that residential tariffs will rise by over 20 percent due to “emergency infrastructure costs.” The misinformation has sparked public frustration, with many citizens already struggling under the pressure of high living expenses and intermittent power cuts.

Eskom spokesperson Daphne Mokwena confirmed that there is no approved tariff adjustment taking effect in November, noting that the National Energy Regulator of South Africa (NERSA) is the only authority that can approve such changes.

Cybersecurity experts have warned that similar scams are becoming more common, often using manipulated websites and messaging platforms to spread false information or collect user data.

Eskom has encouraged South Africans to report any suspicious energy-related messages or websites to its fraud hotline.

The Women's Rugby World Cup opening round is here as the 16 teams confirm their line-ups for the first matches. The competition will begin in Sunderland as hosts England face the USA at the Stadium of Light. Australia will open their campaign the following day as they face Samoa in Salford, just outside of Manchester.

NOTE: All teams will be announced 48 hours prior to kick-off.

Saturday August 23

4:30 am AEST: England v USA
Stadium of Light, Sunderland — ad-free, live and on demand on Stan Sport

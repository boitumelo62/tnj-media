Media Steenhuisen Set to Step Back From DA Leadership Race

February 4, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Democratic Alliance leader John Steenhuisen is expected to withdraw from the party’s leadership race ahead of its national elective congress in April, with an announcement anticipated in Durban. Steenhuisen has led the DA since 2019, overseeing election campaigns, coalition negotiations and internal policy debates. His tenure has included efforts to reposition the party as a governing alternative while navigating ideological divisions within its ranks.

Sources within the party suggest the decision is aimed at reducing internal tension and allowing a clearer leadership contest ahead of the congress. The DA faces growing pressure from rival parties and ongoing challenges in coalition run municipalities.

Supporters credit Steenhuisen with maintaining party stability during a turbulent political period. Critics argue the party has struggled to expand its voter base and redefine its identity under his leadership.

The elective congress is expected to be a turning point for the DA as it reassesses strategy ahead of future elections. Leadership changes could influence policy direction, campaign messaging and coalition approaches.

Political analysts say the manner in which the transition is handled will be critical to maintaining unity and public confidence. Steenhuisen’s expected withdrawal marks a significant moment for the opposition party as it prepares for the next phase of its political journey.

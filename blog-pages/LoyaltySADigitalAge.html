The 2025 ASK AFRICA Orange Index reveals a clear shift in what drives customer loyalty in South Africa. In an economy that’s stretched thin but still moving forward, consumers are becoming more deliberate in how they spend their time and money. Flashy marketing and deep discounts are no longer enough what people value most now are empathy, fairness, and efficiency. Based on nearly 60,000 interviews across 26 industries and 200 brands, the Index shows that emotional connection is becoming just as important as functional delivery. South Africans are under pressure, with around 70% reporting financial stress and many spending a large portion of their income on debt.

But instead of withdrawing, they’re refocusing cutting out waste and rewarding brands that make their lives easier and more meaningful. This shift is visible across sectors like automotive, forecourt convenience, and casual dining. At petrol stations, for example, customers want fast, clean, seamless experiences. In the automotive space, trust is declining due to rising costs and poor after sales service, while newer, more transparent brands especially from China and India are gaining ground. In dining, consumers are opting for mid range options that offer consistent quality and fair pricing.

Digital is now central to almost every customer journey, and ASK AFRICA’s new Digital Experience Barometer highlights how it shapes expectations. While digital excels at speed and convenience, trust and emotional connection still come from human interaction. The top performing brands are those that blend both making tech effortless and letting people handle the feeling. These leading brands also share a common approach they’re radically clear, easy to deal with, and quick to respond when things go wrong. Most importantly, they treat fairness as a business advantage, not just a courtesy.

Customer experience in South Africa is evolving. It’s no longer just about fixing pain points it’s about creating moments of delight and building emotional loyalty. In a market where functional delivery is expected, empathy is what sets brands apart. Those who understand this shift and act on it will lead the way forward.

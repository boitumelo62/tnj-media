Lifestyle The Rise of Ultra-Luxury Home Cinemas

July 27, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

As technology keeps moving forward at lightning speed, the way we experience entertainment at home is also being transformed. Home automation and cinema setups have evolved into something truly futuristic far beyond your average TV and sound system. Unlike phones and laptops that get updated every year, home cinemas are built to grow with your needs. They’re modular, meaning you can upgrade and enhance them over time. But make no mistake these setups come with a hefty price tag, ranging anywhere from about R1 million to well over R14 million.

This isn’t just about adding a TV and a couple of speakers to a spare room. According to Warren Husband, a solutions architect at Homemation, we’re talking about a fully immersive experience that rivals and often beats what you’d get at a commercial movie theatre. He compares these setups to high-end sports cars, each one customized depending on your taste, budget, and ambition.

These rooms aren’t just for movies either. Homeowners are using them for wellness activities like yoga and workouts. Warren explains that his goal is to design spaces that go beyond just loud action movies instead, he wants to create peaceful, immersive environments, like a digital forest filled with calming sounds from all directions.

During a recent media tour, journalists got to experience what’s said to be the most advanced home theatre on the African continent. It was more than just impressive it was mind blowing. The room is designed to handle an incredible sound setup, featuring a massive 3 square meters of subwoofer cone area and a staggering 60,000 watts of amplification. To pull this off, the room was built with precise engineering to eliminate vibrations and deliver ultra-clean, deep bass. When they played the trailer for the movie Prey, the entire room seemed to pulse with energy.

At the heart of the setup is a 28-channel Ascendo audio system in a complex 11.11.6 Dolby Atmos layout. That includes six 21-inch subwoofers hidden behind the screen, four more at the back of the room, and a monstrous 32-inch infrasonic subwoofer all tuned to perfection using Trinnov’s advanced WaveForming tech. The visual experience is just as impressive. A massive 177-inch Stewart Filmscreen made with acoustically transparent fabric ensures the audio passes through clearly, while the Barco Hodr cinemascope projector beams a vivid, cinema-grade image with over 7,500 lumens of brightness.

Of course, a setup like this isn’t exactly for casual viewers. If you happen to have R14 million burning a hole in your pocket and you’re willing to skip buying that dream sports car, this could be your ultimate escape. Whether you want to watch the latest blockbuster, catch an F1 race, or cheer on the Springboks in total isolation, you’ll have a luxurious space to do it in and maybe even a quiet place to hide from the world while you're at it.

Media Protection Order Revealed in Warrick Stock Case as Questions Mount Over Prior Warnings

December 18, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

New information has emerged in the investigation into the murder of Warrick Stock, revealing that the businessman had opened a protection order against several individuals just a week before he was shot dead in the Johannesburg CBD. The disclosure has raised serious concerns about whether warning signs were missed and whether more could have been done to prevent the killing.

According to sources familiar with the matter, Stock approached the courts seeking legal protection after allegedly receiving threats and experiencing ongoing intimidation. The protection order reportedly named multiple individuals, although authorities have not yet confirmed whether any of those listed are directly linked to his murder. Investigators are now closely examining the contents of the order, including affidavits submitted by Stock, to determine the nature of the threats and whether they escalated in the days leading up to his death.

Stock was gunned down in a busy part of the Johannesburg CBD, an incident that shocked the business community and raised fresh fears about violent crime in urban centres. Witnesses described scenes of panic as gunshots rang out, with bystanders scrambling for safety. Emergency services responded swiftly, but Stock was declared dead at the scene.

Police have confirmed that the investigation remains at an early stage, with detectives following several leads. They are reviewing CCTV footage from nearby buildings, analysing cellphone data, and interviewing individuals who may have had recent disputes with the deceased. The existence of the protection order has now added a new layer to the investigation, as officers work to establish motive and identify possible suspects.

Legal experts say protection orders are often sought when individuals believe their safety is at risk, but they also stress that such orders are preventative tools rather than guarantees of protection. The case has renewed debate about the effectiveness of protection orders in situations involving serious threats and organised violence.

Community leaders and business associations have expressed concern, calling for stronger measures to ensure the safety of people operating in the city centre. They argue that the killing highlights broader issues around crime, enforcement, and early intervention when warning signs are raised.

As the investigation continues, police have urged anyone with information to come forward. The case is expected to place renewed focus on how threats are assessed and managed by authorities, particularly when formal legal steps, such as protection orders, have already been taken.

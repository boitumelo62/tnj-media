Media SA Civil Society Takes Fight Against Tariffs to Washington

September 22, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Solidariteit and AfriForum have dispatched a high-level delegation to the United States in an effort to strengthen bilateral ties and advocate for South Africa’s economic interests. The visit comes in the wake of punishing 30 percent tariffs imposed on South African exports, a move that has sparked concern among local businesses and trade experts.

According to the organisations, the mission aims to engage directly with U.S. policymakers, trade representatives, and civil society stakeholders to present South Africa’s case for fairer trade treatment. They argue that the current tariffs threaten jobs and could stifle economic growth, particularly in sectors reliant on exports.

“This is about standing up for South African workers and industries that are being unfairly penalised,” said a spokesperson for AfriForum. “We believe dialogue is the first step toward a solution that benefits both nations.”

Economists have warned that prolonged tariffs could have ripple effects across manufacturing, agriculture, and mining, with small businesses likely to feel the most pressure. The delegation is expected to spend several days in Washington, D.C., holding meetings with key decision-makers and lobbying for the removal or reduction of the tariffs.

The outcome of these engagements could shape South Africa’s trade relationship with the U.S. for years to come, observers say.

Media Madlanga Commission Hears Shocking Account of Whistleblower Murder

November 11, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Madlanga Commission has heard explosive testimony claiming that a whistleblower was murdered after revealing details of an unlawful EMPD operation. The allegation was presented during Tuesday’s session, adding a new and deeply troubling layer to an inquiry that has already exposed a series of operational irregularities within the metro police structure.

According to evidence led before the commission, the whistleblower had confidentially alerted senior officials to an EMPD operation that allegedly violated standard policing procedures and placed civilians at risk. Their assassination, which occurred shortly after the disclosure, has raised fears that the killing may have been an attempt to silence someone who threatened to expose internal wrongdoing.

Commission chairperson Justice Madlanga expressed concern over the gravity of the claim and instructed investigators to treat the information as a priority. Families of the whistleblower have waited years for clarity, and the allegation has revived pressure on the inquiry to determine whether organised interference or cover-ups played a role.

The commission will continue to examine operational failures, internal discipline structures, and the possibility of criminal collusion within the department as more witnesses are expected to come forward.

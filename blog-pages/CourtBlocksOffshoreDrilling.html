Business Court Blocks Offshore Drilling Project off Cape Coast

August 15, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The High Court has overturned the authorisation for TotalEnergies to proceed with its planned offshore drilling operations off the Cape coast, delivering a significant victory to environmental and community groups who have long opposed the project.

In its ruling, the court found that the environmental impact assessment for the venture failed to adequately address the socio-economic and climate implications of the drilling. Critics argued that the proposed exploration posed risks to marine biodiversity, local fishing communities, and South Africa’s broader climate commitments.

Environmental organisations hailed the decision as a milestone in protecting sensitive coastal ecosystems from industrial exploitation. “This sends a strong message that short-term profit cannot outweigh the long-term well-being of people and the planet,” said one campaigner involved in the case.

TotalEnergies has maintained that the project would generate jobs, boost local economic activity, and contribute to South Africa’s energy security. The company has yet to comment on whether it will appeal the decision. The ruling underscores growing judicial scrutiny of large-scale energy projects in the country, particularly those with potential environmental fallout.

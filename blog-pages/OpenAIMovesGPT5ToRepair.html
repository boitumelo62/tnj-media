Tech News OpenAI Moves to Repair GPT-5 After User Outcry

August 12, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

OpenAI is working urgently to address widespread criticism of its newly launched GPT-5 model, after many users reported that it performed worse than expected.

The model, which was billed as a major step forward, has faced complaints that it produces less accurate answers, feels emotionally distant, and struggles with consistency. The company has attributed much of the dissatisfaction to a fault in its model-switching system, which caused unpredictable behaviour and reduced output quality.

In an effort to calm the backlash, Chief Executive Sam Altman confirmed that the previous GPT-4o model would be made available again for paying subscribers. He also promised a series of improvements, including fixes to GPT-5’s routing mechanism, increased usage limits, and the rollout of a new “thinking mode” aimed at improving complex problem-solving.

The controversy has reignited discussion about the relationship between users and AI personalities. Some welcomed GPT-5’s more direct tone, but others expressed disappointment over the loss of the warmth and conversational style they had grown accustomed to with earlier versions.

Media FBI Releases Images in Charlie Kirk Shooting, Offers $100K Reward

September 12, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Federal Bureau of Investigation has released surveillance images and video of a person of interest in the fatal shooting of conservative activist Charlie Kirk. A reward of up to $100,000 is being offered for information leading to the identification and arrest of the suspect.

Investigators have obtained clear video footage showing the suspect climbing to a rooftop, firing the fatal shot, and then fleeing toward a wooded area. A bolt-action rifle believed to have been used in the attack was recovered near the scene. Authorities have also collected shoe impressions, a palm print, and forearm imprints believed to belong to the shooter.

The FBI reports that more than 7,000 tips have already been received and over 200 interviews conducted as the manhunt continues. Two individuals who were initially detained have been cleared of involvement. Utah Governor Spencer Cox has pledged that prosecutors will seek the death penalty if the suspect is caught and convicted.

Charlie Kirk, co-founder of Turning Point USA and a well-known conservative commentator, was shot and killed on September 10, 2025, while speaking at Utah Valley University as part of his “American Comeback Tour.” The gunman is believed to have fired from a rooftop approximately 200 yards from the event site. Security cameras captured the moments leading up to and following the shooting, providing investigators with critical evidence. The motive remains under investigation, and authorities continue to urge the public to come forward with any information.

Tech News Satellite communications milestone for South Africa

February 9, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Communications Minister Solly Malatsi has advanced South Africa’s satellite communications agenda with the launch of Space42’s Thuraya service in the country, marking a significant milestone in expanding national connectivity.

The satellite service is expected to improve communication capabilities in remote and underserved areas, particularly for sectors such as mining, agriculture, maritime operations, aviation, and emergency response services. Satellite connectivity is increasingly seen as essential for bridging the digital divide in rural regions where traditional infrastructure is limited or unreliable.

Government officials say the launch aligns with South Africa’s broader digital transformation strategy and could strengthen national security, disaster management, and economic development initiatives. Enhanced satellite coverage is also expected to support businesses operating in remote locations and improve resilience during network outages.

Industry experts note that increased competition in the satellite market could reduce costs, expand access, and drive innovation in connectivity solutions, positioning South Africa as a growing player in the regional space and communications sector.

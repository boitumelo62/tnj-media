Media Ekurhuleni Defends Landmark Land Grab

October 28, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

A landmark case testing South Africa’s first expropriation without compensation has entered mediation, with the City of Ekurhuleni defending its seizure of 34 hectares of privately owned land for urban development.

The property owners argue that the move is unconstitutional and erodes investor confidence, while Ekurhuleni maintains it acted lawfully to advance equitable land reform. The case is being closely watched as a potential test of how expropriation could work in practice if national legislation is adopted.

This is the first dispute of its kind since government signalled its intent to pursue land reform through expropriation without compensation, a policy rooted in correcting apartheid-era dispossession. Legal experts warn that the outcome could set a crucial precedent for municipalities, either strengthening or stalling the land reform agenda.

The mediation comes amid rising public tension over land ownership and slow progress in redistributing property to historically disadvantaged communities.

Media Light Aircraft Headed to Margate Crashes Near Pietermaritzburg

October 16, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Authorities in KwaZulu-Natal have launched a full investigation after a light aircraft believed to have been en route to Margate crashed near Pietermaritzburg on Wednesday afternoon. The incident occurred shortly after the plane took off from Oribi Airport, forcing the KwaZulu-Natal Road Traffic Inspectorate to divert traffic and secure the crash site while emergency teams rushed to the scene.

Eyewitnesses in the area reported seeing the aircraft flying unusually low before it lost altitude and disappeared behind a line of trees. A thick column of smoke was seen moments later as residents alerted emergency services. Paramedics, firefighters, and members of the South African Police Service arrived within minutes and cordoned off the area to begin rescue and recovery operations.

Early information suggests the aircraft may have experienced mechanical difficulties shortly after departure, though investigators have not yet confirmed the cause. Teams from the South African Civil Aviation Authority have begun gathering evidence, examining wreckage, and interviewing witnesses as part of a formal inquiry.

Provincial transport officials said recovery operations continued late into Wednesday evening and through Thursday morning. At this stage, the number of people on board and the extent of injuries or fatalities have not been confirmed. Officials have appealed for patience while next of kin are notified.

Traffic around the crash site was severely affected, with long delays reported on the routes leading to Oribi and the N3 bypass. The KwaZulu-Natal Department of Transport has urged motorists to avoid the area until clean-up operations are complete. Authorities expect to release a preliminary report once aviation experts conclude their on-site assessment. The crash has sent shockwaves through the Pietermaritzburg community, with local residents describing the incident as both frightening and tragic.

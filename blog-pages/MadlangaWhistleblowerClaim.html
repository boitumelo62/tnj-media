Madlanga Commission Hears Shocking Account of Whistleblower Murder

The Madlanga Commission has returned to the disturbing allegation that a whistleblower was murdered after exposing an unlawful EMPD operation, deepening public concern about the level of corruption and intimidation within the policing environment. This afternoon’s session focused on further clarifying the events leading up to the whistleblower’s death and assessing the credibility of the evidence presented.

Investigators told the commission that new documents and witness statements are being examined to establish whether the whistleblower’s disclosure placed them in immediate danger and whether anyone within the department may have benefited from their silence. The commission is also reviewing past disciplinary cases involving the same operation to determine whether misconduct was already known but ignored.

Justice Madlanga emphasised again that the allegation strikes at the heart of public trust in policing institutions. He encouraged witnesses with information to come forward, assuring them that protective measures will be enforced where needed. Families of the whistleblower attended the hearing and said they hoped this renewed focus would finally bring accountability and closure.

The commission is expected to call additional witnesses and may request cooperation from national law enforcement bodies as it tries to piece together the events behind one of the inquiry’s most troubling claims.

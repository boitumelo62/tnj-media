Media Light Aircraft Crashes Into Alberton School

October 27, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Two people narrowly escaped injury after a light aircraft crashed into a school building in Alberton, Johannesburg, during an evaluation flight on Sunday morning.

According to reports, the aircraft’s engine failed mid-flight, forcing the pilot to attempt an emergency landing that ended with the plane colliding into a section of the school premises. Fortunately, no pupils or staff were present at the time, and both the pilot and co-pilot walked away uninjured.

Emergency services responded quickly to the scene, extinguishing a small fire and securing the area. Civil Aviation Authority officials have since launched an investigation to determine the cause of the engine failure and to assess potential mechanical or procedural faults.

Residents in the area expressed relief that the crash did not result in fatalities, noting that the school is usually busy during the week. The aircraft wreckage is expected to be removed once the investigation concludes.

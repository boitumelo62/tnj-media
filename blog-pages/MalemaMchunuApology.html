Media Malema calls on Mchunu to apologise over ‘misleading’ Parliament claims

October 22, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

EFF leader Julius Malema has called on suspended Police Minister Senzo Mchunu to apologise for allegedly misleading Parliament about threats he claimed to have received from KwaZulu-Natal police commissioner Lieutenant-General Nhlanhla Mkhwanazi.

Mchunu made the claim during testimony before the ad hoc committee investigating corruption and interference within the police ministry. He stated that he had been threatened by Mkhwanazi following his decision to disband the province’s political killings task team.

Malema, speaking during a media briefing, said Mchunu’s statements were reckless and misleading, adding that they damaged the integrity of Parliament. The EFF leader said that if Mchunu could not substantiate the threats, he must apologise publicly and withdraw his comments.

The call comes as Mchunu continues his appearance before the committee, which is probing allegations of abuse of power and political interference in police operations. Parliament has not yet responded to Malema’s demand, but the committee is expected to deliberate further on Mchunu’s testimony this week.

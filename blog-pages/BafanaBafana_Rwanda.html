Sport Bafana Bafana Gear Up for Crucial World Cup Qualifier Against Rwanda

October 13, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Bafana Bafana are preparing for what coach Hugo Broos has described as a must-win World Cup qualifying match against Rwanda at Mbombela Stadium tomorrow evening. Kickoff is scheduled for six o’clock.

The national side enters the fixture under pressure to secure maximum points after a mixed start to their qualification campaign. Broos has urged his players to maintain composure and make use of home advantage, emphasizing the importance of early control and attacking precision.

Team captain Ronwen Williams said morale within the camp was high, with players showing determination during training sessions at the Lowveld venue. Williams noted that the squad was focused on executing tactical instructions and delivering a performance worthy of their home supporters.

Rwanda, who arrived in South Africa over the weekend, are known for their disciplined defensive structure and quick counterattacks, which could test Bafana’s midfield balance and transition play.

Analysts have highlighted that a victory would significantly boost South Africa’s standing in the group and keep their hopes alive for World Cup qualification. The South African Football Association has confirmed that security, crowd management, and transport logistics are all in place ahead of the high-stakes match.

Fans across the country are expected to turn out in large numbers or tune in from home to back the national team as they aim for a decisive win.

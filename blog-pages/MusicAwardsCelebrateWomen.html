Media Basadi in Music Awards Celebrate Women Behind the Scenes

August 3, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

The fourth annual Basadi in Music Awards kicked off on Friday, 1 August, with the Vanguard Awards at the Joburg Theatre. It was a vibrant and emotional evening that paid tribute to women who usually work behind the scenes in the music and entertainment industry people like producers, managers, stylists, and creatives whose efforts are often overlooked but are absolutely essential to the success of artists.

It was a proud night for media personalities. Laconco took home the award for podcast presenter of the year, while Lootlove was named entertainment radio presenter of the year. Relebogile Mabotja was recognised for her work as entertainment TV producer of the year, a nod to her contributions behind the scenes.

The performances were a big highlight of the night. Moneoa and Zama Jobe delivered powerful, soulful sets that stirred emotions in the crowd, and a lively hip-hop medley followed, featuring Dyna Steez, Moozlie, Rouge, and Nadia Nakai. The evening wrapped up on a reflective, elegant note with a smooth performance from Babalwa M.

The heart of the event lay in acknowledging those whose work is rarely in the limelight. As one of the speakers pointed out, people like journalists play a huge role in helping artists be seen and heard highlighting just how many people contribute to success in this industry.

Two women received special honours for their long-standing impact. Portia Mngomezulu, the entrepreneur behind Portia M Skin Solutions, was honoured with the Mosadi in Business award, presented by Brand South Africa. Meanwhile, Bongi “Sis Bongi” Mlotshwa was celebrated as trailblazer of the year for her outstanding contribution to beauty and television over more than 30 years.

Hloni Modise Matau, the CEO of Basadi in Music, reflected on how much the event has grown. What started as a single night to honour women in music has become a two-day celebration of their excellence not just on stage but also behind the scenes, in boardrooms, studios, and creative spaces across the industry. She spoke with pride about shining a spotlight on the hard work and dedication that often goes unnoticed.

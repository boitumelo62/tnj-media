Sports Zakithi Nene Targets Gold in 400m World Championship Final

September 17, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South African sprint star Zakithi Nene is set to compete for gold in the men’s 400m final at the World Championships in Tokyo tomorrow.

Nene qualified impressively by clocking 44.20 seconds in the semifinals, securing one of the fastest times heading into the final. His performance has placed him among the favourites, as he looks to cap off a strong season with a world title.

Athletics South Africa has praised Nene’s consistency, noting that his recent form suggests he is capable of breaking the national record if conditions in Tokyo are favourable.

The 400m final is scheduled for 8:40 PM local time and is expected to be one of the most closely contested races of the championship. Fans back home will be watching eagerly as Nene aims to bring home South Africa’s first gold medal in the event since Wayde van Niekerk’s historic win.

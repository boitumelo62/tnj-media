Sports Proteas Women Reach ICC World Cup Final for the First Time in History

October 30, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Proteas Women have made history by advancing to the final of the ICC Women’s Cricket World Cup for the very first time. Their commanding semi-final performance has ignited national pride and renewed enthusiasm for women’s sport in South Africa.

Led by captain Laura Wolvaardt, the team demonstrated resilience and tactical brilliance, overcoming top-ranked opponents through disciplined bowling and sharp fielding. Their journey to the final marks a defining moment for South African cricket, highlighting years of investment in talent development and the growth of the women’s game.

Cricket South Africa has praised the team for their determination and unity, describing their success as a testament to the country’s rising competitiveness on the world stage. Fans across the nation are eagerly anticipating the final, with hopes that the Proteas Women will bring home South Africa’s first-ever world title in women’s cricket.

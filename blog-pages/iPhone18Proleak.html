Media iPhone 18 Pro leak hints at smaller cutout, new chip

Apple’s next generation iPhone 18 Pro is shaping up to bring a mix of design tweaks and significant internal upgrades, according to new analyst reports and supply chain leaks.

One of the most noticeable changes could be a smaller Dynamic Island. Reports suggest Apple plans to move some Face ID components beneath the display, which would allow the cutout at the top of the screen to shrink without fully removing the feature. This would give users more usable screen space while keeping the familiar interface element.

The camera system is also expected to see a meaningful upgrade. The main 48-megapixel camera on the iPhone 18 Pro is tipped to feature a variable aperture for the first time on an iPhone. This would allow the camera to adjust how much light enters the lens, improving low light performance and offering more control over depth of field. Current iPhone Pro models use a fixed aperture, limiting optical flexibility.

Under the hood, Apple is expected to introduce its A20 Pro processor, built using a next generation two nanometre manufacturing process. The move should deliver better performance and improved power efficiency, potentially boosting battery life and supporting more advanced-on device artificial intelligence features.

Connectivity is also set for an overhaul. The iPhone 18 Pro could debut Apple’s next generation in house wireless and modem hardware, including a new N2 networking chip and a C2 cellular modem. Apple has been gradually replacing third party components with its own silicon to gain tighter control over performance and energy management.

Additional rumours point to further camera sensor upgrades and a larger battery for the Pro Max model, though Apple is unlikely to confirm any details until its usual September launch event in 2026.

If the leaks are accurate, the iPhone 18 Pro will focus on refining the current design while delivering noticeable improvements in performance, photography, and connectivity.

Media Mchunu Unshaken by Madlanga Commission Revelations

October 6, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Madlanga Commission Hears Explosive Testimony on Political Killings

Suspended Police Minister Senzo Mchunu has maintained his composure in the wake of fresh allegations presented before the Madlanga Commission of Inquiry, which linked him to possible political interference in South Africa’s justice system.

Mchunu, who has been on suspension since early 2025 pending the commission’s findings, expressed confidence that the ongoing proceedings would ultimately clear his name. Speaking through his legal team, the former minister said he would not be distracted by what he termed “politically motivated distortions” aimed at undermining his public image.

The revelations surfaced during testimony from senior officials who alleged that Mchunu had influenced the appointment and transfer of certain law enforcement figures to serve political interests. These claims have since sparked widespread public debate, with opposition parties calling for greater transparency in the commission’s proceedings.

Legal experts note that while the allegations are serious, no formal charges have been laid against Mchunu. The commission, chaired by Justice Mandisa Madlanga, is expected to continue hearing evidence throughout October as it examines broader concerns of executive interference in judicial and policing structures.

For now, Mchunu remains suspended pending the conclusion of the inquiry, while the Presidency has reiterated its commitment to upholding due process and ensuring accountability at all levels of government.

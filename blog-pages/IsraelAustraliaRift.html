Media Israel's Prime Minister Accuses Australian Counterpart of Betrayal Amid Diplomatic Rift

August 20, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Israeli Prime Minister has accused his Australian counterpart of “betraying Israel” and “abandoning” Australia’s Jewish community, intensifying diplomatic tensions between the two countries.

The remarks follow recent policy shifts in Canberra that Israel says undermine its position in ongoing Middle East disputes. While details of the disagreement have not been publicly clarified, officials in Jerusalem suggested that Australian decisions on regional diplomacy and support for international resolutions played a role in sparking the rift.

The accusation marks one of the sharpest public rebukes by Israel toward a close Western partner in recent years. Australia has historically maintained strong ties with Israel, balancing them alongside its relationships with Arab and Muslim-majority states in the Asia-Pacific region.

Canberra has yet to issue an official response to the claims, though government sources indicated that its foreign policy decisions are guided by international law and the pursuit of peace in the region.

The diplomatic fallout comes amid heightened global scrutiny of the war in Gaza and Israel’s broader foreign policy stance. Analysts note that the dispute with Australia could complicate Israel’s efforts to maintain consistent international support at a time when criticism of its actions in Gaza has intensified.

Despite the escalating rhetoric, neither government has signalled a formal break in relations. However, observers warn that unless the two sides de-escalate, the disagreement could strain bilateral cooperation across trade, defence, and cultural exchanges.

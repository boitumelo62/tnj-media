Business Why Marketers Must Review Agency Partnerships Now

August 29, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Budget pressures are forcing brands to squeeze more value out of every rand they spend. At the same time, rapid advancements in AI are raising serious concerns around job losses, keeping brand authenticity intact, and whether consumers can still trust what they see and hear. It’s also getting harder to stand out in a media space that’s more crowded than ever cutting through the noise now demands smarter targeting and braver creativity.

But the pressure doesn’t stop there. Today’s marketing setups are more complicated than ever, with the average client managing around 13 agency relationships across everything from media and creative to digital, PR and performance. In this kind of environment, getting everyone aligned around one clear strategy isn’t a luxury it’s a must. Now consider timing. About 70–80% of South African companies wrap up their financial year in February, meaning the clock is ticking for any meaningful agency review before the new cycle begins.

And if you're thinking about making changes or even just evaluating where things stand, you need to start. Doing a proper review of your agency relationships takes at least three months. Onboarding a new partner? That’s another three. Add in December which disappears into the festive break and suddenly, July doesn’t seem that early anymore. Marketers who wait risk entering 2026 stuck in partnerships that aren't aligned to their evolving goals.

This isn’t always about replacing agencies. It’s about checking whether your current partners still have the right talent, tools, and mindset to handle what’s coming next. That’s what Johanna McDowell, CEO of the Independent Agency Search & Selection Company, is pointing to a need for clarity, not knee-jerk changes. Instead of launching a full pitch process, many marketers would be better off starting with an honest audit. Ask, can we level up our current partnerships? Where are the gaps? Are there overlaps that waste time or budget? It’s not about changing for the sake of it it’s about making sure your marketing setup fits your future.

This is where intermediary consultants can make a real difference. They bring something that internal teams and even agencies can’t: objectivity. They don’t have a stake in the outcome. Their job is to find what’s best for the client, not to win business. They also have access to data, global trends, and tools that help shift decisions from gut feel to fact-based strategy. For example, platforms like AGENCY SCOPE track how agencies perform over time, how trends are evolving, and where in-house teams are gaining traction. Insights like these can completely change how a brand evaluates its agency partners and avoid costly missteps.

Brands that don’t get their partnerships right will spend most of 2026 putting out fires instead of driving growth. But those who take the time now to evaluate and align will move into the new year with momentum. If you’re even thinking about reviewing your agency setup for 2026, now’s the time. Whether it's a full ecosystem audit or a targeted review of capabilities, the window is closing. Don’t gamble on whether your current setup can handle the future find out now.

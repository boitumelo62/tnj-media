Business Amazon South Africa to Launch Pop-Up Stores in Cape Town and Johannesburg

September 19, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Amazon South Africa has announced that it will open temporary “pop-up” stores in Cape Town and Johannesburg, offering local shoppers a rare opportunity to experience the online retailer in a physical setting.

The initiative forms part of Amazon’s “Shop Mzansi” campaign, which highlights locally made and locally designed products during Heritage Month. The pop-ups will feature a curated range of South African sellers who trade on Amazon’s online marketplace, giving small and medium-sized enterprises a chance to showcase their goods directly to customers.

The Johannesburg pop-up is scheduled to open first, running for several days in one of the city’s major shopping centres. Cape Town will follow with its own event later in September. Each site will include interactive displays, live demonstrations and opportunities for visitors to meet the creators behind the products. Amazon has indicated that exclusive in-store promotions and discounts will be available for customers who make purchases or sign up for the platform during the events.

Company representatives explained that the project aims to bridge the gap between digital commerce and the tactile experience that many South Africans still value. “These stores allow people to see, touch and understand the stories behind the products they buy,” said a spokesperson. “It is also a way to celebrate the creativity and entrepreneurship of South African businesses.”

Economic analysts view the move as a strategic step to strengthen Amazon’s brand presence in the country, particularly as competition intensifies in the local e-commerce sector. By creating a direct connection between online sellers and physical shoppers, Amazon hopes to foster trust among consumers who may be hesitant to purchase solely through a website.

The pop-up initiative underscores Amazon’s broader commitment to supporting South African entrepreneurs while offering customers a richer shopping experience. After the temporary stores close, all featured products will remain available through Amazon’s local online marketplace, ensuring continued exposure for the participating businesses.

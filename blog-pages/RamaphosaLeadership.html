Media Ramaphosa to complete term as ANC avoids leadership debate

November 12, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The African National Congress (ANC) will not use its upcoming national general council (NGC) to engineer a leadership overhaul, its secretary general Fikile Mbalula said. He reaffirmed that Cyril Ramaphosa will serve out his full term as President and head of the party.

Mbalula stated that the NGC will focus on organisational renewal and policy implementation rather than dissecting leadership positions or dissolving the national executive committee (NEC). He said this decision reflects the party’s commitment to stability and continuity.

In his remarks Mbalula emphasised that the leadership discussions will be free from the theatrics of “a shake up” and that Ramaphosa’s term remains intact until its scheduled end. He insisted the NGC must steer clear of speculation about shifting leadership and instead concentrate on strengthening the ANC’s internal capacity and electoral readiness.

The announcement comes amid ongoing speculation about possible internal change within the ANC following recent electoral setbacks and debates about succession. By publicly ruling out a leadership contest at the NGC, the party seeks to quell uncertainty and project unity.

Political analysts note that the message signals an effort to prioritise issue based work over factional power struggles in the run up to regional and national elections. At the same time it places Ramaphosa firmly in charge for the period ahead, giving opponents and supporters alike clarity on the direction of the ANC’s leadership at least for now.

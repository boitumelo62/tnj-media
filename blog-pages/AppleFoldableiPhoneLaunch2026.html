Tech Apple Expected to Enter Foldable Market with iPhone Launch in 2026

Apple may finally be preparing to step into the foldable smartphone market, joining the ranks of major competitors like Samsung, Huawei and Motorola. The tech giant, which has remained on the sidelines of the foldable trend, is reportedly planning to unveil its first foldable iPhone in 2026.

This information comes from a research note by JP Morgan analyst Samik Chatterjee, who suggests Apple’s foldable model could debut in September 2026 as part of the iPhone 18 lineup. If Apple maintains its current naming convention, the new device could mark a major shift in the design of its flagship smartphones.

Chatterjee described the anticipated model as a “book-style” foldable device, similar in form to Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold. He also noted that with only minor enhancements expected for the iPhone 17, attention is already shifting toward the 2026 release cycle.

One standout detail is the price. According to the analyst, the foldable iPhone is expected to launch with a retail price of around $1,999 (approximately R36,000) — nearly double the cost of today’s iPhone 16 Pro.

While Apple has yet to officially confirm any plans, this development suggests the company is preparing to make a bold entry into the growing market for foldable smartphones.

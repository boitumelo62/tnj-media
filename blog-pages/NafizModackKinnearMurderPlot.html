Media Nafiz Modack Again Rejects Claims Of Using Multiple Cellphones In Kinnear Murder Plot

November 28, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Alleged underworld figure Nafiz Modack has once again denied accusations that he operated multiple cellphone numbers to coordinate the murder of Lieutenant Colonel Charl Kinnear in 2020. The claim has surfaced repeatedly throughout ongoing investigations, forming part of the broader case that links Modack to organised criminal activity within the Western Cape.

Modack has remained firm in rejecting any association with the communication trails presented by investigators. These records were believed to show several numbers tied to him during the planning and execution of the attack. Authorities have argued that these numbers were used to monitor Kinnear and assist the gunmen responsible for the officer’s death.

The case continues to draw national attention. Kinnear was a high-profile investigator whose work targeted gangs and corruption within the police service. His murder sparked outrage and intensified pressure on authorities to dismantle criminal networks operating in the province.

Modack maintains his innocence and says he has been unfairly targeted. The matter remains active and continues to unfold as the state presses forward with its evidence and witness testimonies.

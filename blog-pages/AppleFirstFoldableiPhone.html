Tech News Apple’s First Foldable iPhone Tipped to Feature Liquid Metal Hinge

January 15, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Apple is expected to enter the foldable smartphone market with its first foldable iPhone, widely referred to as the iPhone Fold, with reports suggesting it could be unveiled later this year alongside the iPhone 18 Pro range.

New information from industry leaks points to a strong focus on durability and materials. The foldable device is said to feature a hinge made from liquid metal, a material Apple has reportedly been testing and refining for more than 15 years. The use of liquid metal is expected to improve the strength of the hinge while reducing wear and stress at the folding point, a common challenge in foldable devices.

The main body of the iPhone Fold is also believed to use a new form of titanium. This updated material is said to be stronger and lighter than the titanium currently used in recent iPhone models, potentially helping Apple keep the device slim while maintaining structural integrity.

Reports suggest the foldable iPhone will have an inner display measuring about 7.76 inches, paired with a 5.49 inch outer screen. The device is expected to feature dual rear cameras.

While the announcement is rumored for later this year, the foldable iPhone is not expected to reach consumers until early 2027, as Apple continues to refine the design and production process.

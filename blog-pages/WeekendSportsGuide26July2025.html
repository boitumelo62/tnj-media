This weekend features a packed calendar across rugby, football, cricket, tennis, Formula 1, and golf. With derbies, international finals, and major tour events all scheduled, here are the key fixtures taking place on July 26 and 27.

RUGBY FIXTURES

Crucial Currie Cup derbies headline the domestic rugby weekend, with teams jostling for playoff qualification in the final rounds of the competition.

  • Lions vs Sharks – Saturday, July 26 (14:00)
  • Western Province vs Blue Bulls – Saturday, July 26 (16:10)

FOOTBALL FIXTURES

Clubs across Europe continue their pre-season preparations with friendly matches scheduled ahead of the new campaign.

  • Selected club friendlies – July 26–27 (subject to regional confirmation)

CRICKET FIXTURE

Zimbabwe hosts the final of its T20I Tri-Nation Series featuring South Africa and New Zealand. It is the only major international cricket match scheduled over the weekend.

  • Zimbabwe Tri-Nation Series Final – Saturday, July 26 (Harare)

TENNIS FIXTURES

Several key hard-court events reach their closing stages as players tune up for the US Open. Action continues across Washington, Prague, Umag, and Kitzbühel.

  • Citi Open (WTA/ATP 500) – Ongoing
  • Prague Open, Croatia Open, Austrian Open – Finals weekend (July 26–27)

FORMULA 1 FIXTURE

The Formula 1 calendar continues with the Belgian Grand Prix at Spa-Francorchamps. The Sprint takes place on Saturday, followed by the main race on Sunday.

  • Belgian Grand Prix – Sprint (Saturday, July 26), Race (Sunday, July 27)

GOLF FIXTURES

Two major events conclude this weekend: LIV Golf’s UK tournament and the PGA Tour’s 3M Open. Both play critical roles in shaping playoff fields and player rankings.

  • LIV Golf UK (The Duels) – July 26–27 (JCB Golf & Country Club, England)
  • 3M Open (PGA Tour) – Final Round on Sunday, July 27 (Minnesota, USA)

Media ACSA denies record of proposed luxury terminal linked to Vusimuzi Matlala

Airports Company South Africa has denied having any record of a proposed R70 million luxury private terminal at OR Tambo International Airport, following reports linking the project to businessman Vusimuzi Cat Matlala.

The alleged terminal has attracted public attention amid questions around transparency, procurement processes, and the influence of private individuals within state owned infrastructure projects. ACSA says it has no documentation or formal application on record for such a development and has called for clarity on the source of the claims.

The company has reiterated that any private infrastructure development at national airports must follow strict regulatory and procurement procedures, including feasibility assessments, environmental approvals, and board oversight. It has also stressed that no individual or entity is permitted to develop airport infrastructure without formal approval and contractual agreements.

Analysts say the controversy highlights broader concerns around governance and accountability within state owned entities, particularly in the aviation and transport sectors. Transparency advocates argue that allegations involving high value infrastructure projects must be fully investigated to maintain public trust and ensure compliance with procurement laws.

ACSA has indicated it will cooperate with any inquiries and has urged the public and media to verify claims through official channels. The company maintains that OR Tambo remains a critical national asset and that any developments must align with national aviation policy, security standards, and economic development objectives.

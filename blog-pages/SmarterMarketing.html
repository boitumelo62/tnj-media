Media The Key to Smarter Marketing

November 11, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

The Next Chapter of South Africa’s Media Evolution

For a long time, research sat quietly in the background of marketing reliable but rarely in the spotlight. It was the thing you did once a year or only if your company had the money to spare. But that idea belongs firmly in the past.

Today’s research industry looks nothing like it used to. Thanks to technology and AI, insights that once took weeks can now be gathered overnight. Machines can scan through tens of thousands of social media posts, spot emerging issues, and alert brands before problems blow up. Automated testing tools can show which ad concepts are most likely to grab attention even before a campaign launches. What used to take six weeks can now take six days or even six hours.

But with all this progress comes a new challenge: finding the right research partner is now just as important and as complicated as choosing a creative agency. Despite all the tools and data available, research still isn’t being used to its full potential. Nearly 40% of marketers admit they don’t rely on it as much as they should. That’s a big miss, because media data can tell you where your audience is, but research tells you why they act the way they do. When those two come together, that’s when marketing truly becomes effective.

This is where pitch consultants come in. Just as they help marketers find the right creative or media agencies, they can also help navigate the increasingly crowded research world. The research landscape is full of options global networks, independent specialists, and niche experts. Without help, many marketers simply stick with whoever they used before. A consultant can cut through that confusion, offering objective advice and helping define what kind of research is really needed, what budget makes sense, and which partners are the best fit. It’s a process that saves time, prevents missteps, and ensures research supports growth rather than just spending. As Johanna McDowell from IAS says, “The right consultant helps marketers see research not as a cost centre, but as a growth engine.”

The impact of matching correctly can’t be overstated. A retailer testing a new store layout needs quick behavioural insights, while a company expanding into a new market needs deep cultural understanding. If you pick the wrong kind of research partner, you risk building your entire strategy on the wrong foundation.

In South Africa, the research scene is thriving. Local firms are earning recognition for their expertise, and AI tools are giving them capabilities once limited to the big global players. This means marketers now have access to skilled, cost effective partners who understand local realities while delivering world class results if they know where to look.

At the end of the day, the question isn’t whether to invest in research it’s whether you’re choosing the right research partner with the same care you’d give to selecting your creative agency. For marketers who truly want effective strategies, research shouldn’t be an afterthought or something squeezed in when there’s leftover budget. It should be built into the plan from the very start. Research has evolved. Now it’s time for your marketing strategy to evolve with it.

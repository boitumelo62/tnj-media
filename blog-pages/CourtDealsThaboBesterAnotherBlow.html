Media Court Deals Thabo Bester Another Blow in Transfer Bid

March 4, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Convicted rapist and murderer Thabo Bester has suffered another legal setback after the Pretoria High Court dismissed his latest application to be transferred from eBongweni Super Maximum Prison in Kokstad.

Bester, who is serving multiple life sentences, approached the court seeking relocation from the high security facility, arguing that his continued detention there was unjustified. However, the court found no compelling reason to grant the request, effectively keeping him at the maximum security prison.

The ruling marks yet another chapter in the long running legal saga surrounding Bester, whose dramatic prison escape in 2022 shocked the nation and exposed serious security failures within the correctional system. After faking his own death in a cell fire, he fled the country and was later rearrested in Tanzania in 2023 before being extradited to South Africa.

Since his return, Bester has launched several legal challenges related to his detention conditions and treatment behind bars. Authorities have maintained that given his history and the gravity of his crimes, he remains a high risk inmate who must be housed in a maximum security facility.

The Department of Correctional Services has previously stated that all decisions regarding inmate placement are guided by security assessments and public safety considerations.

With the High Court ruling now handed down, Bester is expected to remain at eBongweni unless he pursues further legal avenues. It remains unclear whether he intends to appeal the latest judgment.

Business ‘Please Call Me’ Battle Heads Back to Court as Makate Challenges Vodacom Again

The long-running legal battle between Nkosana Makate and Vodacom is set to return to the Supreme Court of Appeal next month, following renewed disputes over compensation linked to Makate’s “Please Call Me” invention.

The return to court comes after a single day of arguments in the Constitutional Court earlier this month, where Makate challenged Vodacom’s compensation offer and accused the company of underpaying him for the idea that became a cornerstone of mobile communication in South Africa.

Vodacom, which has spent nearly R13 million in legal costs, maintains that its settlement offer was fair and based on reasonable revenue estimates. Makate, however, argues that the network giant owes him billions in unpaid royalties.

The upcoming hearing marks yet another chapter in a case that has spanned nearly two decades and tested the limits of intellectual property and corporate accountability in South Africa’s telecommunications sector.

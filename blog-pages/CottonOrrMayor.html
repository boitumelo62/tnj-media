Media Durban born Cotton Orr makes history in United States mayoral race

November 7, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Durban born Benita Cotton Orr has been elected Mayor of Sky Valley in the state of Georgia in the United States following this week’s local elections. Her victory places her among the small but growing number of South Africans who have secured public office abroad.

Cotton Orr received strong support from local residents who praised her community involvement and her focus on development issues that have affected the small town for years. Her campaign centred on improving local infrastructure, strengthening public safety initiatives, and boosting economic activity in one of the region’s quieter municipalities.

Residents who voted for her said they were drawn to her approachable style and her emphasis on consultation. They described her as a leader who listened to community needs and offered practical solutions instead of political rhetoric.

Cotton Orr’s family in South Africa expressed pride at the news, noting that her achievement highlights the global impact that South Africans continue to make in various fields. Her election has also been celebrated in her hometown of Durban where former colleagues and friends commended her commitment to public service.

The mayor elect is expected to be sworn in soon and will begin implementing her development plan in early 2026.

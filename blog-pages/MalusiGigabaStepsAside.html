Media Malusi Gigaba Steps Aside from ANC Duties After New Corruption Charge

November 20, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

ANC MP Malusi Gigaba has voluntarily stepped aside from all party duties following the announcement of a new corruption charge linked to investigations that have continued to widen around several state institutions. The ANC confirmed the move earlier today, saying it was welcomed as part of the party’s commitment to its internal step aside resolution.

Gigaba’s decision follows mounting pressure after prosecutors filed a fresh charge that places him among several public figures facing scrutiny over decisions made during his time in government. Although the details of the charge have not been fully disclosed, it is understood to be related to procurement irregularities and alleged interference in decision making processes connected to a state entity.

In a statement issued after the announcement, ANC officials said that Gigaba’s decision would protect the integrity of the party at a time when trust in public leadership remains fragile. They emphasised that stepping aside is not an admission of guilt but a procedural measure intended to allow legal processes to unfold without political interference.

Gigaba’s legal team has indicated that he intends to challenge the charge and will present what they describe as a comprehensive defence once a court date is finalised. They argue that many of the allegations against him recycle claims that have circulated in the public for years but have not been proven in court.

Political analysts say the development adds further weight to ongoing questions about the legacy of governance failures linked to the period often associated with high level corruption networks. The charge also places renewed focus on the effectiveness of the ANC’s step aside policy, which has faced resistance in some regions but remains central to the party’s internal accountability strategy.

Within the ANC, the announcement has sparked discussions about potential shifts in committee assignments and the broader impact on parliamentary work. While Gigaba retains his position as a member of Parliament, his temporary withdrawal from party roles means adjustments may be required in oversight structures where he previously participated.

The case is expected to draw significant public interest in the coming weeks, especially as prosecutors prepare to outline the evidence underpinning the new charge. The ANC has reiterated that it will monitor the proceedings closely and will continue to enforce its resolutions without exception.

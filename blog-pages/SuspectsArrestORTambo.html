Media Four more suspects arrested at OR Tambo

December 1, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Fifth Russia-recruitment suspect held

Four additional suspects have been taken into custody at OR Tambo International Airport after allegedly preparing to travel to Russia to join the country’s military. Their arrest follows an ongoing national investigation into recruitment efforts linked to the Russia Ukraine conflict and comes just days after another suspect was confirmed by authorities.

Officials say the group was intercepted during routine checks, but intelligence work had already placed them under watch. They were detained before boarding and are now expected to face charges relating to unlawful foreign military involvement.

Security agencies believe they are part of a wider network responsible for influencing and facilitating South Africans to fight abroad. Investigators are now working through electronic records, communications and travel patterns to establish whether more individuals were preparing to depart.

The arrests mark another significant step in efforts to stop illegal participation in foreign combat, with investigators warning that further operations are underway. Court appearances are expected once charges are formally finalised.

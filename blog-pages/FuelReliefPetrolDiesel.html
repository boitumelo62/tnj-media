Media Fuel Relief at the Pumps as Petrol and Diesel Prices Fall

February 3, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

South African motorists are getting some relief at the pumps this week after fuel prices dropped across the country, easing pressure on households and businesses grappling with high living costs.

Petrol prices have decreased by 65 cents per litre, while diesel has fallen by up to 57 cents per litre, according to the latest adjustment. The reduction comes at a time when transport costs remain a major contributor to inflation, particularly for food and essential goods.

Fuel prices in South Africa are influenced by several factors, including global oil prices, the rand dollar exchange rate, and local taxes and levies. In recent weeks, international oil prices have softened amid concerns about slowing global economic growth, while the rand has remained relatively stable. These conditions created room for the latest downward adjustment.

Economists say lower fuel prices can have a knock on effect across the economy. Transport operators may see reduced operating costs, which could eventually filter through to food prices and public transport fares. However, analysts caution that the benefits are not always immediate and can be offset by other rising costs.

For many motorists, the decrease offers short term breathing room rather than long term relief. South Africa has seen frequent fuel price hikes over the past few years, driven by volatile global markets and domestic cost pressures. This has made fuel budgeting unpredictable for both households and businesses.

Industry experts warn that future fuel price movements remain uncertain. Any sharp changes in oil prices, geopolitical tensions, or currency weakness could quickly reverse the gains. For now, though, motorists are being urged to make the most of the lower prices while they last.

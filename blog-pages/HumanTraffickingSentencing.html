Media Seven Chinese Nationals Sentenced to 20 Years Each for Human Trafficking in South Africa

September 11, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

A South African court has sentenced seven Chinese nationals to 20 years in prison each after finding them guilty of human trafficking, kidnapping, and forced labour. The ruling was handed down this week following a lengthy investigation by the Hawks, South Africa’s organised crime unit.

According to court documents, the group was involved in a trafficking operation that lured victims into captivity and subjected them to brutal conditions. Victims were allegedly forced into unpaid labour and held against their will, with reports of widespread abuse and threats.

Authorities say the convictions mark a significant breakthrough in dismantling a larger trafficking syndicate believed to operate across provincial and even international borders. Investigations are still ongoing to uncover the full extent of the network.

Anti-trafficking organisations hailed the ruling as a victory for victims and a warning to perpetrators. “This sentence sends a clear message that South Africa will not tolerate human trafficking in any form,” one advocate said.

Officials confirmed that efforts to track down additional suspects are underway, with the Hawks continuing to coordinate with other law enforcement agencies.

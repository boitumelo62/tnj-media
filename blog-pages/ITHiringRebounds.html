Tech News IT Hiring Rebounds as Demand for Tech Skills Surges

December 15, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Technology professionals are making a clear comeback in South Africa, with hiring momentum picking up strongly in the second half of 2025. According to online recruitment platform Pnet, demand for tech skills has rebounded after several difficult years, pointing to a meaningful recovery in the IT sector following a prolonged decline that stretched from 2022 through to the first half of 2025.

Pnet’s latest job market analysis shows that technology vacancies rose by 18% year on year up to the end of October 2025. Even more encouraging is the 10% increase recorded between September and October alone, which signals that the uplift is not a once off spike but part of a sustained upward trend. One of the most telling signs of this turnaround is the return of the Software Developer role to the top of South Africa’s most in demand jobs list in October, something that has not happened in three and a half years.

Anja Bates, Head of Data at Pnet, says the market is clearly shifting again after several years of uncertainty. She notes that demand for experienced and specialised technology professionals had stabilised at a lower level for some time, but recent data now points to renewed growth, largely driven by increased investment in cloud computing and artificial intelligence. This resurgence is reshaping hiring priorities and intensifying competition for scarce skills.

Among all technology roles, Business Analysts are showing the strongest growth, with demand more than doubling since January 2025. Data focused roles are close behind, with vacancies for Data Scientists, Data Analysts and Data Warehousing Specialists rising by nearly 50% over the same period. Infrastructure skills are also in short supply, as Systems and Network Administrator roles have seen a 38% increase in vacancies over the year. Software Developers, while already highly sought after, have still recorded a solid 34% increase in demand since January.

Bates warns that as demand continues to rise, competition for top talent will become more intense. Employers who move quickly to simplify their hiring processes, clearly communicate their value as employers, and offer attractive benefits and working conditions are likely to be best positioned to secure the skills they need as the sector continues its recovery. From Pnet’s perspective, the strengthening of tech hiring is not only good news for IT professionals.

Looking at where hiring activity is most concentrated, Pnet highlights ten technology roles that currently stand out as being in particularly high demand. Business Analysts are sought after for their ability to analyse business processes, identify opportunities for improvement and translate complex requirements into clear instructions for technical teams. Data Analysts continue to be critical as organisations rely on them to turn raw data into insights that support everyday decision making.

Data Scientists play a strategic role by extracting insights from complex datasets through advanced analytics, machine learning and statistical modelling. IT Project Managers are needed to plan, coordinate and deliver major technology initiatives on time and within budget. Developers are sought after for building applications within Microsoft’s .NET environment, particularly in financial services and enterprise systems.

Media SAPS probe claims that Duduzile Zuma Sambudla lured men into Russia’s war effort

November 25, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The South African Police Service has launched a formal investigation into allegations that Duduzile Zuma Sambudla lured 17 South African men into travelling to Russia, where they are now reportedly trapped and forced into fighting in that country’s war against Ukraine without their consent.

Families of the men say they were recruited under false pretences and promised work opportunities and training programmes. Once in Russia, the men were allegedly stripped of their documents and placed into military controlled environments. Several relatives have described the situation as a nightmare, saying they have been unable to communicate freely with their loved ones or secure assistance to bring them home.

SAPS confirmed that the investigation will focus on the circumstances surrounding the recruitment process, the travel arrangements, and whether any financial or political influence was used to facilitate the movement of the men out of South Africa. Investigators will also examine whether any laws relating to trafficking, coercion, or the recruitment of South Africans into foreign armed forces were violated.

Government departments have begun coordinating efforts to retrieve verified information from international partners. Officials say the safety of the men is the priority while diplomatic channels are explored to establish their exact location and conditions.

The allegations have caused widespread public concern as the country continues to monitor global conflicts closely. If proven true, the case would raise serious questions about the manipulation of vulnerable individuals and the use of South Africans in foreign wars without authorisation.

SAPS has appealed to families and community members to come forward with any additional information that may support the investigation. Further updates are expected as more details are confirmed.

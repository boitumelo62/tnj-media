Media EMPD Allegiance Footage Intensifies Scrutiny at Madlanga Commission

November 14, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The Madlanga Commission of Inquiry was presented with striking evidence yesterday as a 2023 video of suspended acting EMPD head Julius Mkhwanazi was played before the panel. In the footage, Mkhwanazi is seen pledging loyalty to former Ekurhuleni city manager Imogen Mashazi, a revelation that immediately raised new questions about the depth of personal and political alliances within the metro police service.

Commission members examined the video closely, noting concerns that relationships of loyalty within law enforcement may have influenced operational decisions and disciplinary processes. Mkhwanazi, who has been suspended for an extended period, has repeatedly argued that his sanction was unjust and politically motivated. The latest disclosure, however, has added pressure on him to explain the circumstances surrounding the recording and its relevance to his tenure.

Observers say the evidence highlights the complex and often blurred lines between administrative authority and policing independence. The inquiry itself was established to investigate the integrity of oversight structures within metropolitan governance, following years of whistleblower reports, contested appointments, and accusations of undue influence at senior levels. The commission will continue to assess whether the Mkhwanazi footage forms part of a larger pattern of inappropriate loyalty networks within the institution.

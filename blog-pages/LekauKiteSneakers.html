Business Lekau Sehoana Turns Setback into a Fresh Start with Kite

October 4, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

A year after losing his previous business, Lekau Sehoana is back with a new sneaker brand, and this time, it’s personal. He’s named it Kite, and for him, it’s more than just a product it’s a symbol of resilience and starting over. In his own words, Kite stands as a reminder that even when life knocks you down, you can rebuild and rise again.

Lekau’s previous brand, Drip Footwear, was liquidated in September last year after a legal dispute. A Johannesburg High Court ruling shut it down, forcing all 14 stores to close and ending contracts with all the employees.

The case was brought by Wideopen Platform, an advertising company that claimed Drip owed them R20 million for services. After the court decision, Lekau’s world turned upside down. He faced commission inquiries, had his bank accounts frozen, and even saw his assets seized. Everyone from staff to lawyers and auditors got dragged into the fallout.

But despite that heavy blow, Lekau didn’t stay down for long. His new brand, Kite, is rooted in a simple but powerful memory from his childhood spending entire days building kites and running around trying to get them to fly.

That image became a metaphor for this new chapter. As he puts it, a kite only takes off if it's built properly and launched against the wind. You have to run hard, face resistance, and be patient. But once it’s up, it soars. He compares that process to building a business. These days, he says, you can just go and buy a kite or even a sneaker brand like his but the core principle remains: you still have to build it right. And just like a kite, a brand only really flies once it’s in the hands of someone who believes in it.

Kite isn’t just a comeback. It’s Lekau’s way of turning a painful ending into a fresh beginning.

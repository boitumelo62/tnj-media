Sport Blockbuster Clashes Set as 2027 RWC Pools Revealed

World Rugby has officially unveiled the pool draw for the 2027 Rugby World Cup in Australia, delivering a set of groups packed with heavyweight clashes, historic rivalries and potential opening-night drama. The announcement, made moments ago, lays out the roadmap for the 24-team tournament, which will feature six pools of four.

Pool A immediately stands out with hosts Australia grouped alongside New Zealand, raising the tantalising possibility that the trans-Tasman rivals could meet in the tournament’s opening match. With Chile and Hong Kong China completing the pool, the Wallabies face a challenging start to their campaign.

Pool D promises one of the most fiercely watched fixtures of the pool stage as Ireland and Scotland are set for another high-stakes Six Nations showdown. With Uruguay and Portugal also in the group, the battle for qualification places could be intense.

In Pool F, traditional rivals England and Wales have been drawn together, ensuring their clash will be one of the marquee matchups of the opening rounds. Tonga and Zimbabwe round out a pool that offers a blend of physicality and unpredictability.

Reigning champions South Africa headline Pool B, joined by Italy, Georgia and Romania, while Pool C features Argentina, Fiji, Spain and Canada—a group with the potential for major upsets given Fiji’s recent tournament pedigree.

Pool E brings together France, Japan, USA and Samoa, offering contrasting styles and significant competitive depth.

With the expanded 24-team format debuting in 2027, the draw highlights the global reach of the sport and sets the stage for a tournament filled with narrative and rivalry. Fans will now look ahead to the match schedule announcement to see whether Australia versus New Zealand will indeed light the fuse on opening night at the revamped Rugby World Cup.

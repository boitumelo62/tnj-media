Media Civilians hailed as heroes after deadly Bondi Beach terror attack

December 16, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

As a Sydney shop owner is being praised for bravely disarming one of the gunmen during a deadly attack at a Jewish holiday gathering near Bondi Beach, attention has also turned to other civilians who lost their lives while trying to protect those around them.

Among them was 62 year old Reuven Morrison who was fatally shot after confronting the attackers. His daughter Sheina Gutnick told an American broadcaster that her father reacted immediately when the shooting began, placing himself in harm’s way to defend the community.

She said Morrison hurled bricks at the gunman while shouting and attempting to distract him from further violence. According to Gutnick, her father’s final moments reflected the values he lived by, adding that if there was any way for him to leave this world, it would be while standing up to terror.

Footage circulating on social media appears to show Morrison chasing after one of the attackers and throwing an object in his direction. This occurred shortly after 43 year old Ahmed al Ahmed, a Muslim father of two, charged at one of the gunmen from behind and managed to disarm him. Morrison, who emigrated from the former Soviet Union, was later shot and killed.

Ahmed remains in hospital recovering from surgery after sustaining gunshot wounds. A public fundraising effort in his support has since raised more than two million Australian dollars.

Morrison was one of 15 people killed in what authorities have described as Australia’s worst mass shooting in almost three decades. Investigators allege the attackers were a father and son and are treating the incident as a terrorist act directed at the Jewish community.

Another unidentified couple also died after attempting to overpower one of the gunmen. Dashcam footage verified by international media shows an older man wearing a lavender shirt grappling with a shooter over a long barrelled firearm near a parked vehicle. The man is later seen standing with the weapon before separate drone footage shows both him and a woman lying motionless near the scene.

The owner of the dashcam footage said the man did not flee but instead ran directly toward danger, using his strength to fight the attacker. She said watching him fall after being shot was heartbreaking.

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese has since commended the civilians who acted selflessly during the attack, saying their courage in running toward danger helped save lives and embodied the best of the nation.

Media Teacher Arrested After Alleged Assault of Grade 11 Pupil

March 3, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Police have arrested a teacher accused of administering 92 lashes with a pipe to a Grade 11 pupil at Tetelo Secondary School in Protea North, Soweto.

According to authorities, the alleged assault took place on school premises and was reported shortly thereafter. The learner is believed to have sustained injuries and received medical attention. The circumstances leading up to the incident remain under investigation.

The South African Schools Act prohibits corporal punishment in all schools, and any form of physical discipline is considered unlawful. Education officials have reiterated that alternative disciplinary measures must be used to address misconduct.

Gauteng education authorities have condemned the incident and confirmed that an internal investigation is under way. The department says it will cooperate fully with law enforcement and ensure that due process is followed.

The accused teacher is expected to appear in court soon on charges related to assault. Community members and parents have expressed shock and concern, calling for accountability and stronger oversight to protect learners.

Police investigations are continuing.

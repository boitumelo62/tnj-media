Saturday, 23 August 2025

Rugby

Preview – South Africa vs Australia (Rugby Championship)
Cape Town hosts a high-stakes clash as the Springboks face Australia in the Rugby Championship. South Africa are looking to cement their position at the top of the standings, while the Wallabies aim to prove their resurgence under new management. A packed stadium and fierce physical contest are expected.

  • Rugby Championship: South Africa vs Australia — 16:10
  • Rugby Championship: Argentina vs New Zealand — 22:10
  • Women’s World Cup: Australia vs Samoa — 12:00
  • Women’s World Cup: Scotland vs Wales — 14:45
  • Women’s World Cup: Canada vs Fiji — 17:30
  • Women’s World Cup: France vs Italy — 20:15

Cricket

Preview – Bangladesh vs India, 3rd ODI
The series decider in Dhaka pits Bangladesh against India in front of a passionate home crowd. India will bank on their batting depth to dominate, while Bangladesh will rely on their spinners to unsettle the visitors on familiar conditions. Both sides are under pressure to claim momentum before the World Cup.

  • Bangladesh vs India — 3rd ODI in Dhaka
  • Men’s Cricket World Cup Challenge League A — Match 4 (St Martin)
  • Caribbean Premier League — Match 10 (Gros Islet, 16:00)

Tennis

Preview – Winston-Salem Open Final
The last stop before the US Open, the Winston-Salem Open final provides players a chance to fine-tune their game and gain confidence. With form and fitness on the line, the winner could carry critical momentum into the season’s final Grand Slam.

  • Winston-Salem Open — Singles Final & Doubles Final (~14:00, 17:00)
  • Tennis in the Land (WTA 250, Cleveland) — finals

Formula 1

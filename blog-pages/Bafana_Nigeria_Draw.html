Sports Bafana Bafana Hold Nigeria to 1-1 Draw in World Cup Qualifier

September 10, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Bafana Bafana secured a crucial point in their 2026 FIFA World Cup qualifying campaign after playing out a 1-1 draw against Nigeria at a packed Free State Stadium in Bloemfontein on Tuesday night.

South Africa made a bright start and were rewarded in the 27th minute when Evidence Makgopa latched onto a clever through ball before slotting past the Nigerian goalkeeper to give the hosts a deserved lead. The goal sparked wild celebrations among the home crowd and boosted Bafana’s confidence.

Nigeria, however, showed their quality and equalised shortly before halftime. A defensive lapse allowed Victor Osimhen space inside the box, and the Napoli striker calmly finished to level the score at 1-1 in the 41st minute.

The second half was fiercely contested, with both teams creating chances. South Africa’s Percy Tau came close with a curling effort that forced a diving save, while Nigeria nearly stole the win when Ademola Lookman struck the post late in the game.

Goalkeeper Ronwen Williams was outstanding for Bafana, making several key saves to deny the Super Eagles and preserve the draw. Coach Hugo Broos praised his side’s determination, saying the result showed South Africa can compete with Africa’s top teams.

Nigeria will feel disappointed not to have capitalised on their dominance in possession, but the point keeps both teams well in contention for qualification. The focus now shifts to the next round of fixtures, where every result will prove decisive in the race to reach the 2026 World Cup.

Media Client Relationships Are the Heart of Public Relations

September 10, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

In public relations, it’s easy to think success is all about delivering results and meeting business goals. That’s part of it, of course, but the real measure of long term success is trust. Trust is what keeps clients coming back, and it’s built through strong, honest relationships. After more than 20 years in the industry, I can say with confidence that when you put client relationships at the centre of what you do, everything else falls into place. Results, reputation, retention it all starts with trust.

PR is, at its core, a people business. Yes, media, influencers and stakeholders matter. But the connection you build with your client is what ultimately shapes your success. That relationship needs care and attention. It’s not just about getting along, it’s about becoming a trusted part of their team. To do that, you need to understand their world. Clients aren’t looking for someone who just secures coverage. They want a partner who understands their business inside and out, someone who sees the bigger picture and can offer real advice when it counts. That means staying curious, doing the work and always keeping their goals in mind.

Communication is key. You can’t build a solid relationship if you disappear after the pitch. Clients want to know you're there, not just watching from the sidelines. Check in regularly, be proactive, and always keep them updated. Don’t wait for them to ask show them that their business matters to you. It’s also important to remember that being helpful doesn’t mean saying yes to everything. Part of being a strong partner is having the confidence to push back when needed. If something won’t work, explain why and offer a better option. Clients respect honesty and expertise more than blind agreement.

While winning new clients is always exciting, the real value lies in keeping the ones you have. Retention is where growth and stability come from. When clients are happy, they stay longer, spend more, and recommend you to others. That only happens when you consistently deliver and genuinely care. In person time still matters. Video calls are convenient, but they can’t replace the connection you build when you sit down with someone face to face.

A coffee, a lunch, even a quick visit can strengthen the relationship in ways that emails never will. And don’t forget your team. Happy teams create happy clients. When your people feel supported and energised, they do their best work. Clients notice that. They can feel when your team is switched on, passionate and proud of what they’re delivering. That energy is contagious.

Lifestyle Salamina Mosese Inspires Young Readers with New Book

September 6, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Salamina Mosese is a familiar name in South African entertainment, with a career that spans more than two decades. She's not only an accomplished actress, known for her roles in popular shows like Soul Buddyz, 7de Laan, and Abo Mzala, but also a producer and entrepreneur. As a co-founder of Sorele Media, Salamina is committed to telling authentic African stories and promoting the work of women in film and television.

Her background in corporate communications adds depth to her storytelling, and her passion for content that resonates across generations is clear in everything she does. Beyond the screen, she’s also made a name for herself as a children’s author. Her first two books, Disaster at Gogo’s Spaza and Cook Off at Gogo’s Spaza, are rich in local culture and have been well received by young readers and parents alike. In fact, Cook Off at Gogo’s Spaza has been shortlisted for the 2025 South African Book Awards.

Now, Salamina is back with a new children’s book, Neo’s Invisible Blanket, published by Pan Macmillan South Africa. The story is inspired by her own family – especially her nephew and daughters. It follows Neo, a young boy who feels nervous and unsure of himself. His sister Thandi gives him an “invisible blanket” to help him feel brave. While it helps him at home, Neo soon has to face bigger challenges like starting at a new school and taking part in a swimming gala. The story gently explores themes of fear, courage, and self-belief.

Salamina says the book came from a very personal place, watching her loved ones tackle their own small, everyday fears. She wanted to create something that lets children know it's okay to be scared, but that they all have inner strength too. The book is brought to life by illustrator Subi Bosa, whose vibrant, playful artwork perfectly captures the warmth and humor of Neo’s journey. And in keeping with Salamina’s mission to reach as many children as possible, Neo’s Invisible Blanket will be available in English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sepedi, and Afrikaans.

With her growing list of creative achievements both on screen and in print, Salamina Mosese continues to use her voice to uplift, inspire, and entertain.

Sports Banyana Banyana Ready for Crucial WAFCON Qualifier Against DR Congo

October 27, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Banyana Banyana are in final preparations in Johannesburg ahead of their crucial Women’s Africa Cup of Nations (WAFCON) qualifier against the Democratic Republic of Congo at Dobsonville Stadium.

The reigning continental champions are determined to secure an early advantage before the return leg, with coach Desiree Ellis emphasizing focus, composure, and teamwork as key to maintaining their dominance in African football. Training sessions this week have centered on sharpening the team’s attack and tightening defensive coordination.

Star players such as Thembi Kgatlana and Hildah Magaia are expected to play pivotal roles, bringing both experience and creativity to the lineup. Ellis has also expressed confidence in the younger players stepping up to complement the team’s seasoned core.

Supporters are expected to fill Dobsonville Stadium, with the match seen as an important step toward qualifying for next year’s WAFCON tournament and continuing South Africa’s impressive record in women’s football.

Business Dis-Chem Confirms Executive Change as Saul Saltzman Prepares to Exit Role

October 8, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

After nearly two decades of service, Saul Saltzman, son of Dis-Chem founders Ivan and Lynette Saltzman, has officially stepped down from his role as Executive Director of the company, with his resignation set to take effect on 27 February 2026. While Saul will no longer be involved in the day to day operations of the business, he will remain on the board as a non-independent non executive director, continuing to play a part in shaping the company’s strategic path and governance.

The board has welcomed this transition and expressed gratitude for his long standing leadership and contributions. Saul’s departure comes as part of a broader restructuring within the Dis-Chem group, a shift that has been gradually unfolding as the Saltzman family begins to loosen its direct grip on the company they built over the last four decades. This change in leadership echoes a larger generational transition, Ivan Saltzman, the company’s co founder and longtime CEO, stepped down from his executive role in June 2023, passing the reins to then CFO Rui Morais, although Ivan remains on as an executive director.

These developments suggest a deliberate move by the family to reposition their involvement while still keeping a hand on the wheel. This context is especially significant considering the Saltzman family’s considerable stake in the company. In June 2025, a large scale internal share transfer reshaped the family’s holdings. Brothers Dan and Mark Saltzman inherited roughly R6.8 billion in Dis-Chem shares each receiving about R3.4 billion despite previously holding no direct beneficial interest. This transaction, part of a larger family restructuring effort, saw the Saltzmans’ investment vehicle, Ivlyn, reduce its shareholding from nearly 30% to just over 4%.

Even so, the family’s ownership structure remained intact, with shares simply shifting within the family circle. Until recently, the presence of Ivan, Lynette, and Saul as executive directors underlined this continued involvement, but with Saul stepping back and Lynette having exited her executive director role in 2022 to focus on Dis-Chem’s beauty division, Ivan is now the only family member still serving in a full time executive capacity. Over the past year, the restructuring was further emphasized through significant share sales by the Saltzman family and other executives. Saul alone sold shares worth over R120 million in 2024, while fellow executive director Stan Goetsch sold more than R300 million’s worth.

Industry analysts viewed these shifts as clear signs of a generational transition, potentially signalling a shift in vision or a future strategy that relies more on professional leadership than family control. This evolution in leadership and ownership is remarkable when one considers Dis-Chem’s humble beginnings. The company was born in 1978 when Ivan and Lynette Saltzman, working with a R10,000 capital investment, took over a small pharmacy in Mondeor, Johannesburg. Ivan, who had worked as a locum at the pharmacy, saw untapped potential and decided to take the leap. By 1984, they had already opened a second store, this time in Randridge Mall an area others were hesitant to invest in at the time.

Fast forward to 2025, and Dis-Chem has transformed into a retail giant with over 330 stores, including 285 pharmacies and 45 baby outlets, with a market capitalisation of R27 billion. While the company continues to grow, the leadership changes over the past few years mark a new chapter one where the founding family appears to be stepping into more strategic, background roles while handing over operational control to professional executives. Saul Saltzman’s resignation as Executive Director is not just a personal decision, it’s part of a broader narrative of transition, evolution, and the subtle passing of the torch to a new generation not only within the Saltzman family but within Dis-Chem’s broader leadership structure.

Media Makini Schools Brand Grows as ADvTECH Invests Further in Kenya

August 7, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

ADvTECH, one of South Africa’s leading private education groups, is continuing to grow beyond its home borders. The company has just announced the acquisition of another school in Kenya as part of its international expansion plans.

Known for owning major brands like Crawford International, Varsity College, Trinityhouse, and Pinnacle College, ADvTECH is further expanding its presence in East Africa through its Makini Schools brand. This latest move involves buying Regis Runda Academy in Nairobi for R172 million.

The school is located in the growing Runda area, northeast of the city, and can accommodate up to 2,000 students. It offers a complete K-12 curriculum and will now operate under the name Makini Schools Runda, joining the six other Makini campuses already established in Kenya.

ADvTECH’s CEO, Geoff Whyte, expressed enthusiasm about the deal, saying the company is excited to strengthen its footprint in Kenya and to offer more families access to the Makini educational experience.

This isn’t the first time ADvTECH has invested in education on the continent. In late 2024, it acquired five Flipper International Schools in Ethiopia for R135 million. The group also operates several schools in Botswana and continues to grow its reach in the tertiary education sector. Notably, it’s preparing to open Rosebank International University College in Accra, Ghana, with classes set to begin in September 2025.

All of this underscores ADvTECH’s long-term commitment to expanding high-quality education across Africa.

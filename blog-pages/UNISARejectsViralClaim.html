Media UNISA Rejects Viral Claim About Sarah-Jane Trent’s Law Degree

March 9, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

The University of South Africa has dismissed a viral social media claim suggesting that Sarah-Jane Trent did not graduate with a law degree from the institution. The university says the allegation is false and warned that misinformation about academic records can cause serious reputational harm.

The claim began circulating online over the weekend, quickly gaining traction across several platforms. Posts alleged that Trent, who has previously been associated with forensic investigator Paul O’Sullivan, had misrepresented her academic qualifications.

In a statement, Unisa confirmed that Trent did in fact complete her studies and graduate with a law qualification from the university. Officials described the online claims as inaccurate and misleading. University representatives said that spreading false information about a graduate’s credentials undermines trust in academic institutions and can damage the reputations of both the individual and the university itself.

The institution added that academic records are verified through strict administrative processes before degrees are awarded. According to the university, those procedures ensure that qualifications are only granted to students who have completed the required coursework and met graduation standards.

The controversy highlights the growing impact of misinformation on social media, where unverified claims can spread rapidly before being fact checked.

Unisa has urged members of the public to verify information before sharing it online and to rely on official sources when questions arise about academic credentials. The university says it remains committed to protecting the integrity of its qualifications and the reputations of its graduates.

Media MultiChoice Lifts Ban on Leon Schuster Films After Five Years

September 2, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

MultiChoice has reinstated several of Leon Schuster’s older films on its DStv and Showmax platforms, ending a five-year ban originally introduced in mid-2020. The company confirmed that the titles returned to its catalogue from 1 September 2025, marking a significant reversal in policy.

The films, which include popular comedies such as You Must Be Joking, Mama Jack, Mr Bones, Sweet ’n Short, and Oh Schucks, It’s Schuster, were removed at the height of global debates around representation and racial sensitivity. At the time, MultiChoice cited concerns over content such as the use of blackface and outdated stereotypes, arguing that the material no longer aligned with its standards for social responsibility.

The decision to bring the films back has sparked a wide spectrum of responses. For many South Africans, Schuster’s slapstick humour and candid-camera style remain an important part of local entertainment history. His films consistently drew large audiences, and his work has long been associated with family viewing and cultural nostalgia.

However, critics caution that some of the material may still be offensive in today’s social climate. Advocacy groups have raised questions about whether the return of these films undermines progress made in addressing harmful stereotypes in media.

MultiChoice has not provided a detailed explanation for the timing of its decision. Some commentators suggest it may be driven by consumer demand, while others point to a broader industry trend of reintroducing controversial but popular classics with clearer content warnings or disclaimers.

As of early September 2025, the films are once again accessible to subscribers. Whether they are embraced as a return to lighthearted entertainment or critiqued as outdated reflections of the past, their reappearance highlights the continuing debate over cultural memory, comedy, and representation in South African media.

Tech News Apple’s Stealth M5 Launch

February 5, 2026 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Tech enthusiasts and professional users are on high alert this week as a series of unusual software manoeuvres and inventory shortages suggest Apple is preparing for a surprise hardware launch. All signs point to the imminent arrival of the high-end M5 Pro and M5 Max MacBook Pro models.

The primary evidence lies in a strategic disruption of Apple’s typical software release cycle. While the company recently issued a "Release Candidate" (RC) for Xcode 26.3, it notably withheld the corresponding RC versions for macOS Tahoe 26.3 and iOS 26.3.

Historically, Apple uses this tactic to prevent "data miners" from digging through public code to find references to unreleased hardware. By delaying the macOS update, the company effectively hides new model identifiers that would confirm the existence of the M5-series chips. This pattern was previously seen during the surprise M3 Mac launch in 2023 and the M1 debut in 2020.

Beyond the digital breadcrumbs, physical supply chains are also tightening. Reports from authorized resellers indicate that current MacBook Pro stock levels have plummeted, a classic precursor to a refresh. While the entry-level 14-inch MacBook Pro with the base M5 chip is already on shelves, the "heavy lifters"—the Pro and Max variants—remain the missing pieces of the 2026 puzzle.

Adding to the mystery, supplies of the HomePod mini have also begun to dwindle globally. While this could indicate a simple supply chain bottleneck, it has sparked rumours that a second-generation smart speaker might accompany the new laptops.

Industry analysts expect the M5 Pro and M5 Max to debut via a press release in the coming weeks, likely before mid-March. However, for those not in a hurry, rumours persist that a more radical redesign featuring OLED displays and touchscreen capabilities is slated for late 2026. For now, power users should keep their eyes on the Apple Newsroom, as the next "Pro" jump appears to be just days away.

Lifestyle The Movie making kids sing and adults smile

December 13, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

If you’re in the mood for something vibrant, uplifting, and full of heart, KPop Demon Hunters on Netflix is exactly the kind of film that can brighten your whole weekend. I realised just how infectious it is when I stepped out onto my balcony and heard my neighbour’s son currently on school holidays and clearly living his best life singing “Soda Pop” at the top of his lungs. I couldn’t even blame him. The song is catchy, joyful, and impossible not to enjoy, just like the movie itself.

What makes the film stand out is how confidently it embraces its Korean identity. From its partially Korean voice cast to the Hangul lettering woven into its colourful scenes, the movie feels rooted in culture while still being incredibly welcoming to international audiences. It’s fun, it’s energetic, and it’s filled with the kind of warmth that crosses borders and generations.Directed by Maggie Kang and Chris Appelhans, with a screenplay co written alongside Danya Jimenez and Hannah McMechan, the film comes from Sony Pictures Animation in collaboration with Netflix. The voice cast is impressive, with Arden Cho, Ahn Hyo seop, May Hong, Ji young Yoo, Yunjin Kim, Daniel Dae Kim, Ken Jeong, and Lee Byung hun bringing humour, heart, and personality to every character.The story follows Huntrix, a massively popular K pop girl group that leads a thrilling double life. On stage, they’re global sensations. Off stage, they’re fierce demon hunters keeping the world safe from supernatural danger.

Their biggest challenge comes when the Saja Boys another hit group start to overtake them. The twist? The boys are actually demons using their rising fame to unleash chaos. It’s a fun mix of adventure, humour, friendship, and fantasy, all tied together with a message that sticks unity and loyalty are more powerful than anything that tries to divide us. Maggie Kang draws inspiration from Korean mythology and modern pop culture, creating a world that feels imaginative and wonderfully fresh. And then there’s the soundtrack, which might be one of the movie’s strongest highlights. Songs like “Soda Pop,” “Night Pulse,” and “Shine On Us” have already gone viral, and five tracks have even earned Grammy nominations.

The musical sequences burst with energy, mixing polished choreography, glossy visuals, and lively vocals that make every performance feel like a cross between a concert and an action scene. Visually, the movie is stunning. Bright neon landscapes, glowing magical effects, and dynamic fight scenes make the whole experience feel alive and endlessly fun. It’s the kind of animation that instantly captures your attention and keeps you glued to the screen. KPop Demon Hunters is the rare film that appeals to everyone kids, teens, adults, longtime K pop fans, and complete newcomers. It’s catchy, heartfelt, visually exciting, and filled with a universal message about the strength we find when we work together.

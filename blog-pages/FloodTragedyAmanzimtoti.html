Media Flood Tragedy Claims Lives in Amanzimtoti as Search Continues

December 22, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Two men have been found dead in Amanzimtoti after floodwaters swept away the vehicle they were travelling in on Sunday night, following heavy rainfall in parts of KwaZulu Natal. Emergency services have confirmed that search teams are still looking for one missing person believed to have been in the same vehicle.

The incident occurred after intense downpours caused rivers and streams in the area to swell rapidly, leading to dangerous driving conditions. Authorities say the vehicle was caught in fast moving floodwaters and washed off the roadway, leaving occupants with little chance to escape.

Search and rescue teams, including police, disaster management officials and volunteers, were deployed shortly after the incident was reported. The bodies of the two men were recovered downstream after several hours of searching. Efforts to locate the third individual have continued despite challenging conditions, including strong currents and debris.

Local authorities have expressed condolences to the families of the victims and urged residents to exercise extreme caution during periods of heavy rain. Motorists are being warned not to attempt crossing flooded roads or bridges, even if water levels appear low.

Disaster management officials say the incident highlights the dangers posed by sudden weather changes during the summer rainfall season. Communities in low lying and flood prone areas are being advised to remain alert to weather warnings and follow instructions from emergency services.

As the search continues, officials have reiterated calls for public awareness and preparedness, stressing that lives can be saved by avoiding unnecessary travel during severe weather and respecting road closure notices.

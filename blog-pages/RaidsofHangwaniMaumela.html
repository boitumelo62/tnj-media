Media Law enforcement raids homes of Hangwani Maumela and Shadrack Sibiya

October 10, 2025 ● Tebuho Zongwana

Law enforcement agencies carried out coordinated raids on Thursday targeting businessman Hangwani Maumela and suspended Deputy National Police Commissioner Lieutenant General Shadrack Sibiya. The operations formed part of an ongoing investigation into alleged corruption and irregular tenders linked to public institutions.

The Special Investigating Unit led the raid on Maumela’s residence in Sandton where luxury vehicles, artworks and several documents were seized. The raid reportedly took place early in the morning with investigators spending several hours on the property. Sources close to the operation confirmed that warrants were issued as part of a larger probe into contracts worth hundreds of millions of rand connected to Tembisa Hospital.

At the same time, police units searched Sibiya’s home in Centurion where electronic devices including cellphones and computers were confiscated. No arrests were made but authorities confirmed that the searches were linked to allegations of money laundering and obstruction of justice.

Sibiya expressed outrage after the raid claiming he was being targeted unfairly. Speaking to the media he compared his situation to the biblical story of Joseph saying he had been betrayed by his colleagues. He vowed not to resign and stated that if officials wanted him removed from his position they would have to dismiss him formally.

The timing of the raids has raised eyebrows as they took place while President Cyril Ramaphosa and Deputy President Paul Mashatile were both abroad. Some observers believe the developments form part of a wider power struggle within the security cluster.

The Special Investigating Unit has not yet confirmed whether further arrests or asset seizures will follow but has indicated that it will provide more details on the operation in due course.

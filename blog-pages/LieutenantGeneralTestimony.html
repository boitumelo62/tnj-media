Madlanga Commission Hears Five Days of Testimony

The Madlanga Commission continued its hearings today at 1pm with critical testimony from Crime Intelligence head Lieutenant General Dumisani Khumalo. He told the inquiry that political killings had dropped significantly after the creation of a task team in 2018. The commission is probing the extent of political violence and its links to structures of power in the country.

Khumalo’s appearance was one of the most anticipated moments of the inquiry. He detailed how intelligence operations had targeted high risk areas in KwaZulu Natal and Mpumalanga, which were considered hotspots for violence tied to political competition. He emphasized that the task team’s coordinated approach across police units was central to curbing the killings.

However, the commission may soon hear even more explosive evidence. It has emerged that conversations between Vusimuzi Matlala, a known political figure, and several high ranking officials could be presented as part of the inquiry. These discussions are expected to shed light on whether political influence extended into security operations or if there were attempts to shield certain individuals from investigation.

The hearings have become a focal point for the public as they attempt to understand the roots of political killings that have haunted South African democracy for years. With the possibility of implicating powerful individuals, today’s developments underline the high stakes of the Madlanga Commission.

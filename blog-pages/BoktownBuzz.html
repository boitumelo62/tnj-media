Sports Join the Boktown Buzz – Rugby, Music, and Mzansi Vibes Await

August 23, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

This Saturday, if you can't be at the stadium to watch the Springboks in action, don't worry Gauteng has you covered. Fans will be coming together in Soweto and Midrand for an all-day celebration filled with rugby, live music, and top-tier entertainment. Castle Lager Boktown is back, and it's bringing that unbeatable match day vibe to two lively locations. From the heart of Soweto to the buzz of Midrand, rugby lovers are in for something special on 23 August.

In Soweto, Elkah Cricket Stadium will be the place to be. Thousands of supporters will gather to watch South Africa take on Australia, surrounded by massive screens, ice cold Castle Lager, and an incredible lineup of artists. Focalistic, Njelic, Babalwa M, Thee Buhle, Jay Sax, Kay T Direct, and TallArseTee De MC will all take to the stage to bring the heat before and after the final whistle. It’s not just a game it’s a celebration of everything we love about our country: music, energy, culture, and that unshakable Springbok pride.

Meanwhile, over in Midrand at Vodacom World, the vibe will be just as electric. Fans can expect the same winning combo of live rugby and entertainment, with De Mthuda, 25K, Josiah De Disciple, Ceejay Clyde, Bablo Freaks, Bugh Daad, and Hloks MC keeping the crowd on its feet all day long. Giant screens will capture every moment of the match, making you feel like you're right there in the stadium.

But Boktown isn’t only about rugby. It’s a full-on festival atmosphere with something for everyone. There are family zones for a chilled-out day with the kids, food stalls to keep you fuelled, and an exclusive 18+ Castle Corner for those wanting a more premium experience. Whether you’re a die-hard supporter or just in it for the vibe, Boktown delivers.

Tickets start from just R100 and are available now on Howler. So, if you’re not heading to the stadium, grab your friends, throw on your green and gold, and make your way to Soweto or Midrand for a day of unforgettable gees. This is more than a match it’s a moment.

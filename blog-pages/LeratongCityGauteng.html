Media Leratong City Set to Become Gauteng’s Next Major Smart City

December 2, 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

Leratong City is set to become South Africa’s next major smart city, built around a brand new shopping centre that anchors a R5 billion development. Located in Mogale City in Western Gauteng, the precinct is being designed as a modern, technology driven urban space focused on efficiency, sustainability, and better living standards.

The construction of the 42,000 square metre Leratong City shopping centre is the project’s first big step forward, and the mall is expected to open on 29 October 2026. The area, once a barren piece of land, has changed quickly since construction began on 5 June 2025. The vision for the project takes its cue from President Ramaphosa’s 2019 call for South Africa to develop smart cities that can tackle issues like unemployment, slow economic growth, and inequality.

The new city will rise beside the long established Leratong Hospital, which has served the community since the early 1970s, and the nearby Chamdor precinct. With its multi billion rand investment, the development aims not only to boost economic activity but also to bring advanced digital infrastructure to the area so that progress benefits local communities.

Right now, the project employs 495 workers, and more than 20 small businesses from surrounding wards have been contracted to take part. A project steering committee is in place to ensure community involvement, proper oversight, and alignment with the city’s development goals. As construction continues, the city expects to attract more private sector investment and create ongoing opportunities through new retailers, food outlets, a mini taxi rank, and other mall related offerings.

Leratong Smart City is imagined as a dynamic district that will support economic growth across the West Rand. The larger plan includes around 15,000 housing units, along with schools, a private hospital, early childhood development centres, parks, transport hubs, and essential government services. With its R5 billion value, it will be one of Mogale City’s most significant developments in the coming decade.

Part of preparing for this future included building a 5 million litre reservoir in Kagiso Extension 13 in 2024, right next to the mall site, to secure water supply for the wider smart city. Although the project focuses on the Leratong Hospital precinct and surrounding neighbourhoods, it is designed to uplift areas beyond its immediate borders. The goal is to spark urban renewal throughout the West Rand, creating a stronger economic centre and improving underdeveloped parts of the region through thoughtful planning and increased density.

Overall, the project ties in with national and provincial goals for integrated communities, fair spatial planning, and inclusive development. Mogale City sees the Leratong Smart City as a blueprint for transforming township and peri urban spaces through smart infrastructure, strong public private partnerships, and well designed mixed use environments.

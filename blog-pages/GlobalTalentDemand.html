Business Global Demand for Skilled South Africans on the Rise

9 July 2025 ● Uhone Kwinda

International companies are actively recruiting skilled South Africans to fill global talent shortages, according to Rob Mailich, Global CEO of REDi Holbourne Recruitment Group in the UK. There’s been a noticeable increase in demand across fields like healthcare, education, engineering, IT, and finance.

Countries such as Canada, Germany, the UK, and the UAE are among those headhunting South African talent. Many professionals — especially the younger generation — are taking up these offers, moving to major cities like London, Amsterdam, New York, and Paris. Some are attracted by tax breaks, while others seek better job prospects and a higher quality of life. South Africans are well regarded for their confidence, strong work ethic, adaptability, and solid academic backgrounds from respected universities like UCT, Wits, and Stellenbosch.

Their clear English accents and cultural flexibility also make them ideal for global business environments. While better pay used to be a key driver, Mailich says this is shifting. Salaries at the top end in South Africa are now more aligned with international rates. However, fields like healthcare and education still offer better financial prospects abroad. Improved public infrastructure and safety overseas are also strong incentives for those looking to relocate.

Technology roles like developers, cloud engineers, and data analysts are especially in demand, along with auditors, actuaries, and various engineering professionals. The growing popularity of remote work is also opening doors for roles like virtual assistants and remote support staff.

South Africa’s BPO (Business Process Outsourcing) industry continues to attract international attention as a reliable and cost-effective talent source. Mailich encourages South Africans to keep upskilling, learn new languages, and build well-rounded CVs to stay competitive in this fast-moving global job market.
